## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD07`** (blocks `STEP-014` & `G05`; feeds `G06`, `G08`).

## 2. Decision Question
- Should models be trained and evaluated strictly on whole-breast `2D` mammograms (`FFDM` vs `SFM` tracks isolated), lesion-level ROI patches, or separated dual tracks — and how are `mass` vs `calcification` lesion morphologies handled?

## 3. REPORT FACT (`RF`) Locators
- `RF05`: Binary 2D mammogram classification (`Benign` vs `Malignant`).
- `RF08`: Grad-CAM explainability over mammogram classifications (`requires spatial context including surrounding parenchyma/chest wall`).
- `RF15`: Pure 2D mammography (`zero` DBT/ultrasound/histopathology/CESM contrast-subtracted `CM`).

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
- **Option A (Recommended — Whole-Image 2D Classification with Strict `FFDM` vs `SFM` Isolation):**
  - Train and evaluate whole-breast models exclusively on whole `2D FFDM` scans (`Primary: DS0003 CMMD`; `Secondary Localization/External: DS0006 CDD-CESM LE`, `DS0005 BMCD`, `DS0002 DMID`), and evaluate digitized `SFM` (`DS0001 MIAS`) solely as an external cross-sensor & corner-marker shortcut probe (`zero pooling` of `FFDM` and `SFM` or lesion crops).
  - Preserve `mass` vs `calcification` attributes solely for subgroup auditing (`STEP-037`).
- **Option B (Rejected):** Cropped ROI patch classification (requires ground-truth bounding box / ROI crop coordinates at test time, violating realistic whole-mammogram CAD screening and making post-hoc Grad-CAM localization `RQ3` trivial/leaky).

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- Whole-image classification requires higher input resolution care and faces background/border shortcut risk (`RISK0004`, explicitly observed in `DS0001` & `DS0005`), but directly validates clinical-grade spatial XAI faithfulness (`RQ3`) without oracle lesion boxes at inference time.

## 6. Recommendation
Adopt **Option A** inside [label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/protocols/label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md) (`§1`) for ratification at **`G05`**.
