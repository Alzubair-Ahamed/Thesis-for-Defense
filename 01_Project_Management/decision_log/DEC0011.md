## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD11`** (blocks `STEP-020` & `G07`).

## 2. Decision Question
- What internal train/val/test split proportions, cross-validation structure, seed, stratification variables, and internal-vs-external isolation design resolve `RF19` cleanly without cross-center leakage?

## 3. REPORT FACT (`RF`) & Observed Counts
- `RF19`: Title report specifies zero train/val/test split ratio or grouping level.
- Reconciled cohort sizes (`LBL0001–LBL0005`): `DS0003 CMMD` (`3,738` biopsy FFDM scans across `1,772` clean components); `DS0006 CDD-CESM LE` (`919` non-excluded / `581` `SubTrackA` / `569` lesion masks across `312` components); `DS0005 BMCD` (`260` non-excluded / `81` components); `DS0002 DMID` (`470` scans); `DS0001 MIAS` (`322` SFM across `161` L/R women).

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
- **Primary Architecture (`SPLIT0001`, `seed = 20261128`):**
  1. **Primary Internal Track (`DS0003 CMMD`):** Stratified connected-component **`70% train` (`2,614` imgs / `1,241` comps) / `15% val` (`560` imgs / `266` comps) / `15% sealed internal_test` (`564` imgs / `265` comps)** stratified by `(ComponentMaxTarget x BilateralDiscordant x DominantMorphology)` -> keeps Malignant prevalence within `0.15%` (`70.31% / 70.36% / 70.21%`), PLUS deterministic **`5-fold` `dev_fold_5`** strictly inside `train + val` (`634–636` imgs per fold, zero overlap with sealed `internal_test`).
  2. **Zero-Shot External & Localization Tracks (`0%` Primary Training Exposure):** Keep `DS0006` (`external_eval_loc`, `919` scans / `569` lesion masks, with secondary patient-grouped `cohort_subsplit` `70/15/15`), `DS0005` (`external_test_bmcd`), `DS0002` (`external_test_dmid`), and `DS0001` (`external_test_mias_sfm`) strictly isolated until `G11/G12`.

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- **Rejected:** Image-level random splits (`causes multi-view patient leakage`); pooling `CMMD + CDD-CESM + BMCD + DMID` into one internal training split (`destroys multi-center zero-shot external validation power for RQ2 & RQ3`); leaking `internal_test` into K-fold hyperparameter search.

## 6. Recommendation
Ratify **`SPLIT0001`** (`70/15/15` primary `DS0003` + `5-fold` dev CV + sealed `internal_test` + 4 zero-shot external cohorts `DS0006/05/02/01`).
