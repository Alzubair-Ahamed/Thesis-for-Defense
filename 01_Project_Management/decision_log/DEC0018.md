## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD18`** (blocks `STEP-015` & `G05`; feeds `G13`).

## 2. Decision Question
- How is qualitative XAI usefulness assessed retrospectively without crossing Section 8's ban on real-patient clinical trials (`RF15`), and what honest fallback applies if no qualified breast radiologist completes scoring?

## 3. `REPORT FACT` Locators
- `RF15`: Explicit exclusion of clinical patient trials or PACS clinical deployment.
- `RF11 / O5`: Visual explanations supporting diagnostic interpretability.

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
Adopt [qualitative_review_rubric.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/xai_protocols/qualitative_review_rubric.md):
1. **Stratified `N = 40` Blinded Sample:** `10 TP, 10 FN, 10 FP, 10 TN` selected deterministically post-`G11` across `ACR` density `A–D` & `mass/calcification` (`zero cherry-picking`), blinded to GT/confidence.
2. **Structured `1–5` Likert Rubric:** Anatomical accuracy, marker/border shortcut absence (`RISK0004`), morphological plausibility, and interpretability value (`plus FN/FP failure taxonomy`).
3. **Mandatory Non-Fabrication Fallback:** If no qualified breast radiologist is available before `G13`, record **`QualitativeReviewStatus = Unresolved_NotPerformed_AdvisorUnavailable`** honestly and rely solely on quantitative `XAIP0001` metrics (`§5–§8`).

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- **Rejected:** Unblinded qualitative cherry-picking of `3–5` clean TP examples, treating student/AI impressions as radiological validation, or framing retrospective review as a clinical workflow trial (`RF15` violation).

## 6. Recommendation
Ratify **`UD18`** ([qualitative_review_rubric.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/xai_protocols/qualitative_review_rubric.md)) at **`G05`**.
