## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD17`** (blocks `STEP-015` & `G05`; feeds `G09`, `G11`, `G12`).

## 2. Decision Question
- Which exact causal faithfulness perturbations, Adebayo sanity checks, chance-normalized localization metrics, and seed/input stability metrics govern quantitative XAI evaluation at `STEP-035`?

## 3. `REPORT FACT` Locators
- `RF08`, `RF11`: Quantitative validation supporting explainable mammogram detection (`RQ2–RQ4`, `GAP0001`).

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
Lock all `4` Q1 quantitative pillars prospectively in [XAIP0001.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/xai_protocols/XAIP0001.md) (`§5–§8`):
1. **Faithfulness:** **`Deletion AUC`**, **`Insertion AUC`** (`Gaussian blur sigma=15` baseline), **`ROAD`** linear neighbor imputation (`5%` steps `0–100%`), plus fixed-seed random-mask control.
2. **Sanity Checks:** **Adebayo cascading & independent parameter randomization** + **label-permutation control**, measured via Spearman $\rho$, SSIM, and HOG similarity (`flagging Sanity_Failed_EdgeInvariance if rho > 0.30 or SSIM > 0.50`).
3. **ROI Localization (`Post-Hoc Only`):** **`Pointing Game`** (`0px & 15px`), chance-normalized **`Delta-PG`** (`PG - ROI/BreastArea`), **`EBPG`**, and thresholded **`IoU/Dice`** (`top-10%, top-20%, 0.50`) on verified `DS0006/DS0005/DS0002` masks (`+ coarse DS0001 SFM separately`).
4. **Stability:** Benign input-perturbation (`±2%` contrast, `±2px` shift) SSIM/$\rho$, multi-seed consistency, and inter-XAI agreement.

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- **Rejected:** Visual heatmaps alone (`Q1 rejection risk`), zero-pixel deletion without `ROAD` (`OOD sharp-edge artifact`), and naive `PG` without `Delta-PG` (`large-lesion size inflation`).
