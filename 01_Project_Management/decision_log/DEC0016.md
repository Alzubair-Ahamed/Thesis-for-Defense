## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD16`** (blocks `STEP-015` & `G05`; feeds `G09`, `G11`, `G12`).

## 2. Decision Question
- Which XAI attribution methods complement the mandatory `Grad-CAM` baseline (`RF08`), how are architecture-specific target layers and logit targets bound before training, and which methods are explicitly excluded?

## 3. `REPORT FACT` Locators
- `RF08`: Grad-CAM explainability over transfer learning mammogram predictions (`pp. 3, 5, 7–9`).

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
Adopt [XAIP0001.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/xai_protocols/XAIP0001.md) (`§1–§4`):
- **Approved Suite:** `Grad-CAM` (mandatory baseline) + `Grad-CAM++` + `LayerCAM` (high-res spatial maps for small calcifications) + `Score-CAM` (gradient-free) + `Integrated Gradients` (`50`-step axiomatic attribution), with **`Guided Backpropagation`** tracked strictly as a **negative sanity control** (`§6`).
- **Rejected:** `LIME` / `KernelSHAP` (SLIC boundary fragmentation on continuous parenchyma and prohibitive latency vs `Score-CAM/ROAD`).
- **Pre-Test Binding:** Target raw pre-sigmoid Malignant logit $z_{\text{mal}}$ (`non-collapsed rule in §4`); bind exact module strings when `UD13` closes (`STEP-022 / G09`) and lock at `G11`.

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- `LayerCAM` & `Score-CAM` directly address `Grad-CAM`'s coarse `7x7` spatial bottleneck on microcalcifications (`BMCD`, `CDD-CESM`), while raw-logit extraction prevents post-sigmoid gradient saturation bias.

## 6. Recommendation
Ratify **`UD16`** (`XAIP0001 §1–§4`) at **`G05`**.
