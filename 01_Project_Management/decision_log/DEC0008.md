## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD08`** (blocks `STEP-014` & `G05`; feeds `G06`).

## 2. Decision Question
- How are heterogeneous upstream diagnostic labels (`biopsy pathology`, `BI-RADS 1–6`, `Normal`, `DMID 'N'`, `CMMD unlabeled contralateral`, `CDD-CESM postop/NAC`, `BMCD prior vs recent`, and multi-lesion exams) mapped deterministically to binary `0 / 1` or explicit `Excluded_*` tags?

## 3. REPORT FACT (`RF`) Locators
- `RF05`: Binary Benign vs Malignant ground-truth target (`0 / 1`).

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
Auditing raw clinical spreadsheets on `2026-11-28` revealed `5` critical traps requiring strict rules (`§2–§4` of [label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/protocols/label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md)):
1. **`CMMD` (`DS0003`):** Join strictly via `PatientID + DICOM ImageLaterality` (`3,744` annotated scans); exclude `~1,452` unannotated contralateral scans (`Excluded_UnlabeledContralateral`); apply malignant dominance at patient aggregation across `30` bilateral benign+malignant patients while retaining exact breast-side truth; exclude conflicting clone patients `D1-0808/D1-1292` (`4` scans total).
2. **`CDD-CESM` (`DS0006`):** Exclude `80` `postoperative` + `4` `postneoajuvant chemotherapy` LE scans (`removing all 4 BIRADS=6 rows incl. P156`); report **`Sub-Track A (Pure Biopsy: 304 Malignant vs 277 Benign = 581)`** separately from **`Sub-Track B (Full Non-Malignant: 304 Malignant vs 615 Benign+Normal = 919)`**.
3. **`BMCD` (`DS0005`):** Exclude `196` `prior` normal baseline scans from `Suspicious_cases`; map biopsy-confirmed `recent` suspicious (`11` Malignant/DCIS vs `21` Benign) cleanly; exclude `18` suspicious `Biopsy = NOT AVAILABLE` folders from biopsy truth; isolate `Normal_cases` (`BI-RADS 1–2`) inside disclosed `NonMalignant` track (`alongside G04-quarantined Normal_40/Suspicious_16`).
4. **`DMID` (`DS0002`):** Strictly exclude all **`36` spreadsheet `'N'` rows** (`verified via Reports/Img*.txt as BI-RADS 3/4a/4b/4c/0/1 active findings — NOT Normal!`) as `Excluded_AmbiguousClassToken_N`; map `B (157) / M (118)` (`and NORM 200 inside disclosed NonMalignant track`).
5. **`MIAS` (`DS0001`):** Historical `B/M/NORM` tagged as `Tier-B` SFM external sensitivity track (`zero silent biopsy claim`).

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- **Rejected:** Silent coercion of `DMID 'N'` -> `Normal (0)`, silent labeling of `CMMD` unannotated contralateral breasts as `0`, or mixing `Normal (BI-RADS 1)` silently into `Biopsy Benign` without dual sub-track reporting (`RISK0003`).

## 6. Recommendation
Adopt **Protocol §2–§4** ([label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/protocols/label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md)) and verify on the `170`-image `STEP-016` pilot prior to `G06` freeze.
