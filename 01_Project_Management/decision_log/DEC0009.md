- Which exact ROI-mask sources, polygon/color extraction rules, union conventions, alignment checks, and non-leakage safeguards govern quantitative XAI localization evaluation (`RQ3`)?

## 3. REPORT FACT (`RF`) Locators
- `RF08`: Grad-CAM localization evaluation against breast pathology / findings (`quantitative XAI grounding required by GAP0003 & RQ3`).

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
Empirical format verification on `2026-11-28` established exact mask engineering constraints (`§5–§6` of [label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/protocols/label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md)):
1. **`DS0006 CDD-CESM LE` (`Primary Exact Polygonal Localization`):** Rasterize `1,797` `DM` polygon rows (`657` LE images) from `Radiology_hand_drawn_segmentations_v2.csv` (`VIA JSON`) via `cv2.fillPoly` + logical OR union onto native image `(Height, Width)`.
2. **`DS0005 BMCD` (`Secondary Microcalcification Localization`):** Extract chromatic overlays (`max(R,G,B) - min(R,G,B) > 25`) from `*_GT.jpg` (`392` retained pairs) and aspect-checked `INTER_NEAREST` rescale onto DICOM `(Height, Width)`.
3. **`DS0002 DMID` (`Secondary Indian FFDM Localization`):** **Hard-ban `ROI Masks/*.tif`** (`268/269` fail `libtiff` with `OSError: decoder error -2` from malformed `SampleFormat=(1,1,1)` tag!). Use **`Pixel-level annotation/*.tif`** (`269/269` clean `RGBA` overlays matching DICOM dimensions) via color-channel extraction (`diff > 25`) cross-checked with `Metadata.xlsx (x, y, radius)` centroids.
4. **`DS0001 MIAS` (`Coarse SFM Check`):** Rasterize `(x, y, radius)` with MIAS bottom-left `y`-flip; exclude cases lacking numeric radius from localization denominator.
5. **`DS0003 CMMD`:** Native `0` masks (`LocalizationEligible = False` — zero invented boxes).
6. **Strict Post-Hoc Boundary:** Masks NEVER enter classification training, validation selection, or inference inputs.

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- **Rejected:** Attempting naive ingestion of corrupt `DMID ROI Masks/*.tif` (`would break pipeline or drop 268 cases silently`), or resizing overlays without verifying aspect parity (`BMCD *_GT.jpg`), or leaking ROI bounding coordinates into training classifiers.

## 6. Recommendation
Ratify **Protocol §5–§6** ([label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md](../../04_Labels_XAI_Governance/protocols/label_mask_protocol_v1.0.0.md)) alongside `STEP-015` `XAIP0001` at **`G05`**.
