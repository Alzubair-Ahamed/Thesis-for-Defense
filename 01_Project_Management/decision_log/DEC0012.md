## 1. UD Reference
- **`UD12`** (blocks `STEP-019` completion & feeds `STEP-021 / G08`).

## 2. Decision Question
- How should multi-cohort mammograms (`8-bit`, `12-bit BMCD`, `16-bit CMMD`, identical-channel `RGB` `DMID`/`CDD-CESM`, and `SFM` `MIAS`) be standardized, cropped, resized (`512x512` vs `384x384` vs `224x224`), co-transformed with verified `1,121` ROI masks, normalized without leakage, enhanced (`CLAHE` ablation), and augmented solely during training?

## 3. REPORT FACT (`RF`) Locators
- `RF10`: Report notes standard preprocessing categories (`resizing`, `normalization`, `augmentation`) without fixing mammogram bit-depth scaling, aspect-ratio padding, border cropping, or CLAHE ablation separation.
- Measured QC inputs (`DS0001–0006_quality.md`): `5,194` `uint8` + `2` `uint16` (`IMG00003519–3520`) in `CMMD`; `400` `12-bit uint16` (`4095`) in `BMCD`; `488` identical `RGB` + `22` `MONOCHROME2` in `DMID`; `1,002` tightly-cropped distinct sizes in `CDD-CESM`; wide horizontal black scanner bars + film tags in `MIAS`.

## 4. `PLANNING RECOMMENDATION` Options & Evidence
1. **Intensity & Polarity Normalization (`Adopted Design`):** Dynamic header/dtype divisor (`255` vs `4095` for 12-bit `BMCD` vs `65535` for 16-bit `CMMD`), `MONOCHROME1 -> MONOCHROME2` inversion, single-channel collapse of `R=G=B`, `[0.0, 1.0]` `float32`.
2. **Resolution & Aspect-Ratio Options:**
   - *Option A (`224x224` direct warp):* Severe calcification blur + geometric aspect distortion on tall `1:1.3–1:1.5` mammograms (`CDD-CESM` narrow crops distort by `2x+`).
   - *Option B (`384x384` aspect-preserved pad):* Lower VRAM cost, moderate microcalcification loss.
   - ***Option C (`512x512` aspect-preserving resize + symmetric zero-pad — RECOMMENDED):*** Scales longest side to `512` (`INTER_AREA` downsample) with symmetric `0.0` letterbox pad, paired `1:1` with `INTER_NEAREST` `{0, 1}` ROI mask & exact invertible `SpatialTransformMeta`; preserves fine `BMCD`/`CDD-CESM` calcifications across `ImageNet` CNNs (`UD13`) while keeping batch sizes practical.
3. **Foreground Bounding & Artifact Policy:** Largest foreground bbox + `16 px` (`2%`) margin (auto-skipped if area `>= 92%`), **enforcing `clipped_roi_pixels == 0`** (`automatically expanding bbox if any verified lesion touches breast border`, protecting `CDD-CESM` border-clip cases). Corner film/CAD labels (`MIAS R ML`, `BMCD RCC`) remain unmasked in `PP0001` for honest `RISK0004` XAI shortcut auditing.
4. **Baseline vs CLAHE Ablation:** Keep `PP0001` (`pp_baseline.yaml`) CLAHE-free (`clahe.enabled = false`) and isolate `PP0002` (`pp_clahe_ablation.yaml`, `uint16` CLAHE `clip_limit=2.0`, `8x8` tiles) strictly as a controlled comparative ablation (`RQ1`/`RQ3`).
5. **Leakage-Free Normalization & Training-Only Augmentations:** Default `imagenet_rgb` stats (or `train_zscore` enforcing `FitPartition == "train"` post-`G07`). On-the-fly training augmentations limited to horizontal flip (`p=0.5`), rotation `±10°`, shift `±5%`, scale `[0.95, 1.05]`, intensity/gamma `±8–10%`; **vertical flip, aggressive cropping `<0.85`, cutout/random erasing, and MixUp strictly banned**.

## 5. Tradeoffs & Rejected Alternatives
- **Rejected:** Hardcoding `/255` (`breaks 12-bit BMCD & 16-bit CMMD`), squashing mammograms directly to square (`distorts microcalcification/spiculation morphology`), baking CLAHE permanently into baseline without ablation (`conflates raw transfer performance`), or vertical flipping (`anatomically invalid MLO orientation`).

## 6. Recommendation
Ratify **`PP0001` (`512x512` aspect-preserved baseline)** vs **`PP0002` (`CLAHE` controlled ablation)** + `transforms.py` co-transform contract (`zero fitting/materialization until G07 PASS`).
