## Governing Terms Audited (`STEP-011`)
1. **Permissions:** Free to use, copy, adapt, and cite for research under `CC BY 4.0` with mandatory DOI (`10.7937/29kw-ae92`) and TCIA attribution; derived weights and de-identified figures permitted under [TCIA Data Usage Policy](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/data-usage-policies-and-restrictions/).
2. **Modality & Scope Check (`RF15`, `OS §4.1`):** `326` patients / `2,006` lossless `.jpg` images (`1,003` Low-Energy [`0.64 GB`] standard 2D `FFDM` mammograms + `1,003` contrast-subtracted [`0.82 GB`] images), plus `3.92 MB` DOCX reports, `991.32 KB` polygonal segmentation `CSV`, and `238.81 KB` clinical `XLSX`. Under `RF15`, standard 2D modeling uses **Low-Energy (`LE`) images only** (`contrast-subtracted subset isolated/excluded`).
3. **Intake Caveat:** Main `1.49 GB` image bundle on TCIA is served via **IBM Aspera Faspex** (`requires Aspera Connect plugin`), whereas clinical reports/segmentations/XLSX are direct HTTP downloads.
