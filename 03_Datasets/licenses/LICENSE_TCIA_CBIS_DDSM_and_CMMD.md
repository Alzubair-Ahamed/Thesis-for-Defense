## Governing Terms Audited (`STEP-011`)
1. **No Re-identification:** Users must agree not to use the datasets, alone or in combination with other data, to identify, re-identify, or contact individual subjects or institutions.
2. **Permitted Research & Cloud Processing:** Under `CC BY 3.0` / `CC BY 4.0`, copying, preprocessing, formatting, and processing on local or private/institutional cloud GPU instances is permitted subject to TCIA de-identification rules.
3. **Figures in Thesis / Q1 Papers:** De-identified mammogram images and Grad-CAM heatmaps may be published with mandatory DOI attribution and acknowledgment of TCIA (NIH-designated data repository).
4. **Derived Model Weights (`UD24`):** Permitted under `CC BY` derivative terms provided upstream dataset DOIs and TCIA attribution accompany model cards/repositories.
5. **Intake Readiness:** **Open public download**, gated only on institutional **DIU secondary-data ethics/waiver record (`UD06`)** prior to `STEP-012`.
