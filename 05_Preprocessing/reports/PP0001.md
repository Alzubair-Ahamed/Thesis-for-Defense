## 2. Operations & Bit-Depth Policy
1. **Channel Collapse & Photometric Normalization:** Multi-channel RGB DICOM/JPEG inputs (`DS0002` `448` RGB, `DS0006` `919` RGB JPEG) collapsed to 2D luminance; `MONOCHROME1` inversion supported (`monochrome1_invert: true`); DICOM `RescaleSlope` and `RescaleIntercept` applied (`apply_dicom_rescale: true`).
2. **Bit-Depth Normalization & Lossless Materialization:** Dynamic scaling (`8-bit /255`, `12-bit /4095`, `16-bit /65535`) to `[0.0, 1.0]` `float32`, materialized on disk as `512x512` single-channel **`16-bit` (`uint16`) lossless PNG** (`round(image_01 * 65535)`) so 12-bit `DS0005` BMCD and 16-bit `DS0003` CMMD dynamic precision is preserved without 8-bit quantization.
3. **Bit-Depth Observed Distribution (`5,709` scans):** `8-bit/MONOCHROME2 = 4,999`, `8-bit/RGB = 448`, `12-bit/MONOCHROME2 = 260`, `16-bit/MONOCHROME2 = 2`.

- **Conservative Foreground Crop:** Otsu/low-air threshold (`air_threshold = 0.02`), disconnected border marker filtering (`min_component_area_frac = 0.005`), union over foreground + `roi_mask` (when present) padded by `max(16 px, 2%)`, auto-skipped when coverage `>= 92%` (`skip_if_area_coverage_ge = 0.92`), with hard assertion `enforce_zero_roi_clipping = true`.
