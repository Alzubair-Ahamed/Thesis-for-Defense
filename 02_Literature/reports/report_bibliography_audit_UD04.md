## 1. Executive Summary of `UD04` Findings

Under [OS §1.2 (`RF17–RF18`)](../../BC_XAI_RESEARCH_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md#os-01-2) and [protocol.md §7](../searches/protocol.md), every printed entry in `Title_defence_evaluation_report.pdf` `p. 6 Table 1.1 (SL 1–10)` and `p. 10 Section 9 References ([1]–[12])` — registered as seed records `P0063–P0075` — was audited directly against primary publisher portals (Europe PMC JATS full-text XML, Crossref/DOI records, and academic indexers).

**Key Forensic Findings:**
1. **Zero Valid 2D Mammogram Primary DL Comparators in `Table 1.1` (`0 / 10`):** Not a single entry in `SL 1–10` represents an unconfounded primary 2D mammography CNN/XAI comparative benchmark suitable for `Bucket B1/B2`.
2. **Severe Full-Text Integrity & Dataset Mismatch in `SL 1` / `[1]` (`P0063`, Khalid et al. 2023):** Full-text inspection of `PMC10572157` ([P0063.md](../summaries/P0063.md)) proves that despite the Abstract claiming *"3002 merged pictures gathered from 1501 individuals who had digital mammography"*, Section 4 (`Table 3`) and Section 5 analyze the **569-row tabular `Wisconsin Breast Cancer Diagnostic (WDBC)` Kaggle CSV** (`Radius_Mean`, `Texture_Mean`, etc.) using classical ML (`Random Forest = 96.49%`) with benign-only outlier removal and zero CNN results in the empirical tables. The report's `Table 1.1 SL 1` uncritically copied `"Mammogram Images"` and `"96.49%"` from the Abstract/Figure 9.
3. **Citation-Mismatch Confirmation on `SL 10` vs Ref `[10]` (`P0073` vs `P0072`) & `SL 5` vs Ref `[5]` (`P0067`):** `Table 1.1 SL 10` cites *"Hassan et al. [10] | EfficientNet + CNN | Histopathology Dataset | 99%+"*, whereas printed Ref `[10]` on `p. 10` is **D. Bazazeh & R. Shubair (2016)** (*IEEE ICEDSA*, `10.1109/ICEDSA.2016.7818560`, classical ML comparison on Wisconsin tabular data). Similarly, `SL 5` attributes a `"97% CNN"` experiment to `[5]`, whereas **A.-A. Nahid & Y. Kong (2017)** (`10.1155/2017/3781951`) is a **histopathology survey paper**.
4. **Hard Scope Boundary Enforcement (`RF15`):** Works on **WDBC tabular features** (`[1]`, `[6]–[10]`), **BreakHis/histopathology images** (`[2]`, `[5]`, `SL10`), and **secondary reviews** (`[3]`, `[4]`, `[5]`) are strictly quarantined under `EX01_NON_MAMMO_MODALITY` / `B4_CTX` (excluded from 2D mammogram baseline comparisons). Uncited epidemiology reports `[11]` (WHO 2024) and `[12]` (ACS 2024) are verified for background Introduction context only and scheduled for proper bracketed in-text citation in Thesis Chapter 1.
5. **Remaining Physical-PDF Verification Item (`[6]`–`[8]`):** Because local gitignored `Title_defence_evaluation_report.pdf` is stored outside this checkout ([T04_SEED_title_report.md §10](../searches/T04_SEED_title_report.md)), exact bibliographic title/author strings for the ink-annotated `[6]`–`[8]` entries on PDF `p. 10` (`P0068–P0070`) remain marked `Pending_Physical_PDF_Check` for supervisor confirmation before `G03`, while their substantive quarantine as Wisconsin tabular studies (`RF17`) stands locked.
