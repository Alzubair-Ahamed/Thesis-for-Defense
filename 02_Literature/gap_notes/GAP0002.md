## 1. Evidence of Systemic Irreproducibility Across High-Accuracy Mammography Papers
- **Pre-split augmentation / oversampling leakage (`+10–15%` inflation):** `P0004` augments `266 -> 1,064` before 90/10 split; `P0005` claims `98–99%` with ambiguous pre-CV augmentation; **`P0023`** evaluates `9,943` oversampled images (`3,937` BI-RADS 5) synthesized from just **`24` BI-RADS 5 images (`6` patients)** to report `99.0%` vs `98.02%`.
- **Diagnostic-vs-screening contamination:** `P0035` proves including diagnostic mammograms inflated Mirai's 5-year AUC by `+0.05` (`0.76 -> 0.71`).
- **Side-level vs patient-level evaluation confound (`P0385`):** Pooling breasts independently vs patient-level `OR` aggregation creates a **`+17.5` AUC gap (`0.946 vs 0.770`)** on `CMMD` while masking `CMMD`'s **`96.2%` patient malignancy prevalence** (specificity `0.03–0.22`).
- **Cross-dataset semantic mismatch (`P0039`, `P0040`):** Unbiopsied consensus `BI-RADS` screening triage (`VinDr-Mammo`, zero BI-RADS 6) cannot be conflated silently with biopsy-confirmed cohorts (`CMMD`, `INbreast`, `CBIS-DDSM`).

## 2. Q1 Opportunity (`RQ1`)
- Establishing a **connected-component patient-grouped benchmark** (`O1, O2, O4`) that isolates biopsy pathology tracks from BI-RADS screening triage, reports both **patient-level and side-level AUCs**, prevents test-set preprocessing leakage (`fit-free` or `train-only`), and pairs classic/modern CNNs + hybrids under identical seeds directly resolves the `98%`-illusion vs `0.78–0.90` reality divide.
