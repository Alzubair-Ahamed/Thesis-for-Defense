## 1. The Split Between External Calibration Benchmarks and External XAI Benchmarks
- **`Sabry 2026` (`P0018`):** Confirms only **`18.5%` (`12/65`)** of breast imaging AI papers test independent external cohorts.
- **External Calibration & Shift Without XAI (`P0034`, `P0037`):** `P0034` tests zero-shot transfer across 4 countries (`CBIS-DDSM -> CMMD, DMID, MIAS`), documenting `-0.165 to -0.320` AUC collapse and **`ECE 0.186–0.446`** where temperature scaling fails (`<= -0.049`) — yet evaluates **zero XAI methods**. `P0037` documents `-0.09 C-index` hardware shift (`Philips vs GE`, `p<0.001`) in `134k` women while noting Mirai lacks XAI and needs system-specific calibration.
- **External XAI Without Calibration (`P0014`, `P0675`):** `P0675` proves `VGG-16 Grad-CAM` collapses externally (`0.64 -> 0.16` `Activation Precision`) while `IAIA-BL` preserves `0.92–1.00` focus yet still misclassifies `85.5%` (`65/76`) of benign external masses due to threshold/score shift (`Fig. 7`), without formally computing `ECE`, `Brier`, or full-image detection!
- **Modern `VinDr-Mammo` Baselines Without External Test or Calibration (`P1030`, `P1433`):** Both train/evaluate cleanly on `VinDr-Mammo` internally yet omit zero-shot external validation (`INbreast`, `CMMD`, `CBIS-DDSM`) and omit `ECE/Brier`.

## 2. Flagship Q1 Synthesis (`RQ2`)
- **Unexplored Cross-Center Bridge:** Training/evaluating across isolated public `FFDM` + `SFM` benchmarks (`e.g., VinDr-Mammo, CMMD, INbreast, CBIS-DDSM, MIAS/DMID`) while **jointly tracking zero-shot AUC shift + `ECE/Brier` + `Spec@95%Sens` drift + XAI faithfulness/localization retention** (`DeLong + clustered bootstrap + Holm-Bonferroni/BH-FDR`) directly unites the isolated contributions of `P0034`, `P0675`, and `P1030`.
