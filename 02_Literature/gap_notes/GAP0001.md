## 1. Original Report Premise Tested (`RF16`, `p. 5, 7–8`)
- *"Most previous research does not integrate Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) techniques into the diagnostic process."*

## 2. Counterevidence Test (`352` Stage-1 + Extracted `B1/B3_METH` Works)
- **Empirical Refutation of Naive Premise:** Combined Stage-1 database screening (`335` Open-DB `B1` + `17` local `B1`) and Systematic Reviews (`P0003`, `P0006`, `P0017`, `P0018`, `P0022`, `P0156`) show that **CNN + qualitative Grad-CAM / SHAP / LIME in mammography is already heavily saturated**. Any thesis claim asserting "we are the first to apply Grad-CAM to mammogram CNNs" would face immediate Q1 desk-rejection.

## 3. Surviving True Q1 Gap (`Why Plausibility != Verification`)
- **`Cerekci 2024` (`P0012`)**, **`Pertuz 2023` (`P0014`)**, **`Kag 2026` (`P0016`)**: Show vanilla CNN saliency fails localization rigorously (`PG = 0.115–0.41`; median `Dice = 0.042–0.380`).
- **`Ciardiello 2026` (`P0022`) & `Pesapane 2026` (`P0156`)**: Prove XAI evaluation in mammography over-indexes visual/box `Coherence` (`~33/35` papers in `P0022`), while only `8/35` check `Correctness` (faithfulness), **`2/14` (`P0156`) test external XAI transportability**, and `0/14` conduct reader workflow checks.
- **Unclosed Triplet:** Even 2026 landmarks (`Kumar P0015`: internal `Deletion/Insertion/PG` at `224x224` without `Adebayo` sanity; `LENS P1030`: internal `PG 73.1%` without external test or causal perturbation; `Moffett P0675`: ROI-only external `ActPrec` without `Deletion/Insertion` or `Adebayo`) leave a clear gap: **zero full-image, patient-disjoint FFDM benchmarks co-evaluate causal faithfulness (`Deletion/Insertion/ROAD`) + `Adebayo` model/data randomization sanity checks + chance-normalized ROI localization (`PG/Delta-PG/IoU`) + seed stability**.
