## 1. Converging Evidence on Density Masking, Resolution Collapse & Architecture Choices
- **Dense Tissue XAI & Diagnostic Degradation (`P0810`, `P0033`, `P0039`, `P0041`):** East Asian `FFDM` cohorts (`VinDr-Mammo 89.96% C+D`, `Taizhou 89.1% C+D`) are overwhelmingly dense. `P0810` reveals `DenseNet-121 Grad-CAM` loses **`-37.3` percentage points** of chance-normalized localization (`Delta-PG`, `p=0.008`) inside dense tissue (`98%` spurious glandular distraction). `P0034` shows naive **Density-Aware Focal Loss (`DAF`)** hurts both AUC (`0.836->0.806`) and `ECE` (`doubling to 0.104–0.116`).
- **Resolution vs Architecture Inductive Bias (`P0009, P0016, P0385, P1030`):** At `224x224`, CNNs beat vanilla `ViT-B/16` (`P0385`) and `DINOv2/Rad-DINO` (`P0034`), yet CAM Pointing Game collapses to `<=11.5%` (`P0016`) and subtle microcalcifications disappear. Conversely, higher/rectangular resolutions (`1024x512` `LENS`/`Swin-B` in `P1030` or `1664x2048` in `P0036`) restore spatial sensitivity (`PG 73.1%`, `Macro-AUC 87.6–90.2%`) — **yet `P1030` and `P1433` omit `BI-RADS A–D` density breakdowns and omit CLAHE/normalization vs resolution ablations under calibration & causal XAI**.

## 2. Controlled Experimental Target (`RQ4`, `O2, O3, O5`)
- Enables tightly controlled single-factor ablations under matched splits (`UD12–UD16`): quantifying whether higher-resolution / aspect-preserving preprocessing (`vs 224x224`), `CLAHE`, or modern CNN/hybrid backbones (`ConvNeXt / Swin / LENS-style vs ResNet50 / DenseNet121 / EfficientNetB0`) improve **both dense-breast calibration (`ECE`) and chance-normalized XAI faithfulness (`Deletion/Insertion + Delta-PG`)**.
