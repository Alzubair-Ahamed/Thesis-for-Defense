## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Design a compact custom CNN (`LBNet`, 2.4M params) combining multi-stage filtering, SHAP + Grad-CAM explainability, and cross-dataset validation across RSNA, CBIS-DDSM, and MIAS (Abstract, p. 1; pp. 2–3).
- **Task:** Binary `Cancer` vs `Non-Cancer` 2D mammographic classification (pp. 4, 9–10).
- **Modality:** **Pure 2D mammography** (RSNA FFDM mirror + Mendeley CBIS-DDSM/MIAS digitized SFM; whole/center-cropped images resized down to `64x64`, Tables 3–4, p. 8; though p. 14 mentions `256x256x3` TPU computation).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **RSNA Breast Cancer Dataset (Kaggle mirror `hey24sheep/rsnabreastcancerdataset`, Ref [39], p. 4):** Raw `8,013` JPEGs (`4,015` Cancer, `3,998` Non-Cancer; 640x640) -> augmented globally to **`12,338`** (`6,134` Cancer, `6,204` Non-Cancer; p. 6; 5-fold validation sums in Table 5 p. 11 & Fig. 6 p. 12 equal **`12,338`**). Patient counts: `not reported`.
- **External Mirrors (Mendeley Data `10.17632/ywsbh3ndr8.2`, Ref [40], pp. 4, 6, 13):**
  - **CBIS-DDSM:** Stated `12,328` (`7,158`/`5,170`) -> balanced `12,000` -> `2,400` evaluated (`1,200`/`1,200`, Table 6, p. 13).
  - **MIAS:** Stated `3,816` (`1,440`/`2,376` — ~12x pre-augmented mirror of 322 MIAS films) -> balanced `12,000` -> `2,400` evaluated (`1,200`/`1,200`, Table 6, p. 13). Selection rule (`12k -> 2.4k`) & patient counts: `not reported`.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Binary `Cancer` vs `Non-Cancer` inherited from Kaggle/Mendeley mirrors (pp. 4, 18); biopsy vs BI-RADS provenance and Normal vs Benign mixture in `Non-Cancer`: `not reported`.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`5fold_cv_post_aug_no_patient_split`**. Table 5 & Fig. 6 fold supports sum to `12,338` (post-augmentation size), demonstrating severe **pre-CV augmentation leakage** across folds (Fold 1 `92.74%` -> Fold 5 `99.84%`) alongside absent patient-ID grouping.
- **External Validation:** Claimed on Mendeley CBIS-DDSM & MIAS (`2,400` images each, Table 6), confounded by pre-augmented mirrors, undocumented subsampling, and ambiguous zero-shot vs retraining wording (pp. 6, 10).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`LBNet` (scratch, `2,408,386` trainable params, Fig. 5 p. 7, Table 3 p. 8):** 5 Conv2D (`48, 128, 192, 192, 128`) + ReLU + BN + MaxPool + Dense head (`3,062` text p. 6 vs `1024->1024->2` Fig. 5; `(64,64,1)` Table 3 vs `(64,64,3)` Table 4 vs `(256,256,3)` Table 7 TPU contradiction!). Adam (`lr=1e-4`, weight decay `1e-7`, `50` ep, batch `32`, Sparse CE `from_logits=True` on Softmax!, EarlyStopping patience `10`, LR `x0.1` every 10 ep).
- **6 Baselines (scratch & ImageNet TL, p. 9):** VGG19, InceptionV3, ResNet152V2, DenseNet201, ResNeXt101, SE-ResNet152 (no baseline hyperparameter/metric table). Random seeds: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Stacked enhancement (p. 4): CLAHE -> Gaussian blur -> Bilateral filter -> Non-local means -> Unsharp masking (`alpha=1.5`; filter params & normalization `not reported`). Unmasked `L-CC`/`CC` markers visible (Figs. 3, 4, 8, 9).
- **Augmentation (pre-CV global, pp. 4–6; Fig. 4):** ±15° rotation, center crop, brightness/contrast, random scaling, H/V flips (ablation Table 2 p. 6 shows H-flip removal drops acc `97.28% -> 88.92%`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Methods:** **SHAP** (`DeepExplainer`) & **Grad-CAM** (Algorithm 1, p. 8; Figs. 8–9, pp. 13–14).
- **Target Layer:** Generic `"last convolutional layer"` (p. 9; Table 3 calls final conv `Conv2D_4`).
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`2` SHAP plots Fig. 8, `4` Grad-CAM overlays Fig. 9, plus qualitative visual scan of `1,000` RSNA maps without recorded pass/fail counts, p. 10). Zero deletion/insertion/ROAD, zero Adebayo sanity checks, zero ROI overlap (noted in Conclusion, p. 17). Fig. 9 top-right FP highlights the corner `CC` text tag.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **RSNA 5-fold CV (N=12,338, Table 5, p. 11; Fig. 6, p. 12):** Fold Acc `92.74%–99.84%`; aggregated Acc **`97.28%`** (vs `97.02%` in Fig. 13, p. 16); Cancer P/R/Spec/F1 = `99%/96%/99%/97%`.
- **Mendeley External (N=2,400 each, Table 6, p. 13):** CBIS-DDSM Acc **`99.54%`** (contradicts `34/2,400` misclassified = `98.58%` on p. 10!); MIAS Acc **`98.50%`**.
- **Baselines (RSNA summary text/plots only, Abstract p. 1; pp. 11, 14):** Scratch VGG19 `87.54%`, SE-ResNet152 `87.50%`; TL ResNet152V2 `87.37%`, DenseNet201 `87.15%`.
- **Efficiency (Table 7, p. 17):** `2.40M` params, `7.92 GFLOPs`, `23.33 ms/img` (computed on `256x256x3` TPU logs despite `64x64` T4 GPU claim on p. 9!).
- **AUC-ROC, Calibration (ECE/Brier), 95% CIs, Statistical Tests (DeLong/McNemar):** `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (pp. 15, 17):** Heavy SHAP runtime, coarse Grad-CAM without quantitative ROI overlap, single grayscale modality.
- **Reviewer critique:** Classic `RF16` counterexample demonstrating major methodological vulnerabilities: (1) global augmentation before 5-fold CV (`12,338` sum); (2) pre-augmented third-party Mendeley mirrors; (3) severe internal contradictions (`64x64x1` vs `64x64x3` vs `256x256x3`, `3,062` vs `1,024` dense, `T4` vs `TPU`, `from_logits=True` on Softmax, `99.54%` vs `34/2400`, `97.28%` vs `97.02%`, Fig. 6(f) `5582` vs `5882`); (4) zero AUC-ROC, zero calibration, zero CIs/stats, zero quantitative XAI, and corner `CC` shortcut saliency.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Strictly Annotated/Quarantined for Leakage & Mirror Defects):** Direct 2D mammography CNN+Grad-CAM+SHAP paper spanning RSNA/CBIS-DDSM/MIAS; essential negative-control evidence justifying patient-level splitting, native high resolution, AUC-ROC, calibration, and quantitative XAI.
