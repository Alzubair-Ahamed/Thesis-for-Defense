## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Samara Acosta-Jiménez, Miguel M. Mendoza-Mendoza, Carlos E. Galván-Tejada, José M. Celaya-Padilla, Jorge I. Galván-Tejada, and Manuel A. Soto-Murillo.
- **Title:** *Explainable Deep Learning for Breast Lesion Classification in Digital and Contrast-Enhanced Mammography*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Diagnostics* (MDPI; **2024 JIF 3.3, Q2** WoS), **2025**, Vol. `15`, Issue `24`, Article `3143`.
- **DOI / IDs:** `10.3390/diagnostics15243143` | `PMCID: PMC12732085` | `PMID: 41464144`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`CDD-CESM XAI Comparator — Directly Compares DM vs CESM Using SHAP`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Compare `ResNet-18`, `DenseNet-121`, and `EfficientNet-B0` across `3` binary tasks (`Normal vs Benign`, `Benign vs Malignant`, `Normal vs Malignant`) separately on low-energy `DM` vs contrast-subtracted `CESM` images of `CDD-CESM`, evaluated with `SHAP` spatial explanations (`Sec. 2`).
- **Modality:** `CDD-CESM` lossless JPEG (`DM` `1,003` imgs + `CESM` `1,003` imgs).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **`CDD-CESM` (`TCIA 10.7937/29kw-ae92`, `Table 1`):** `326` female patients (`18–90` yrs), `2,006` total images (`DM`: `341` Normal / `331` Benign / `331` Malignant = `1,003`; `CESM`: `416` Normal / `256` Benign / `331` Malignant = `1,003`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance and Normal-case handling

- Separate pairwise binary formulations (`Normal vs Benign`, `Benign vs Malignant`, `Normal vs Malignant`) without pooling Normal into Benign silently.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Patient-level Split (`Table 3`):** `20%` independent patient-level held-out test split, with `3-fold` cross-validation strictly on the remaining training set.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- `ResNet-18`, `DenseNet-121`, `EfficientNet-B0` (ImageNet-pretrained; weighted `BCE` by inverse class frequency, `Adam` `lr = 1e-3`, `StepLR`, batch size `8`, `30` epochs).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Training-only augmentation (brightness/contrast, horizontal/vertical flips, rotation); `224 x 224` resize + ImageNet normalization; test/val unaugmented (`Sec. 2.2`).

## 8. XAI method(s) and quantitative evaluation

- **SHAP (`GradientExplainer`):** Computed on `EfficientNet-B0` test predictions using training split as background (`Sec. 2.5`).
- **Evaluation Gap:** Strictly **qualitative** visual inspection (`Fig. 4`) — does **not** leverage `CDD-CESM`'s `Radiology_hand_drawn_segmentations_v2.csv` masks for quantitative `Pointing Game / IoU / ROAD / Adebayo` audits!

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics

- Reports `Accuracy`, `Precision`, `Recall`, `F1`, and `AUC-ROC` (`Tables 4–6`): `EfficientNet-B0` leads (`Normal vs Malignant` test AUC `97%` on `DM` and `93%` on `CESM`; `DenseNet-121` DM AUCs: `44.88%` Normal vs Benign, `65.50%` Benign vs Malignant, `75.46%` Normal vs Malignant; CESM: `55.90%`, `78.55%`, `93.87%`). Zero calibration (`ECE/Brier`) or DeLong/McNemar CIs reported.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Built on public `CDD-CESM` (`TCIA 10.7937/29kw-ae92`); code URL not reported in main summary.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- Uses `224 x 224` resolution (risks losing microcalcification detail), `Normal vs Benign` AUC weak (`44.88%–62.05%`), qualitative `SHAP` only despite available radiologist segmentations (`Table 6` confirms gap vs quantitative XAI & calibration).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` / `B4_CTX` (Recent Peer-Reviewed XAI Study on `CDD-CESM`):** Directly illustrates `CDD-CESM` usage on `DM` vs `CESM` while reinforcing `GAP0003` (qualitative-only XAI & absent calibration).

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- Exact DeLong `p`-values, `95%` confidence intervals, and quantitative mask-overlap scores for SHAP maps.
