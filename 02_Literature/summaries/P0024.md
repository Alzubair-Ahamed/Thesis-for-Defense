## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Shubhi Sharma, Yeshwant Singh, and Tanupriya Choudhury.
- **Title:** *Advanced deep learning architectures for enhanced mammography classification: a comparative study of CNNs and ViT*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Discover Artificial Intelligence*, **2025**, Vol. `5`, Article `187`, pp. `1–21`.
- **DOI:** `10.1007/s44163-025-00426-2`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q3` — 4-Dataset 15-Architecture Benchmark With Proclaimed ECE, DeLong, and Grad-CAM).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Benchmark 15 CNN, ViT, and hybrid architectures across 4 mammography datasets with efficiency, calibration, external transfer, and Grad-CAM comparisons (`pp. 1–8`).
- **Modality:** **Cropped ROI patches** (`Sec. 3.1`, p. 8) extracted using dataset lesion bounding boxes and resized to `224 x 224` (mix of SFM `CBIS-DDSM`/`MIAS` and FFDM `INbreast`/`VinDr-Mammo`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Datasets Listed (`Table 1`, p. 8):**
  - `CBIS-DDSM` (`3,102` images), `INbreast` (`410` images), `MIAS` (`322` images — **erroneously described as `"CC only"` in Table 1** when MIAS is exclusively MLO!), `VinDr-Mammo` (`5,000` exams / `20,000` images).
  - *Critical reporting gap:* The text never identifies unambiguously which single dataset or pooled dataset backs the main comparison table (`Table 3`, p. 12) vs `Table 5`.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Mixed provenance across datasets (`CBIS-DDSM` biopsy-verified; `VinDr-Mammo`/`INbreast` radiologist BI-RADS + lesion findings). Specific class/normal counts used inside the unified evaluation split are `not reported`.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split (`Sec. 3.2`, p. 9):** `70%` train / `15%` validation / `15%` test plus `5-fold CV` on train.
- **Leakage Risk (`UNKNOWN`):** Fails to declare whether splitting was enforced at **patient level or image/ROI level** across `CBIS-DDSM`, `INbreast`, `MIAS`, and `VinDr-Mammo` (`high leakage risk` if bilateral/multi-view ROIs were split randomly).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **15 Architectures Benchmarked (`Tables 2–3`, pp. 10, 12):** `ResNet50`, `DenseNet121`, `EfficientNetB0`, `ViT`, `ConvNeXt`, `CoAtNet`, `FCCS-Net`, `TransM`, `GLAM-Net`, `GMIC`, `MetaNet`, `MammoNet`, `AG-CNN`, `DL-Mammo-TL`, `ViT-Mammo` (`Adam`, `lr = 1e-4`, batch size `32`, `50` epochs, BCE loss).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Bounding-box ROI cropping (`Sec. 3.1`, p. 8) -> adaptive histogram equalization -> intensity scaling `[0, 1]` -> resize `224 x 224`.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Methods (`Sec. 4.7.1`, `Figs. 4–5`, pp. 15–16):** `Grad-CAM` overlays on `ResNet50`, `DenseNet121`, `ViT-Mammo`, `FCCS-Net`, `GLAM-Net`.
- **Evaluation:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`zero` PG/Dice/IoU/faithfulness/Adebayo numbers; only narrative claims that hybrids look sharper).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Reported Main Results (`Table 3`, p. 12):**
  - `FCCS-Net`: `AUC 0.965, Acc 92.0%, F1 0.923, Spec 0.912`
  - `GLAM-Net`: `AUC 0.963, Acc 91.8%`
  - `ViT-Mammo`: `AUC 0.961`
  - `ResNet50`: `AUC 0.935`
- **DeLong Significance (`Sec. 4.3`, p. 13):** `FCCS-Net` beats `ResNet50`, `DenseNet121`, `EfficientNetB0` (`p < 0.01`), while `FCCS-Net` vs `ViT-Mammo` and `GLAM-Net` are non-significant (`p > 0.05`).
- **Proclaimed Narrative-Only `ECE` & External Transfer (`Sec. 4.5, 4.7.4, 4.7.5`, pp. 13, 15–16):**
  - `ECE`: un-tabulated prose claims ViT/CoAtNet `7–9%` vs DenseNet121/EfficientNetB0 `3–5%`, improving with temperature scaling (`GLAM-Net 2.8%`).
  - External transfer: un-tabulated prose claims up to `4.2%` AUC drop `CBIS -> VinDr` (`Sec. 4.5`), whereas `Sec. 4.7.5` claims `>5%` drop on `INbreast/MIAS` for `ResNet50`/`ViT` and `<2%` for `ViT-Mammo`/`FCCS-Net`/`GLAM-Net`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Severe Internal Consistency & Integrity Anomalies Found During Audit:**
  1. **Internal Parameter & Latency Contradiction:** `Table 3` (p. 12) reports `GLAM-Net = 19.5M / 14.2 ms` and `FCCS-Net = 18.4M / 13.2 ms`, **directly contradicting `Table 4` (p. 17)** (`GLAM-Net = 61.7M / 38 ms GPU`; `FCCS-Net = 36.2M / 26 ms`)!
  2. **Ranking Reversal (`Fig. 3` vs `Table 3`):** `Fig. 3` (p. 14) plots `GLAM-Net AUC = 0.98 > FCCS-Net ~0.97`, reversing `Table 3` (`FCCS-Net 0.965 > GLAM-Net 0.963`).
  3. **Wrong Citations & Dataset Error:** `Table 1` labels `MIAS` as `"CC only"` (MIAS is MLO); `Table 2` cites an MRI paper (`Rasti 2017 [57]`) as `AG-CNN` and a bibliometric survey (`Lyu 2022 [59]`) as `ViT-Mammo`; Ref `[8]` contains raw XML `[CDATA[16 times 16]]`.
  4. **Un-tabulated claims:** Neither `ECE` nor cross-dataset external AUCs nor exact test cohort sizes are presented in proper result tables.
