## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Israa Abdelsabour, Ahmed Elgarayhi, Mohammed Sallah, and Mohammed Elmogy.
- **Title:** *Different BI-RADS breast cancer diagnosis using MobileNetV1 and vision transformer based on explainable artificial intelligence (XAI)*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2026**, Vol. `16`, Article `7190`, pp. `1–26` (Published 17 Feb 2026).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-026-37199-2`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2` — BI-RADS FFDM MobileNetV1+ViT Hybrid Classification & Grad-CAM Case Study).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Classify bilateral **2D full-field digital mammograms (FFDM)** into 4 diagnostic BI-RADS categories (`BI-RADS 1, 3, 4, 5`) using a fused `MobileNetV1` + `ViT` deep feature extractor paired with PCA and bagging logistic regression, plus post-hoc visual saliency (`pp. 1–5`).
- **Modality:** Whole-image 2D FFDM (`bilateral CC + MLO` DICOM converted and resized to `224 x 224` pixels).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **King Abdulaziz University Breast Cancer Mammogram Dataset (`KAUBC` / `KAUBCMD`, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia; `Table 2`, p. 6; pp. 9–10):**
  - `1,416` women aged `25–75` years (mean `48.3`), totaling `6,087` FFDM images across 6 raw BI-RADS categories (`BI-RADS 0: 440`, `1: 1,884`, `2: 3,250`, `3: 387`, `4: 102`, `5: 24`).
  - **Selected Study Subset:** Excludes BI-RADS 0 and 2, retaining **`2,397` raw images across `593` cases** (`BI-RADS 1: 1,884`, `BI-RADS 3: 387`, `BI-RADS 4: 102 [26 patients]`, `BI-RADS 5: 24 [6 patients]`).
  - **Critically Oversampled Evaluation Pool (`Fig. 6a`, p. 19):** The reported confusion matrix sums to **`9,943` evaluated images** (`BI-RADS 1: 1,613`, `BI-RADS 3: 1,780`, `BI-RADS 4: 2,613`, `BI-RADS 5: 3,937`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance (`p. 22`):** Clinical **BI-RADS lexicon score** only; lacks histopathologic biopsy confirmation for included cases (explicitly noted as a limitation). Normal-equivalent cases correspond to `BI-RADS 1` (`1,884` images).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Nominal Split Claim (`p. 10`):** Claims patient-level `80/20` train/test split and `5-fold` cross-validation.
- **Severe Leakage / Pseudo-Replication Flag (`pseudo_replication_oversampling_flag`):** Although raw `KAUBC` contains only `24` BI-RADS 5 images (`6` patients) and `102` BI-RADS 4 images (`26` patients), `Fig. 6a` evaluates `3,937` BI-RADS 5 and `2,613` BI-RADS 4 samples (`~164x` inflation on BI-RADS 5). Evaluating heavily oversampled/augmented twins inside test folds constitutes severe pseudo-replication and inflates statistical reliability.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Hybrid Dual-Stream Pipeline (`Figs. 1, 3, 4`, pp. 5–9; `Table 9`, p. 19):**
  - `MobileNetV1` (`3.2M` params, `SGD`, `lr = 1e-3`) + Vision Transformer `ViT` (`86M` params, `AdamW`, `lr = 1e-5`). *Internal spec mismatch:* `Table 3` (p. 11) specifies `ViT-B/16` while `Table 9` (p. 19) lists `patch 4x4, window 7x7`.
  - Feature concatenation -> **PCA (`95%` retained variance)** -> **Bagging Logistic Regression (`10` base estimators)**.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- DICOM windowing -> bilinear resize to **`224 x 224`** -> **CLAHE** contrast enhancement (`pp. 9–10`); heavy class-balancing augmentation/oversampling prior to evaluation (`Fig. 6a`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Methods (`pp. 18–21`, `Figs. 7–8`):** `Grad-CAM` and `Grad-CAM++`.
- **Evaluation:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** with informal single-radiologist visual inspection (`Prof. Khaled M. Abdel Wahab`, pp. 20, 25). **Zero** quantitative localization (`Pointing Game`, `IoU`, `Dice`), **zero** faithfulness (`deletion`/`insertion`), and **zero** `Adebayo` randomization sanity checks.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Headline vs Cross-Validated Results (`Tables 7–8, 10, 12`, pp. 17–20):**
  - Abstract / `Table 7` / `Table 10`: **`99.0%`** accuracy (best fold/seed), whereas 5-fold mean (`Table 8`, p. 18; `Table 12`, p. 20) is **`98.02 ± 0.18%`**.
  - Per-class AUC (`Table 12`, p. 20): `BI-RADS 1: 1.000`, `BI-RADS 3: 0.998`, `BI-RADS 4: 1.000`, `BI-RADS 5: 0.998`.
  - Statistical test: paired t-test vs SVM (`p = 0.6805` — non-significant!).
- **Calibration:** `not reported` (`zero` `ECE`, `Brier score`, or reliability diagrams).
- **External Validation:** `none` (`p. 23`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`pp. 22–23`):** BI-RADS-only labels without biopsy verification, single-center dataset, lack of external validation.
- **Reviewer critique:** Exemplifies multiple `Q2/Q3` methodological hazards: (1) catastrophic oversampling inflation (`24 -> 3,937` BI-RADS 5 test samples from only `6` patients); (2) abstract headline mismatch (`99.0%` vs `98.02%` mean); (3) conflicting `ViT-B/16` vs `patch 4x4, window 7x7` architecture specs; (4) complete absence of external validation, calibration (`ECE`), or quantitative XAI verification.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B2` (Methodologically Flagged Secondary Comparator):** Not usable as a clean baseline due to oversampled evaluation (`9,943` vs `2,397`), lack of biopsy ground truth, zero external test, and visual-only XAI; valuable empirical evidence for `CLAIM0002` (dataset inflation/leakage distortions) and `RF16` (`RG1`).
