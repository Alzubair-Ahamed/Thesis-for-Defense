- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Cancers* (MDPI), **2026**, Vol. `18`, `1305`, pp. `1–45` (Received 11 Mar 2026 / Accepted 14 Apr 2026 / Published 20 Apr 2026, p. 1).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Systematically review AI/DL diagnostic performance, architectural paradigms (CNNs, ViTs, GNNs, hybrids), clinical validation barriers (PROBAST), and XAI across breast imaging modalities (`Jan 2018–Dec 2024`, Sec. 1 & 5.1, pp. 2, 18).
- **Task & Modality (Secondary Review):** Multi-modal PRISMA systematic review (`65` peer-reviewed studies across Mammography `12`, Ultrasound `10`, MRI `11`, MBI `10`, PET/SPECT `10`, Histopathology `11`, plus cross-references; `Tables 5–10`, pp. 23–36).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Review Search Scope (`Sec. 5.1`, `Fig. 7`, pp. 18–19):**
  - Identified **`132` records** across `PubMed, IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Web of Science, arXiv` -> `14` duplicates, `8` automation ineligible, `4` other removed -> `106` screened -> `93` sought -> `82` full-text assessed -> excluded `17` (`3` retracted, `8` biased methodology, `6` wrong setting) -> **`65` included studies**. (Not PROSPERO-registered, p. 18).
  - Summarizes dataset table (`Table 2`, p. 12: `DDSM`, `CBIS-DDSM`, `INbreast`, `MIAS`, `NYU`, `BUSI`, `OASBUD`, `Duke-MRI`, `QIN-Breast`, `ABIDE`, `BreakHis`, `BACH`, `Camelyon16`, `TCGA-BRCA`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Not applicable (PRISMA systematic review aggregating heterogeneously labeled multi-modal studies).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Key Synthesis Finding (`Sec. 5.1–5.2`, pp. 18, 20):**
  - **`PROBAST` Quality Appraisal (`p. 18`):** **`41.5%` (`~27/65`) of reviewed studies rated HIGH risk of bias** due to inadequate external validation, retrospective single-center designs, and **lack of calibration reporting**.
  - **External Validation Rate (`pp. 10, 20`):** Only **`18.5%`** employed external multi-institutional validation, while **`67.7%`** relied exclusively on internal k-fold or single-center splits!
  - **Domain Shift Drop (`pp. 10, 20`):** Models trained on public benchmarks (`DDSM`, `BreakHis`) degrade by **`8.2%–14.6%`** on institution-specific or vendor-diverse cohorts.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Synthesizes CNNs (`ResNet`, `AlexNet`, `EfficientNet`, `U-Net`, `CapsuleNet`), ViTs (`BUViTNet`, `DecT`, multi-res ViT), GNNs, and CNN-Transformer hybrids (`Sec. 4.3–4.4`, `5.9`, pp. 14–15, 35–36).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Catalogs normalization, noise filtering, CLAHE/contrast enhancement, ROI cropping, and generative augmentation (`StyleGAN-XL`) (`Sec. 4.1–4.2, 5.3`, pp. 13–14, 20).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Synthesized XAI Limitations (`Sec. 4.6`, `Table 4`, p. 17):** Covers `LIME`, `SHAP`, `Grad-CAM`, `EigenCAM` (`Fig. 6`, p. 13). Explicitly flags that **`Grad-CAM` suffers from gradient saturation and coarse localization**, while **`LIME`/`SHAP` are sensitive to input perturbations and background correlations**, calling for clinically grounded BI-RADS-aligned XAI (`p. 17`). Does not pool quantitative XAI faithfulness or pointing-game metrics.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Aggregate Across `65` Included Studies (`Sec. 5.3`, p. 22):**
  - Overall median accuracy **`94.2%`** (IQR `91.0%–97.1%`), AUC-ROC `0.85–0.99`.
  - Mammography CNNs median accuracy **`96.5%`** *(reflecting unstratified heterogeneous/often leaky public benchmarks, e.g., Kavitha `98.5%` DDSM / `97.55%` Mini-MIAS, Alshammari `42`-case `100%`, Table 5 p. 23!)*.
  - Transformer histopathology WSIs median AUC **`0.97`**.
- **Calibration Gap (`pp. 18, 36`):** Explicitly notes widespread **omission of calibration metrics** (`Sec. 5.1`, p. 18; `Sec. 5.9`, p. 36: *"standardized validation frameworks that report calibration metrics, not merely discriminative performance"*).
