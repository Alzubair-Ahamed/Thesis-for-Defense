## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Jiayi Zhu, Fuxiang Huang, Qiong Luo, and Hao Chen.
- **Title:** *A Benchmark for Breast Cancer Screening and Diagnosis in Mammogram Visual Question Answering*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Nature Communications* (**Q1, 2024 JIF 18.1**), **2025**, Vol. `16`, Issue `1`, Article `11683`.
- **DOI / IDs:** `10.1038/s41467-025-66507-z` | `PMCID: PMC12748590` | `PMID: 41309622`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`Multi-Cohort Q1 Benchmark Integrating Both BMCD & CDD-CESM`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Evaluate whether `12` general & medical Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) plus domain-adapted baselines (`LLaVA-Mammo`, `ResNet-50`, `DINOv2`, `ViLT`) accurately solve mammogram interpretation (`BI-RADS`, density, pathology, laterality, view, abnormality) internally and externally (`Abstract`, `Results`).
- **Modality:** Multi-cohort mammography benchmark (`MammoVQA`), unifying `11` internal datasets + `4` external validation cohorts.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Internal Cohorts (`Table 1`, `131,847` image-level imgs / `420,923` QA pairs; `72,518` exams):** Explicitly includes **`BMCD`** (`400` images, Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.5036062`: Density, BI-RADS, Laterality) and **`CDD-CESM`** (`1,003` Low-Energy breast images, TCIA `10.7937/29kw-ae92`: Density, BI-RADS, Laterality, Pathology, View), alongside `CBIS-DDSM`, `DMID`, `INbreast`, `MIAS`, `CSAW-M`, `KAU-BCMD`, `VinDr-Mammo`, `RSNA`, `EMBED`.
- **External Cohorts (`Table 2`):** `DBT` (`270` finding crops), `LAMIS` (`2,212` imgs), `MM` (`745` imgs), `NLBS` (`23,706` imgs).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance and Normal-case handling

- Harmonizes heterogeneous cohort labels into closed-set taxonomies (`9` question topics incl. `pathology (breast/finding)`, `BI-RADS`, `density`, `abnormality`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- Uses proportional `7:1:2` train/val/internal-test split across `11` internal datasets plus `4` strictly held-out external datasets (`Methods`). Notes internal `7:1:2` stratification over images/datasets rather than full connected-component patient clustering across all `11` internal cohorts (`limitation for individual image datasets`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Benchmarks `12` existing LVLMs (`MiniGPT-4`, `BLIP-2`, `InstructBLIP`, `LLaVA-NeXT-Interleave`, `InternVL3-8B`, `Qwen2.5-VL-7B`, `LLaVA-Med`, `RadFM`, `Med-Flamingo`, `MedVInT-TD`, `MedDr-40B`, `MedGemma-4B`), `LoRA`-tuned `LLaVA-Mammo` (`r=8, alpha=8`), linear-probed vision backbones (`ResNet-50`, `DINOv2`), and `ViLT-Mammo`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Standardized QA prompt templating with option-order randomization (`Methods`) and finding-crop extraction paired with normal random crops.

## 8. XAI method(s) and quantitative evaluation

- Does not compute spatial pixel-level XAI attribution maps (`Grad-CAM`/`SHAP`) or Pointing Game / Deletion faithfulness metrics; focuses on closed-ended diagnostic QA reliability.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics

- Reports `Absolute Accuracy`, `Weighted Accuracy`, `Macro-F1`, and `95%` confidence intervals via `9,999` bootstrap iterations (`Fig. 3`).
- `12` pretrained LVLMs perform near random guessing on mammograms; `LLaVA-Mammo` achieves `73.89%` average internal absolute accuracy (`+38.07%` over MedGemma) / `50.32%` weighted accuracy (`+19.66%`) and `+19.87%` absolute / `+21.21%` weighted accuracy gain across `4` external cohorts (`DINOv2` outperforms `ResNet-50` & `ViLT`). No `ECE/Brier` calibration reported.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- MammoVQA benchmark & links documented in *Nature Communications* (`PMC12748590`, Harvard HAREC ethics `HREP-2025-0025`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Authors (`Discussion`):** Backbone scale capped at 7B, label imbalance across QA topics, closed-set VQA only, technical proficiency does not prove clinical diagnostic utility.
- **Critique:** Demonstrates both **`BMCD`** and **`CDD-CESM` (Low-Energy `1,003` imgs)** are actively standardized in top-tier (`Nature Communications`, `IF 18.1`) Q1 benchmarks; however, `MammoVQA` lacks spatial XAI localization (`Pointing Game / ROAD`), calibration (`ECE`), and mixed-provenance segregation (`pools BI-RADS triage & biopsy cohorts`).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B2` / `B4_CTX` (Flagship Q1 Multi-Dataset Evidence):** Validates active 2025 Q1 adoption of both **`BMCD`** (`400` imgs) and **`CDD-CESM`** (`1,003` LE imgs) while highlighting why our patient-grouped CNN + calibration + quantitative XAI benchmark remains distinct and needed.
