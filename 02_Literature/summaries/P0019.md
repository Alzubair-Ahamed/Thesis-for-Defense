## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Synthesize technical, data-centric, cognitive, and regulatory barriers to trustworthy XAI in oncology imaging across 5 research questions (`RQ1–RQ5`, 2023–2025 focus, pp. 3–4): barriers to trust, clinician trust measurement, intrinsic vs post-hoc interpretability architectures, clinical–epistemic alignment, and uncertainty calibration.
- **Task & Modality (Secondary Review):** Cross-cancer imaging review (`Table 1`, pp. 11–14; `Tables 4–5`, pp. 29–34) covering MRI, CT, WSI pathology, PET, ultrasound, dermatology, and selected mammography/DBT works (`Ahmed 2024 CBIS`, `Vara MG`, `RSNA`, `BCS-DBT`, `IAIA-BL`, `MASAI`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Key Synthesis Findings (`Sec. 3.2.4, 3.3, 6.3.6`, pp. 20–21, 54):**
  - Cites review of `86` radiology DL algorithms where **`81%` lost accuracy externally** and **`~25%` suffered `>=0.1 AUC` drop** (`Suleman 2025 [116]`, p. 20).
  - Defines a 3-tier evidence hierarchy (`p. 54`): `Tier 1 Proof-of-Concept` (retrospective single-center), `Tier 2 External Validation` (multi-center multi-vendor), and `Tier 3 Prospective Clinical Evidence` (`MASAI [187]`, `NELSON [188]`).
  - Notes `~95%` of oncology AI studies omit race-related metadata (`D'Amiano 2025 [128]`, p. 22).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Categorizes CNNs, ViTs, GNNs, hybrid CNN-Transformers, Concept Bottleneck Models (`CBMs`, concept-task leakage `CTL`), prototype models (`ProtoPNet`, `IAIA-BL`, `MProtoNet`), and causality-aware architectures (`CausalX-Net`, `Tables 3–5`, pp. 25–34).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Notes DICOM metadata encoding (`Protocol Genome`), batch-normalization domain adaptation (`DoSReMC`), and harmonized region extraction (`Sec. 3.2.4–3.3`, pp. 20–21).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Key Empirical Citations & Taxonomy (`Sec. 3.4, 4, 5.4.2, 5.7`, pp. 22–34, 42–49):**
  - Cites meta-analysis (`Singh 2025 [9]`, pp. 23, 28, 36, 45) showing explanation methods fail to faithfully represent model reasoning in **`55%–68%` of cases** across `67` studies, and explanation stability drops by up to **`53%`** with `10%` input noise (`p. 23, 36`).
  - Distinguishes **plausibility vs faithfulness (`Table 7`, p. 54)**: `"Visually Persuasive"` (`Grad-CAM`, saliency -> `"illusion of interpretability"`, risk of shortcut learning) vs `"Trust Calibrating"` (`CBMs`, uncertainty maps -> faithfulness/fidelity metrics via deletion/insertion).
  - Highlights **`cognitive load exploitation`**: AI heatmaps can **increase reading time by `33%` and delay dictation by `>40%`** due to `authority modulation` (`Cheung 2023 [171]`, pp. 37, 48).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators
