## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Core `CO-12` Empirical Audit (`Table 1`, pp. 24–25; `Fig. 3`, p. 21; `n=35` studies):**
  - **Heavily Over-Represented:** **`Coherence` (plausibility / localization overlap)** (`~33` papers: `15+` named + `18` other, e.g., `Cerekci 2024`, `Pertuz 2023`, `Barnett 2021`) and **`Context`** (`13` papers, e.g., `Jungmann 2023`, `Lotter 2021`).
  - **Severely Neglected (`Table 1`, pp. 24–25):**
    - **`Correctness` (faithfulness to true reasoning):** only **`8` papers** (`Barnett 2021, Chen 2025, Donnelly 2024, Klanecek 2024, Major 2023, Rossi 2024, Shen 2017, Sugawara 2025`).
    - **`Consistency`:** **`3` papers** (`Ahmed 2024, Balve 2024, Klanecek 2024`).
    - **`Continuity`:** **`4` papers**; **`Contrastivity`:** **`3` papers** (`Major 2023, Rossi 2024, Tardy 2021`).
    - **`Completeness`:** **`2` papers** (`Donnelly 2024, Tardy 2021`); **`Covariate complexity`:** **`2` papers**.
    - **`Confidence` (uncertainty as explanation):** only **`3` papers** (`Dembrower 2025, Jungmann 2023, Oh 2022`).
    - **`Controllability`:** **`0` papers found** (`"No paper found"`, Table 1 p. 25!).
  - **Major Conceptual Warnings (`Sec. 1.3, 4.1, 7.2.1`, pp. 5, 12, 20):**
    - **"Plausible is NOT faithful"**: cites `Wilming 2023` & `Haufe 2024` proving saliency maps highlight `"suppressor variables"` with zero causal link (`"actively dangerous"`, p. 5, 20); cites **`Adebayo 2018` parameter randomization** as a mandatory sanity check (`p. 12`) and formal `Infidelity`/`Sensitivity-n` faithfulness metrics (`p. 12`); warns of the **`fidelity paradox`** when valid subtle context makes a faithful map score poorly on lesion localization (`p. 20`).
    - **Human-factors risks (`Sec. 5.1.2`, p. 15):** Over-reliance / `automation bias` (`Kunar 2025`: accuracy details impaired search) vs `algorithm aversion` (`Dembrower 2025` ScreenTrustCAD).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`Sec. 1.4, 5.3, 7.2`, pp. 5–6, 16, 20):** Single database (`PubMed` + `4` manual/arXiv additions), `2020–Jul 2025` window (predates late-2025/2026 landmarks like `Jaheen 2026 P0810`, `Kag 2026 P0016`, `Kumar 2026 P0015`), non-standardized reader studies.
- **Reviewer critique:** Best conceptual anchor in the entire corpus for mammography XAI (`RG1`, `RG2`, `RG4`): documents the **`CO-12` evaluation asymmetry** (33+ `Coherence` vs 8 `Correctness`, 3 `Confidence`, 0 `Controllability`), formalizes `Adebayo` randomization + perturbation faithfulness + uncertainty/calibration (`Confidence`) + `OOD` awareness (`Tier 2` shift). Must be paired with empirical mammography studies because `P0022` itself runs zero experiments.
