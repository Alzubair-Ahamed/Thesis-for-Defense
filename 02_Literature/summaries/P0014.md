## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Said Pertuz, David Ortega, Érika Suarez, William Cancino, Gerson Africano, Irina Rinta-Kiikka, Otso Arponen, Sara Paris, and Alfonso Lozano.
- **Title:** *Saliency of breast lesions in breast cancer detection using artificial intelligence*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2023**, Vol. `13`, Article `20545`, pp. `1–9`.
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-023-46921-3`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Independently evaluate out-of-the-box published mammography AI CAD models (`End2End`, `DMV-CNN`, `GMIC`, `GLAM`) on an external FFDM screening cohort for both malignancy classification AUC and pixel-level saliency localization overlap (`DSC`) against expert radiologist lesion contours (Abstract & Sec. Intro, p. 1).
- **Task & Modality:** Whole-image **2D FFDM** (`Philips MicroDose SI` `22%` + `GE Senographe Essential` `78%`, `100 um/px`, `16-bit`; Sec. Methods, p. 2) per-image malignancy prediction & lesion saliency localization.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Tampere University Hospital Screening Cohort (Finland, 2015–2017; Sec. Methods, p. 2):**
  - **`382` women:** `191` asymptomatic screen-detected biopsy-proven unilateral cancers + `191` matched controls (`1,582` images total; `370` manual lesion contour segmentations across CC + MLO views of affected breasts; `12` views omitted as lesion visible on single view only).
  - *(Verified Table Discrepancy: `Table 1`, p. 2 prints `N=143` Cases / `N=143` Controls rather than `191`/`191`, attributable to clinical-characteristic missingness subset or table printing typo.)*

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Biopsy-proven asymptomatic screen-detected unilateral breast cancer for cases (`p. 2`); matched screening controls (`p. 2`). Ground-truth lesion segmentations drawn manually in `ImageJ` by an experienced breast radiologist (`>20 yrs`) + radiology resident on `370` malignant-breast views.
- **Normal-case Handling:** Matched screening normal controls (`191` women).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`100% independent external_test_cohort`** — zero retraining or fine-tuning on Tampere data; all `4` public pretrained models evaluated out-of-the-box (`Tables 2–3`, pp. 3, 5).
- **Leakage Assessment:** Zero overlap between original training cohorts (`DDSM/INbreast` or NYU private cohort) and the independent Finnish FFDM evaluation set.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **4 Public Published Deep Learning Mammography Models (`Tables 2–3`, pp. 3, 5):**
  1. **`End2End`** (`Shen et al., 2019` — original reported AUC `0.950`)
  2. **`DMV-CNN`** (`Wu et al., 2020` — original reported AUC `0.886`)
  3. **`GMIC`** (`Shen et al., 2021` — original reported AUC `0.930`)
  4. **`GLAM`** (`Liu et al., 2021` — original reported AUC `0.882`)

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing:** Raw `16-bit`, `100 um/px` DICOM FFDM converted/resized per each public model's native published preprocessing pipeline (`no retraining`, Sec. Methods, pp. 2–3).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method:** Per-image **Grad-CAM** (or native model saliency normalized `[0,1]`; `p. 3, Table 3 p. 5`).
- **Target Layer:** Exact target layer names: `not reported` (uses default/native published saliency extractors).
- **Quantitative Localization Metric (`Eq. 1`, p. 3; `Table 3`, p. 5; `Fig. 3`, p. 6):** **Upper-bound Dice Similarity Coefficient (`DSC`)** via iterative per-image saliency thresholding (`[0,1]`) to **maximize** overlap against radiologist contours across `370` segmented views (`median [IQR]`):
  - **`End2End`:** **DSC `0.042 (0.151)`** *(lowest overlap despite highest AUC!)*
  - **`GLAM`:** DSC `0.108 (0.520)`
  - **`GMIC`:** DSC `0.291 (0.481)`
  - **`DMV-CNN`:** **DSC `0.380 (0.612)`**
- **Faithfulness / Sanity Checks:** Zero Deletion/Insertion/ROAD faithfulness; zero Adebayo randomization checks.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **External Finnish FFDM Classification (`Table 3`, p. 5; `95%` bootstrap CIs):**
  - **`End2End`:** **AUC `0.688` (`[0.655–0.719]`)** vs orig `0.950` *(Abstract/Text prints `0.694`)*
  - **`GMIC`:** **AUC `0.571` (`[0.535–0.606]`)** vs orig `0.930`
  - **`DMV-CNN`:** **AUC `0.571` (`[0.536–0.607]`)** vs orig `0.886`
  - **`GLAM`:** **AUC `0.528` (`[0.494–0.561]`)** vs orig `0.882` *(not statistically different from chance)*
- **Key Domain-Shift & Accuracy-Explainability Finding (`pp. 5–7`):** External AUC collapsed by **`-0.256` to `-0.361`** across all models, AND the highest-AUC model (`End2End`) showed only **`4.2%`** median oracle-thresholded lesion overlap (`DSC 0.042`), proving saliency-accuracy dissociation under external domain shift.
- **Calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`), DeLong/McNemar:** `not reported`.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Evaluation scripts: `"available upon reasonable request"` (`p. 7`; evaluates 4 previously published public repos).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 7`):** Modest sample size (`382` women), vendor difference vs model training domains (GE/Philips vs NYU Hologic/Siemens or DDSM film), no model retraining/fine-tuning performed, calls explicitly for density-stratified XAI analyses.
- **Reviewer critique:** Landmark empirical proof of both severe cross-site AUC collapse (`-0.26 to -0.36`) and the **accuracy–saliency decoupling paradox** (`End2End` highest AUC `0.688` yet worst `DSC 0.042`, even with optimistic oracle per-image thresholding!). Omits calibration (`ECE/Brier`), DeLong pairwise p-values, perturbation faithfulness (`ROAD/Deletion`), and density-stratified saliency breakdowns.

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Landmark External FFDM Generalization & Saliency-AUC Decoupling Benchmark):** Core anchor justifying mandatory external validation and quantitative XAI evaluation beyond visual anecdotes (`RG1/RG3/RG4`).
