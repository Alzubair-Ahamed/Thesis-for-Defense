## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Marco Cantone, Claudio Marrocco, Francesco Tortorella, Alessandro Bria (p. 1).
- **Title:** *Convolutional Networks and Transformers for Mammography Classification: An Experimental Study* (p. 1).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Sensors*, **2023**, Vol. `23`, No. `3`, `1229`, pp. `1–17` (Published 20 Jan 2023, p. 1).
- **DOI:** `10.3390/s23031229` (p. 1).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q1b`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Systematically benchmark CNN vs Vision Transformer families for whole-mammogram binary classification, analyzing per-lesion-type sensitivity and 8 input resolutions (`256x128` to `2048x1024`) (Sec. 1, p. 3).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary **Normal vs Malignant** classification on **single-view whole 2D FFDM images** (`for-presentation` Hologic only; Sec. 2.1, pp. 4–5).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **OMI-DB / OPTIMAM Steering Subset (UK Screening, 2010–2020; Sec. 2.1, pp. 4–5):**
  - Subset `148,460` images (`6,000` women) filtered to Hologic `for-presentation` + annotated malignant/normal without clips/implants/artifacts -> **`15,696` FFDM images**: **`5,801` malignant** (`3,175` Mass, `2,563` Calcification, `593` Focal Asymmetry, `499` Architectural Distortion `MIXED`, Table 1 p. 5) and **`9,895` normal** (`1:1.7` imbalance).
  - **Test Set (20%, Table 2, p. 7):** `1,160` malignant images (`630` Mass, `531` Calc, `122` Asymmetry, `92` Distortion `MIXED`). Unique patient counts per split & normal test N: `not reported`. External cohorts: None (`not reported`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Positive = surgery, biopsy, or previously classified malignant (`p. 5`); Negative = Normal breasts (`9,895` images; Benign findings excluded, p. 4–5).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`image_level_80_20`** (randomly split `80:20` stratified by class without explicit patient-ID grouping; Sec. 2.4, p. 7). Two training runs per model with **best of 2 runs** reported (`p. 9`). Zero external validation (`not reported`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`33` Architectures (ImageNet-1K TL, Torchvision / official SwinV2 / `timm`, all layers fine-tuned; pp. 7–9):**
  - `19` CNNs: ResNet (`18,34,50,101`), DenseNet (`121,161,169,201`), EfficientNet (`B0–B7`), ConvNeXt (`T,S,B`).
  - `14` Transformers: ViT (`T/16,S/16,B/16`), DeiT (`Ti,S` distilled from EfficientNet-B3), SwinV2 (`T/S/B` with `W8/W16`), NesT (`T,S,B` at `736x736` square).
- **Training (Sec. 2.4, pp. 8–9):** Input `1024x512` (Phase 1); `256x128`..`2048x1024` (`2:1` aspect ratio, Phase 2). SGD (`momentum=0.9`), `lr=1e-3`, Cosine Annealing warm restart at ep `15` / total `30` ep, BCE, `batch=4` (`2` or `1` at largest SwinV2), NVIDIA A100 (`80 GB`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (Sec. 2.2, p. 5):** DICOM -> **16-bit PNG** -> threshold breast-air crop (visually verified/corrected) -> resize keeping `2:1` -> linear normalization `[0,1]` -> right views horizontally flipped to common left orientation -> 3x channel grayscale stack. CLAHE & training augmentation: `not reported` (not applied).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Evaluated:** **None** (`not reported` / not performed; conceptual review of Grad-CAM, Attention Rollout, GradCAT in Sec. 4.4 pp. 14–15; explicitly acknowledged absent experimentally in Sec. 4.5 limitation `(v)`, p. 15).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Phase 1 (`1024x512`, Table 3, p. 10):**
  - **Best CNN — `EfficientNet-B3`:** Acc **`85.2%`**, MCC **`67.7%`**, **AUC `90.2%`**, Sens `74.5%`, Spec `91.5%` (`7 min/ep`).
  - `EfficientNet-B0`: `84.2%` Acc, `89.5%` AUC; `ResNet50`: `83.7%` / `88.8%`; `ResNet101`: `84.3%` / `88.3%`; `DenseNet-121`: `82.7%` / `88.3%`; `ConvNeXt-B`: `83.1%` / `89.4%` (ConvNeXt-T/S collapsed to `63.0% / 0.0% MCC`).
  - **Best Transformer — `SwinV2-B-W8`:** Acc `83.4%`, MCC `63.8%`, **AUC `88.7%`**, Sens `65.2%`, Spec `94.1%` (`22 GB` vs `6 GB` for EfficientNet-B0, p. 14). Vanilla `ViT-S/16` `78.9%` AUC; `DeiT-S` `78.2%` AUC; `NesT-S` `80.0%` AUC.
- **Phase 2 Resolution Sweep (`Table 4, p. 12`; `Fig. 6, p. 13`):**
  - `EfficientNet-B0`: `256x128` AUC **`76.3%`** (`MCC 41.4%`) -> `512x256` `80.6%` -> `1024x512` `89.6%` -> `1280x640` `91.0%` -> `1536x768` **`86.3%` Acc / `70.2%` MCC / `91.2%` AUC** -> `2048x1024` **`92.1%` AUC**.
  - Calcification sensitivity lags Masses dramatically at low resolution (`256x128` MCC gap `0.305` shrinking to `0.012` at `1536x768`, p. 11) — **authors recommend `> 1200x600` (`p. 14`)**.
- **Calibration (ECE/Brier), 95% CIs, Statistical Tests, Confusion Matrices:** `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (Sec. 4.5, p. 15):** Best-of-2 runs insufficient for statistical significance tests; binary Normal vs Malignant only; no HPO; architectures uncustomized; **zero experimental XAI**.
- **Reviewer critique:** Crucial architectural & resolution benchmark (`33` backbones + `8` resolutions establishing why `224x224` destroys calcifications and EfficientNet competes cleanly at high res); weakened by image-level `80:20` splitting without patient ID grouping, Normal-vs-Malignant rather than Benign-vs-Malignant framing, best-of-2 cherry-picking, single-vendor Hologic OMI-DB restriction, and absent XAI/calibration/CIs/stats.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B2` (Direct Whole-FFDM Multi-Architecture & Resolution Comparator):** Foundational methodological benchmark informing input-resolution (`> 1200x600` vs `224x224`) and CNN-vs-ViT design choices (`EfficientNet-B3` `90.2%` AUC), though separated from Benign-vs-Malignant patient-grouped XAI tables because it classifies Malignant vs Normal using image-level `80/20` splits without XAI.
