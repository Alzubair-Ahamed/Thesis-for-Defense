## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Filippo Pesapane, Chiara Trentin, Federica Ferrari, Giulia Signorelli, Priyan Tantrige, Marta Montesano, Crispino Cicala, Roberto Virgoli, Silvia D'Acquisto, Luca Nicosia, Daniela Origgi, and Enrico Cassano.
- **Title:** *Deep learning performance for detection and classification of microcalcifications on mammography*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *European Radiology Experimental*, **2023**, Vol. `7`, Article `69`, pp. `1–13` (Published 7 Nov 2023).
- **DOI:** `10.1186/s41747-023-00384-3`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Biopsy-Confirmed Patient-Split Microcalcification Patch Cohort Stratified by ACR Breast Density).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Detect/localize suspicious breast microcalcifications (`Task 1`) and classify them as histologically benign vs malignant (`Task 2`) across heterogeneous ACR breast densities on diagnostic 2D FFDM (`pp. 1–4`).
- **Modality:** **Patch-based `112 x 112` ROI extraction** (with whole-mammogram sliding-window reconstruction for Task 1 heatmaps) from 12-bit 2D FFDM (`1,912x2,294` to `4,700x5,800` px).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **IEO Milan Referral Center (`p. 3, 6`, `Table 1` p. 8):**
  - `1,986` diagnostic FFDM images from **`1,000` biopsied patients** (`mean 45 ± 10` yrs, range `21–73`; ACR density `A = 180 [18%]`, `B = 220 [22%]`, `C = 380 [38%]`, `D = 220 [22%]`; `60%` dense `C+D`).
  - Biopsy distribution (`Table 1`): **`389` malignant** (`132` IDC, `111` ILC, `120` DCIS, `17` inflammatory, `9` TNBC) and **`611` benign** (`310` fibrocystic, `92` hyperplasia, `88` stroma, `59` sclerosing adenosis, etc.).
  - **Extracted `112 x 112` Patches (`pp. 4–5`):** `24,237` microcalc patches (`12,637` benign, `11,600` malignant) + `9,648` non-microcalc background patches (`Total 33,885`; note Task 1 splits text sums `23,730 + 6,789 + 3,393 = 33,912`, minor 27-patch gap, and Task 2 test split prints `"24,26"` typo for `2,426`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Gold-Standard Biopsy Ground Truth (`p. 3`):** Every patient underwent vacuum-assisted biopsy or surgical histology `<1` month after diagnostic mammography, paired with 3-expert radiologist bounding localization (`X-RAIS` tool). Background patches sampled from tissue outside annotated ROIs.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Strict Patient-Level Grouping (`pp. 5–6`):** `70%` train / `20%` validation / `10%` test explicitly grouped so patches from the same image or dual views (`CC/MLO`) of the same patient **never split across train/val/test** (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN` internally). **Zero external validation** (`Limitation p. 10`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`AlexNet`, `ResNet18`, `ResNet34` trained from scratch (`"trained from the ground up"`, `p. 6`)** with binary CE loss, `Adam`, `lr = 1e-4`, `bs = 16`, early stopping. Notably, **`AlexNet` outperformed `ResNet18` and `ResNet34`** (`Task 2` `0.94` vs `0.88/0.92` AUC), reflecting small scratch training dynamics.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Blur, normalization, resize (`112 x 112` patch crops); 12-bit DICOM anonymization (`pp. 4, 6`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Sliding-Window Patch Probability Grid (`p. 6`, `Fig. 3` p. 7):** Partitions whole mammogram into non-overlapping `112x112` grid cells and plots patch malignancy probability heatmaps alongside radiologist annotations (`Fig. 3`) and false-positive/negative error galleries (`Figs. 4–5`, p. 9).
- **Evaluation:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`zero` quantitative localization IoU/PG/Dice or perturbation faithfulness or `Adebayo` checks).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Task 1 (Microcalc Detection vs Tissue, `Table 2`, p. 8):** `AlexNet`: **`AUC 0.98`**, `Acc 0.95 (0.94–0.96)`, `Sens 0.98 (0.98–0.98)`, `Spec 0.89` (*prints typo `"90.88–0.90"` in Table 2!*). `ResNet18`: `AUC 0.98`, `Spec 0.91`. `ResNet34`: `AUC 0.98`.
- **Task 2 (Benign vs Malignant Microcalc, `Table 2`, p. 8):** **`AlexNet`: `AUC 0.94`, `Acc 0.87 (0.86–0.88)`, `Sens 0.85`, `Spec 0.89`**; `ResNet34`: `AUC 0.92, Acc 0.84`; `ResNet18`: `AUC 0.88, Acc 0.80`.
- **Statistical & Subgroup Reporting Gaps:** Confidence intervals use symmetric normal Wald formula at patch level `n` without patient-cluster adjustment (`p. 6`), **no DeLong test**, **no density-stratified `A–D` performance breakdown** despite reporting ACR density demographics (`p. 6`), and **zero probability calibration (`ECE`/`Brier score`)**.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- `not reported` (proprietary `Laife Reply X-RAIS` workflow; IEO imaging restricted, `p. 12`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`pp. 10–11`):** Single-center retrospective cohort, excluded low-resolution exams, restricted solely to microcalcifications (no masses, distortions, or bilateral/prior comparisons), no DBT, no external validation.
- **Reviewer critique:** Positive methodological model for **patient-level multi-patch grouping** (`pp. 5–6`), gold-standard biopsy provenance (`Table 1`), and explicit ACR density demographics (`60%` dense `C+D`). However, undermined as a Q1 classifier benchmark by scratch-trained legacy CNNs (`AlexNet`), `112x112` patch isolation, patch-level Wald CIs (ignoring within-patient patch correlation!), typos (`"90.88–0.90"`, `"24,26"`), zero density-stratified AUCs, zero calibration, zero external test, and visual-only probability grid heatmaps.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (Patient-Disjoint Patch Grouping & Histological Microcalcification Cohort Reference):** Reinforces patient-split integrity (`pp. 5–6`) and highlights microcalcification sub-task hardness (`AUC 0.98 detection -> 0.80–0.94 classification`), while patch-only setup and missing external/XAI/ECE validation exclude it from primary baselines.

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- ACR density `A–D` stratified AUC/sensitivity; probability calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`); DeLong & patient-clustered CIs; quantitative localization (`IoU`/`PG`) of Fig. 3 grids vs radiologist contours; external validation; public code/weights.
