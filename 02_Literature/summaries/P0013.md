## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Ozden Camurdan, Toygar Tanyel, Esma Aktufan Cerekci, Deniz Alis, Emine Meltem, Nurper Denizoglu, Mustafa Ege Seker, Ilkay Oksuz, and Ercan Karaarslan.
- **Title:** *Annotation-efficient, patch-based, explainable deep learning using curriculum method for breast cancer detection in screening mammography*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Insights into Imaging*, **2025**, Vol. `16`, Article `60`, pp. `1–11`.
- **DOI:** `10.1186/s13244-025-01922-w`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Test whether patch-based curriculum learning (`128->256->512->1024`) with partial bounding-box supervision (`0%`, `20%`, `40%`, `100%`) improves both whole-mammogram cancer detection and Grad-CAM lesion localization overlap on internal and cross-vendor external FFDM cohorts (pp. 1–3).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Cancer vs Control classification on whole-image **2D FFDM** (`1024x1024` padded breast-cropped CC & MLO views, with ipsilateral CC+MLO score averaging at exam inference; pp. 3–6).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Internal Cohort (`3` private Turkish centers, GE Senographe Essential & Hologic Lorad/Selenia Dimensions, Jan 2020–Jan 2022; Sec. Methods, p. 3, p. 6):**
  - Screened `18,233` -> `106` excluded -> **`1,976` women** (median age `52`, IQR `9`, `1` exam/patient): **`988` pathology-proven cancer** + **`988` stratified undersampled BI-RADS 1–2 controls** (`>=1 yr` follow-up or benign pathology).
  - **Split:** `75%/25%` patient-level -> `1,482` development / **`494` held-out test women**.
- **External Cross-Vendor Cohort (`state hospital`, Siemens MAMMOMAT Revelation, Jan 2018–Jan 2020; p. 3):**
  - **`4,276` women** (`2,138` pathology-proven cancer + `2,138` BI-RADS 1–2 controls with `>=2 yr` follow-up or benign pathology).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Histopathologic biopsy proof for all malignant cases; `>=1 yr` (internal) or `>=2 yr` (external) negative follow-up or benign pathology for BI-RADS 1–2 controls (`p. 3`). Radiologist consensus lesion bounding boxes annotated for curriculum patch extraction and Grad-CAM Ground Truth Overlap Ratio scoring.
- **Normal-case Handling:** BI-RADS 1–2 normal/benign controls matched 1:1 via stratified undersampling.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`patient_level_internal_plus_external_cross_vendor`** (`1,482` dev / `494` held-out internal test, `1` exam/patient; tested across `4,276` completely independent Siemens external women, `p. 3, p. 6`).
- **Leakage Assessment:** Zero patient or site leakage across internal test and external vendor evaluation.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Architecture (`pp. 4–6`):** **`ResNeXt50_32x4d`** (PyTorch, NVIDIA L4 GPU), trained in **4 supervision regimes** (`0%` `Baseline`, `20%` `C20`, `40%` `C40`, `100%` `C100` bounding-box fraction), across **`10` independent random training seeds** per regime.
- **Initialization & Training:** Transfer-learning weight initialization source: `not reported`. Curriculum sequence: `3` patch stages (`128 -> 256 -> 512`, `100` epochs each) followed by **`15` epochs** whole-image **`1024x1024`** fine-tuning; Adam (`lr=1e-5`), Cross-Entropy; ipsilateral CC+MLO probability averaging at test time. Exact seed IDs: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (`pp. 3–5`):** Whole-breast bounding-box crop with radiologist QC, padded resize to `1024x1024`; curriculum patches cropped via a `400`-px cropper (`>=50%` overlap positive / `<10%` overlap negative). Intensity normalization / CLAHE details: `not reported`.
- **Augmentation:** Not reported in verified extraction beyond patch sampling/curriculum staging.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method:** **Grad-CAM** (`p. 6, Fig. 3 p. 8`).
- **Target Layer:** Exact target layer name `not reported`.
- **Quantitative Localization Metric (`Tables 1–2`, pp. 8–9):** **Grad-CAM Ground Truth Overlap Ratio** (`overlap pixels / GT bounding-box pixels` on `top-p` saliency pixels; exact `top-p` threshold `not reported`):
  - **Internal Test ($n=494$, `Table 1`, p. 8; mean ± SD across `10` seeds):** `Baseline` **`60.26 ± 1.91%`**, `C20` `62.13 ± 1.2%`, `C40` `62.26 ± 1.52%`, `C100` **`64.18 ± 1.37%`**.
  - **External Test ($n=4,276$, `Table 2`, p. 9; mean ± SD across `10` seeds):** `Baseline` **`57.2 ± 1.7%`**, `C20` `60.53 ± 0.94%`, `C40` `61.02 ± 1.3%`, `C100` **`62.88 ± 1.57%`**.
- **Faithfulness / Sanity Checks:** Zero perturbation faithfulness (`Deletion/Insertion/ROAD`), zero Adebayo randomization checks.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Internal Held-Out Test ($n=494$ women, `10` seeds, `Table 1`, p. 8):**
  - `Baseline (0%)`: F1 **`80.55 ± 0.88`**
  - `C20`: F1 `82.41 ± 0.47`
  - `C40`: F1 `83.03 ± 0.31`
  - `C100`: Recall `84.01 ± 0.53`, Precision `84.49 ± 0.39`, **F1 `83.95 ± 0.55`**
- **External Cross-Vendor Siemens Test ($n=4,276$ women, `10` seeds, `Table 2`, p. 9):**
  - `Baseline (0%)`: F1 **`74.65 ± 1.35`**
  - `C20`: F1 `77.7 ± 0.73` *(Abstract prints `77.77 ± 0.73`)*
  - `C40`: F1 `78.23 ± 1.78`
  - `C100`: Recall `78.87 ± 1.33`, Precision `79.71 ± 1.85`, **F1 `78.73 ± 1.25`**
- **Omitted Metrics (`Tables 1–2`, p. 10):** **AUROC, AUPRC, Specificity, Calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`), DeLong / McNemar tests**: `not reported`.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Code / weights: `"available upon reasonable request"` (`p. 10`; no public GitHub/OSF URL).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated:** Balanced case-control undersampling differs from clinical prevalence; requires at least `20%` lesion box supervision to capture curriculum benefit.
- **Reviewer critique:** Exceptionally strong methodology combining `10` random training seeds, `1024x1024` patient-split FFDM, large `n=4,276` cross-vendor external test (showing `-5.2` to `-5.9` F1 point domain drop), and quantitative `Grad-CAM` overlap (`57.2%–64.18%`); however, omits AUROC/AUPRC, calibration (`ECE/Brier`), DeLong/McNemar significance testing, exact `top-p` threshold, and perturbation faithfulness/sanity checks, and lacks public code.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Multi-Seed Internal + Large Cross-Vendor External FFDM Benchmark with Quantitative CAM Overlap):** Gold-standard evidence showing both cross-vendor external drop (`F1 83.95 -> 78.73`) and how curriculum patch supervision improves Grad-CAM overlap (`57.2% -> 62.88%`).
