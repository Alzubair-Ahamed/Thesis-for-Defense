## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Hitesh Kag, Madhu Shukla, and Dinesh Kumar.
- **Title:** *Attention Is Not Localization: A Leakage-Free, End-to-End Evaluation of Weakly-Supervised and Supervised Breast-Lesion Localization in Mammography*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Pages:** *IEEE Access*, **2026**, Vol. `14`, pp. `176532–176552` (Published 9 Nov 2026; current version 17 Nov 2026, p. 1).
- **DOI:** `10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3751717`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2` — flagship methodological benchmark).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Answer under a single strictly patient-disjoint protocol: (1) do attention/CAM maps of image-level classifiers localize lesions, or does resolution/encoder strength (`Mammo-CLIP` up to `1520x912`) rescue localization; and (2) what does supervised full-image detection vs end-to-end detect-then-delineate achieve vs conditional ROI crops (`pp. 1–2`)?
- **Task & Modality:** Whole-image **2D SFM (`CBIS-DDSM`, `Mini-MIAS`)** and **2D FFDM (`INbreast`, `VinDr-Mammo` DICOMs)**: (i) mass vs calcification / normal vs abnormal weakly-supervised localization (`224x224` to `1520x912`), and (ii) full-image lesion detection (`FROC`, `PG`) & segmentation (`Dice`, `IoU`) at `256x256` (`pp. 4–8`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **`CBIS-DDSM` (Patient-Disjoint Internal Train/Test, `Sec. III-A`, p. 4):**
  - Identified **`31` overlapping patients** shared between official mass and calcification train/test splits (`~8.5%` of test rows); removed from train -> strictly patient-disjoint **`2,408` training** (`2,796` lesions; `15%` patient-grouped val = `352` images, seed `2021`) and **`399` test mammograms** (`422` lesions: `361` mass images, `38` calc-only images, `Table 6`).
- **`Mini-MIAS` (`Sec. III-A`, p. 4):** `322` MLO films (`161` left/right woman pairs, `207` normal, `115` abnormal, `111` with center/radius circles), evaluated via **patient-grouped 5-fold CV** (+ image-wise control).
- **`INbreast` (100% External Validation, `Sec. III-A`, p. 4):** **`107` mass images (`116` polygon-annotated masses)** + **`67` BI-RADS 1 normal images** (point-only calcifications excluded).
- **`VinDr-Mammo` Original DICOMs via PhysioNet (100% External Validation, `Sec. III-A`, p. 4):** **`1,363` lesion images (`692` exams, `1,679` boxes: `1,226` mass + `543` suspicious calcification [`90` overlap])** + **`300` random BI-RADS 1 normal controls** across Siemens (`1,194`), Planmed (`249`), IMS Giotto (`220`). *(Author audited and rejected unregistered PNG distribution after revision!)*

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Biopsy-proven pathology + full-image SeriesInstanceUID-resolved ROI masks on CBIS-DDSM; OsiriX polygon contours on INbreast; radiologist bounding boxes on VinDr-Mammo DICOMs (`p. 4`).
- **Normal-case Handling:** Explicit FP/normal-mammogram auditing on **`67` INbreast BI-RADS 1 normals** (`Table 7`) and **`300` VinDr-Mammo BI-RADS 1 normals** (`Table 10`), exposing clinically unacceptable `1.15–1.69 FP/normal` at `0.5` operating threshold (`p. 12–13, 17`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`strict_patient_disjoint`** (`Algorithm 1`, p. 6; removes `31` leaking patients in official CBIS-DDSM split; pairs left/right MIAS films per woman `mdb2k-1/mdb2k`; augmentation strictly inside training partitions; external cohorts untouched except documenting VinDr-Mammo DICOM display-LUT correction `Sec. III-B/J`, pp. 4, 9).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Weakly-Supervised Classifiers (`Tables 1–5`, pp. 6–7, 10, 12):**
  - `EfficientNet-B0` (`224x224`) + `SE`, `CBAM`, `SE+CBAM`; `Attention-MIL` (`4x4` `896x896` tiles).
  - Frozen **`Mammo-CLIP` (`EfficientNet-B5`)** + ImageNet `EfficientNet-B5` control (`PCA 512`, `380x228`, `760x456`, `1520x912`, `3` seeds each) with `GAP`, `CBAM`, and `gated-attention MIL` heads.
  - Pretrained public NYU models: **`GMIC` (5 models)** & **`GLAM` (2 models)** (`2944x1920`, fixed PyTorch floor-division bug, `p. 7`).
- **Supervised Detectors/Segmenters (`Table 1`, p. 7, 10):**
  - `Attention U-Net` (`v1` scratch, `v2` frozen `MobileNetV2`, `v3` two-phase fine-tuned `MobileNetV2` × `3` seeds, Focal-Tversky + BCE, `256x256`).
  - `nnU-Net v2` (`2D`, `100` ep) and `SegFormer (MiT-B2)` (`60` ep).
  - `Pipeline-aware delineator` (`3` seeds, trained on detector error offsets/scales + lesion-free crops, `p. 8`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (`Sec. III-B`, p. 4):** Grayscale, **CLAHE** (`clip=2.0`, `8x8` tiles); `INbreast` 14-bit min–max to 8-bit; `VinDr-Mammo` **DICOM VOI LUT / window center-width + MONOCHROME1 inversion** (increasing intensity span from `49%` to `65%`, tested both with & without LUT, `p. 4, 17`). `GMIC/GLAM` & `Mammo-CLIP` used native author preprocessing (`0.5x` film resample + zero film background for CBIS).
- **Train-Only Augmentation (`Table 1`, p. 10):** H/V flips, `90°` rotations (strictly post-split).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Methods Evaluated (`Algorithm 2`, p. 6; `Table 2`, p. 10):** `Grad-CAM`, `Grad-CAM++`, `LayerCAM`, `Score-CAM`, `CBAM spatial map`, `MIL attention weights`, `GMIC/GLAM` saliency maps. Target layers specified cleanly (`CBAM output 7x7`, `block5c_add 14x14`, `p. 6`).
- **Quantitative Localization (`Tables 2–6, 10`, pp. 10, 12, 17):**
  - **Low-res (`224x224`) `EfficientNet-B0` on CBIS (`399` imgs):** Pointing Game = **`0.0%–11.5%`** (`Frozen Grad-CAM 0.5%`, `Fine-tuned Grad-CAM 2.8%`, `Grad-CAM++ 1.0%`, `LayerCAM 11.5%`, `Score-CAM 4.0%`, `MIL 5.3%`, `chance 0.6%`), IoU `0.006–0.061` -> ALL vastly worse than supervised `256x256` Attention U-Net `54.9%` (`McNemar p <= 1.3e-41`). `Mini-MIAS` patient-grouped PG = **`0.0%`** (`AUC 0.521`).
  - **Resolution & Instance-Attention (`Table 4`, p. 12):** **`Mammo-CLIP MIL` PG jumped `4.3 ± 0.8%` (`380x228`) -> `16.5 ± 1.5%` (`760x456`) -> `39.2 ± 3.9%` (`1520x912`, `IoU 0.169`)** (while CAM stayed `9.0 ± 1.6%`); ImageNet-B5 MIL localized equally (`42.6 ± 1.8%`) despite lower AUC (`0.729` vs `0.753`). Calcification PG lagged masses (`21.1%` vs `41.1%`).
  - **External `GMIC/GLAM` (`Tables 5 & 10`, pp. 12, 17):** `CBIS-DDSM`: failed transfer (`GMIC 9.5%`, `GLAM 8.5%`). `INbreast` (`107` mass): `GMIC 43.0%`, `GLAM 30.8%` vs detector `85.0%`. `VinDr-Mammo` (`1,363` lesion imgs): **`GLAM 59.1%` (`[56.1, 62.0]`) outperformed supervised `256x256` U-Net `53.8%` (`p=8.4e-4`) due to calcifications (`65.6%` vs `30.8%`)**, while `GMIC` hit `41.0%`.
- **Faithfulness & Sanity Checks:** Explicitly **NOT tested** (`"Throughout, we measure localization, not the faithfulness of a map as an explanation"`, pp. 2–3, 17–19).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Auxiliary Classification AUCs (`Sec. IV-H`, p. 16; `Tables 4–5`):** `Mini-MIAS` `0.521`; `CBIS` mass-vs-calc `0.556–0.615` (`B0`), `0.753 ± 0.015` (`Mammo-CLIP MIL`); `GMIC/GLAM` lesion-vs-normal `0.853/0.789` (`INbreast`) & `0.834/0.823` (`VinDr`).
- **End-to-End vs Conditional Delineation (`Tables 7–10`, pp. 13, 15, 17):** Conditional Dice **`0.871`** (`CBIS`) / **`0.797`** (`INbreast`) is inflated by oracle crops (centered geometric disk alone scores **`0.781` / `0.739`**!); true end-to-end Dice collapses to **`0.360`** (`CBIS`) and **`0.555`** (`INbreast`).
- **Statistical Rigor (`Sec. III-I`, p. 9):** Exact McNemar, `5,000`-patient-clustered bootstrap, Wilcoxon signed-rank, multi-seed SDs.
- **Calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`):** **`not reported`**.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- **Fully Open & Reproducible:** `https://github.com/hitesh712/attention-is-not-localization` & Zenodo archive `https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23571756` (released code, weights, patient-disjoint CBIS splits, per-image/per-lesion CSV outputs; p. 19).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`Sec. VI`, pp. 18–19):** Supervised detectors restricted to `256x256` due to 6 GB RTX 4050 VRAM; `Mammo-CLIP` kept frozen; `nnU-Net` stopped at 100 ep; **perturbation faithfulness (`ROAD/ROAR/Deletion`) not tested**; VinDr-Mammo LUT adjustment made after first pass.
- **Reviewer critique:** Gold-standard negative-result and methodological benchmark: exposes official `CBIS-DDSM` `31`-patient leakage, `Mini-MIAS` paired-film leakage, `VinDr-Mammo` PNG-vs-DICOM misregistration, `224x224` CAM localization failure (`0%–11.5%`), and oracle-crop segmentation inflation (`0.871 -> 0.360`). Leaves **`4` direct complementary frontiers** explicitly open: (1) **faithfulness & Adebayo sanity audits** (authors state `"localization is not faithfulness"`, p. 17), (2) **calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`)**, (3) **density-stratified XAI/calibration**, and (4) **whole-image high-res Vision Transformer malignancy classification + faithfulness across `VinDr`/`CMMD`/`DMID`/`CBIS`**.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` / `B3_METH` (Flagship Methodological & Primary Empirical Benchmark):** Essential baseline defining our leakage-free split standard (`31`-patient CBIS deduplication, DICOM LUT handling for VinDr-Mammo, `FP/normal`), proving why low-res (`224x224`) CAM claims fail (`<=11.5%` PG), and explicitly leaving faithfulness, calibration, and high-res ViT classification unaddressed.

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- Perturbation faithfulness (`Deletion/Insertion/ROAD`), Adebayo randomization sanity checks, calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`), BI-RADS breast density stratification, and high-resolution ViT end-to-end training.
