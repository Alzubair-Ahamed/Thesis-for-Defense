## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Daesung Kang, Hye Mi Gweon, Na Lae Eun, Ji Hyun Youk, Jeong-Ah Kim, and Eun Ju Son (p. 1).
- **Title:** *A convolutional deep learning model for improving mammographic breast-microcalcification diagnosis* (p. 1).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2021**, Vol. `11`, Article `23925`, pp. `1–7` (Published 14 Dec 2021, pp. 1, 6).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-021-03516-0` (p. 1).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q1b`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Evaluate whether fine-tuned ImageNet DCNNs can differentiate benign vs malignant suspicious microcalcifications to improve screening PPV above standard `<=30%` clinical PPV and reduce unnecessary biopsies (Abstract & Intro, p. 1).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Benign vs Malignant **microcalcification** classification (`microcalcifications associated with masses excluded`, pp. 3–4) on **2D FFDM** (Hologic Lorad Selenia, CC/MLO + spot-magnification views, p. 4), using **radiologist-cropped `512x512` ROI patches** (`p. 5–6`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Gangnam Severance Hospital Screening Cohort (Seoul, Korea, July 2007–Dec 2019; IRB `3-2018-0176`, pp. 1, 3–5):**
  - **Total:** `821` patients, `1,579` mammographic microcalcification ROIs (`589` malignant [`37.3%`], `990` benign; p. 2, p. 4).
  - **Train/Val Period (`2007–2017`):** `676` patients / `1,319` images -> randomly divided into **Train `1,121`** (`418` pos, `703` neg) + **Val `198`** (`74` pos, `124` neg) (pp. 4–5).
  - **Temporal Test Period (`2018–2019`):** `145` independent patients / **`260` images** (`97` pos, `163` neg; pp. 4–5). External cohorts: None (`not reported`, p. 3).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Histopathologic gold standard via **SVAB (stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy) or surgical excisional biopsy** (`DCIS` and invasive carcinoma = Malignant; all other results including high-risk lesions = Benign; p. 6). Normal (BI-RADS 1): excluded (biopsied suspicious microcalcifications only, pp. 3–4).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`temporal_and_patient_level`** between `2007–2017` train/val (`676` patients) and `2018–2019` held-out test (`145` patients) (`p. 4`). *(Caveat: internal `1,319 -> 1,121/198` train/val split says `"randomly chosen"` at image level, p. 5; augmentation strictly post-split on `1,121` train images only, p. 6).* External validation: None (single institution).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **5 ImageNet-Pretrained CNNs + Probability-Averaged Ensemble (pp. 2, 4–6):** **ResNet-101** (`batch=16`), **Xception** (`32`), **Inception-v3** (`32`), **Inception-ResNet-v2** (`16`), **DenseNet-201** (`8`) + 5-model `Ensemble`. MATLAB R2020a Deep Learning Toolbox, GTX 2080Ti, all conv+classifier layers fine-tuned, Adam (`lr=1e-4`; also `1e-5` in Supplement, p. 2, `L2 wd=0.0005`, patience `20`), best selected by validation AUC; Youden index cutoffs (`Table 1, p. 2`). Random seeds & input scaling dims: `not reported` (aside from `512x512` ROI crop, p. 6).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing:** Radiologist (>10 yrs exp.) crops **`512x512` square ROI** in MATLAB (pp. 5–6). Bit-depth, CLAHE, normalization, artifact removal: `not reported`.
- **Train-Only Augmentation (`1,121` images, p. 6):** Random H & V flips, rotations `1°–359°`, translations `±30 px`.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method:** **Grad-CAM** applied (visualized for `Inception-ResNet-v2`; Figs. 2–3, pp. 4–5).
- **Target Layer:** Generic `"each convolutional layer"` (p. 2); exact layer name `not reported`.
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`4` overlaid heatmaps: 1 TP + 1 FN in Fig. 2 p. 4; 1 TN + 1 FP in Fig. 3 p. 5). Zero Deletion/Insertion/ROAD faithfulness, zero Adebayo sanity checks, zero ROI-mask overlap, zero reader study.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Test Set ($n=260$ images / 145 patients, `lr=1e-4`, Table 1, p. 2):**
  - **Ensemble (`>0.247`):** **AUC `0.856` (`0.806–0.907`, 1000-bootstrap `Fig. 1b`)**, Sens `72.16%`, Spec `86.50%`, Acc `81.15%`, PPV `76.09%`, NPV `83.93%`.
  - **ResNet-101 (`>0.056`):** AUC `0.837` (`0.784–0.890`), Sens `64.95%`, **Spec `91.41%`**, **Acc `81.54%`**, **PPV `81.82%`**, NPV `81.42%`.
  - **Inception-ResNet-v2:** AUC `0.838`, Acc `78.85%`, Sens `75.26%`, Spec `80.98%`.
  - **DenseNet-201:** AUC `0.832`, **Sens `82.47%`**, Spec `69.33%`, Acc `74.23%`, **NPV `86.92%`**.
  - **Xception / Inception-v3:** AUC `0.817` (`80.00%` Acc) / AUC `0.792` (`77.69%` Acc).
- **Statistical Tests (Table 1 p. 2; p. 6; SAS 9.4):** **DeLong test** across AUCs (`p < 0.0001`), **GEE** comparing Sens (`p=0.0011`), Spec (`p < 0.0001`), PPV (`p < 0.0001`), Acc (`p=0.0870`), NPV (`p=0.1293`).
- **Calibration (ECE/Brier), PR-AUC, Confusion Matrices, McNemar:** `not reported`.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Code / weights repository: `not reported` (proprietary clinical data). License: CC BY 4.0 (p. 7).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (p. 3):** Small single-center population, modest sensitivities (`64.95%–82.47%`), biopsy selection bias.
- **Reviewer critique:** Rigorous histopathologic biopsy labels, temporal holdout, bootstrap CIs, DeLong, and GEE clustering inference; limited by manual radiologist ROI cropping (excluding masses/normal scans), single hospital cohort without external validation, absent calibration/PR-AUC, and qualitative-only Grad-CAM.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Biopsy-Confirmed FFDM Microcalcification ROI Benchmark):** Key FFDM multi-CNN + DeLong/GEE + Grad-CAM comparator (`AUC 0.856` ensemble; `0.837` ResNet-101), not directly pooled with whole-breast FFDM benchmarks due to radiologist-cropped `512x512` microcalcification ROIs.
