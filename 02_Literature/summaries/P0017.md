- **Authors:** Amirehsan Ghasemi, Soheil Hashtarkhani, David L. Schwartz, and Arash Shaban-Nejad.
- **Title:** *Explainable artificial intelligence in breast cancer detection and risk prediction: A systematic scoping review*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Cancer Innovation* (Wiley / Tsinghua), **2024**, Vol. `3`, `e136`, pp. `1–22` (Received 2 Feb 2024 / Accepted 30 Apr 2024, p. 1).
- **DOI:** `10.1002/cai2.136`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2` — Systematic Scoping Review).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Systematically scope peer-reviewed XAI applications in breast cancer detection, biomarker classification, and risk/survival prediction across all data modalities (`Jan 2017–Jul 2023`, Abstract & Sec. 3, pp. 1, 5–6).
- **Task & Modality (Secondary Review):** Multi-modal systematic scoping review covering `30` included studies across clinical/tabular (`SHAP 13/30`), genomics/microarray, ultrasound, histopathology (`BreakHis`, WSI), MRI, DBT, and only **`3` mammography X-ray studies** (`Huang 2020`, `Xi 2020`, `Gerbasi 2023` [labeled scanned-film though includes INbreast]; `Tables 6–7`, pp. 11–12).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Review Search Scope (`Fig. 4`, `Table 3`, pp. 5–6):**
  - Identified **`193` records** (`Scopus 104`, `IEEE Xplore 9`, `PubMed 30`, `Google Scholar first 50`) -> excluded `144` (reviews `16`, unrelated BC `18`, unrelated XAI `20`, preprints `7`, conference `34`, duplicates `37`, books/misc `12`) -> `49` full-text screened -> `19` irrelevant/no access -> **`30` included peer-reviewed journal articles**.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Key Synthesis Finding (`Sec. 6`, p. 15):** Explicitly concludes that **XAI methods across the `30` reviewed breast cancer studies only provided a qualitative `"sanity check"` (`visual inspection`), were never evaluated by oncologists/clinicians (`"not evaluated by oncologists"`), and lacked quantitative faithfulness/localization validation (`Sec. 6`, p. 15)**. Lists XAI taxonomies & code toolboxes in `Appendix A (Tables A1–A4, pp. 21–22)`.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Review paper (`data within paper`, p. 15); Appendix Tables `A2`/`A4` compile `16` open-source XAI library links (`shap`, `lime`, `grad-cam`, `zennit`, `alibi`, etc., pp. 21–22).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`Sec. 5.5`, p. 15):** Excluded non-English, paywalled, conference, preprint, and low-citation studies (`Jan 2017–Jul 2023` cutoff).
- **Reviewer critique:** Demonstrates how heavily pre-2024 "Breast Cancer XAI" literature was skewed toward **tabular/omics `XGBoost + SHAP`** (`13/30`) rather than 2D mammography (`only 3/30` studies used DDSM/MIAS/INbreast!), and corroborates lack of clinician evaluation (`Sec. 6`, p. 15); omits PROBAST/QUADAS-2 quality scoring, split-leakage tracking, and quantitative XAI metrics.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B4_CTX` (Context-Only Multimodal XAI Scoping Review — Non-Quantitative / Non-Mammogram-Dominated):** Useful PRISMA background documenting XAI method prevalence (`2017–2023`) and qualitative-only evaluation gaps; not a primary mammography benchmark.
