## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated:** Field lacks objective XAI evaluation metrics, clinician trust validation, and multi-center/BRCA-stratified datasets (pp. 5, 8–9). No self-limitations section for the SLR methodology.
- **Reviewer critique:** (1) PRISMA math fails (`646 - 132 - 501 = 13 != 79`; `132` vs `134` duplicates; `3%` vs `2.17%` Fig. 2); (2) misapplied Cochrane ROB-2 grading (`100% Low` across D1–D5; Fig. 3 swaps [29] for [43]); (3) stale Sep 2023 cutoff with zero standard 2D mammography Benign/Malignant CNN+Grad-CAM studies among the 5 mammography records, plus mislabeling US study [29] as `TI-RADS` and cost model [40] as 100k US images; (4) zero classification/AUC extraction.
