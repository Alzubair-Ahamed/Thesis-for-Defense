## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Esma Cerekci, Deniz Alis, Nurper Denizoglu, Ozden Camurdan, Mustafa Ege Seker, Caner Ozer, Muhammed Yusuf Hansu, Toygar Tanyel, Ilkay Oksuz, and Ercan Karaarslan.
- **Title:** *Quantitative evaluation of Saliency-Based Explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) methods in Deep Learning-Based mammogram analysis*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *European Journal of Radiology*, **2024**, Vol. `173`, `111356`, pp. `1–8`.
- **DOI:** `10.1016/j.ejrad.2024.111356`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Quantitatively evaluate whether post-hoc saliency-based XAI heatmaps (`Grad-CAM`, `Grad-CAM++`, `Eigen-CAM`) accurately localize biopsy-proven malignant breast lesions on 2D full-field digital mammograms (Sec. 1–2).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Cancer vs Control whole-breast **2D FFDM** classification using a **2-channel ipsilateral CC + MLO input** (`512x512` padded breast bounding-box crop; Sec. 2.1–2.3, pp. 2–3).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Multi-center Turkish Clinical Screening Cohort (3 university hospitals, Jan 2016–Jan 2020; Sec. 2.1–2.2, p. 2, p. 4):**
  - Screened pool: `28,324` women -> `124` excluded for prior breast surgery/chemotherapy -> **`2,992` women** included (median age `49`, IQR `8`, 1 exam per patient, p. 4).
  - **Cancer group:** **`1,496` women** with pathology-proven breast cancer (masses, calcifications, mixed lesions).
  - **Control group:** **`1,496` women** age- and BI-RADS-stratified undersampled controls (BI-RADS 1–2 with `>=1 yr` negative follow-up or benign pathology).
  - **Split counts:** `2,244` development (`75%`) / **`748` held-out test (`25%`)** (`p. 2, p. 4`). External cohorts: None (`p. 6`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Histopathologic biopsy proof for all `1,496` cancer cases; BI-RADS 1–2 with `>=1 yr` follow-up or benign biopsy for controls (Sec. 2.1, p. 2). Ground-truth lesion localization boxes (`1–2` foci/lesion on CC and MLO views) drawn by `3` board-certified breast radiologists (`>=5 yrs` experience) on 6MP EIZO monitors in `matrix.md.ai` consensus (inter-rater variability not assessed).
- **Normal-case Handling:** BI-RADS 1–2 normals/benigns retained in the balanced `1,496`-woman control arm via age/BI-RADS-stratified undersampling.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`patient_level`** (`75%` development `2,244` women / `25%` held-out test `748` women; `1` scan per patient, pp. 2, 4).
- **Leakage / External Validation Assessment:** Clean internal patient-level split, but **no external cross-site validation** (`p. 6`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Architecture (`Sec. 2.3`, pp. 2–3):** ImageNet-pretrained **ResNet-50** modified for **2-channel CC + MLO input** + linear-sigmoid FC head (PyTorch `1.7.1`).
- **Training:** Cross-Entropy loss, Adam (`lr=1e-4`), StepLR (`factor 0.5` every `20` epochs), `100` epochs, batch size `16`. Random seed: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing:** Automated patch-based breast bounding-box extraction followed by radiologist QC and **`512x512` padded resizing** (Sec. 2.1–2.2, p. 2). Bit-depth / normalization details: `not reported`.
- **Augmentation:** Random flip, rotate, shift, scale, and transpose during training (`Sec. 2.3`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Methods (`Sec. 2.4–2.6`, pp. 3–4):** **Grad-CAM**, **Grad-CAM++**, and **Eigen-CAM**, evaluated per view (`CC` and `MLO` individually).
- **Target Layer:** Exact target layer name `not reported`.
- **Quantitative Localization Evaluation:** **Pointing Game Score** (`tau = 15` pixel tolerance around radiologist consensus lesion bounding box, Fig. 2, p. 4) on cancer test cases & True Positives:
  - **Grad-CAM:** **`0.41`** (all cancer; `0.41` TP-only).
  - **Grad-CAM++:** **`0.30`** (all cancer; `0.31` TP-only).
  - **Eigen-CAM:** **`0.35`** (all cancer; `0.36` TP-only).
  - Kruskal-Wallis test showed no statistically significant localization difference among the three methods (`Wilcoxon p < 0.05` threshold used; exact p-values `not reported`). Visual heatmaps (`Figs. 4–5`) demonstrate frequent heatmap peaks outside actual lesions.
- **Faithfulness / Sanity / Reader Study:** Zero Deletion/Insertion/ROAD faithfulness, zero Adebayo sanity checks, zero radiologist utility study.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Held-Out Test Classification (`n=748` women, `Sec. 3`, p. 4):**
  - **Accuracy:** **`80%`** (`[0.735–0.865]`)
  - **Recall (Sensitivity):** **`69%`** (`[0.621–0.759]`)
  - **Precision:** **`88%`** (`[0.828–0.932]`)
  - **F1-score:** **`0.77`**
  - *(Verified Text Discrepancy: Discussion p. 5 erroneously writes `"88%"` accuracy instead of precision, contradicting Sec. 3 p. 4's `80%` accuracy / `88%` precision.)*
- **Statistical & Calibration Metrics:** Kruskal-Wallis across CAM scores (`exact p not reported`); **AUROC, AUPRC, Specificity, Calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`), DeLong/McNemar**: `not reported`.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Public code / trained weights repository URL: `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 6`):** Lack of external validation (`3` domestic Turkish centers only), single architecture (`ResNet-50`), undersampled balanced control design not matching screening prevalence.
- **Reviewer critique:** Landmark quantitative evidence that standard saliency CAMs miss malignant lesions **59%–70%** of the time on whole FFDM (`Pointing Game 0.30–0.41`), yet misses AUROC, specificity, calibration (`ECE/Brier`), perturbation faithfulness, and Adebayo randomization checks; `512x512` input downsampling likely degrades tiny microcalcification saliency; minor Discussion reporting inconsistency (`p. 5`).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Quantitative Saliency Localization Failure Benchmark on Whole FFDM):** Essential methodological anchor proving weak lesion localization (`PG 0.30–0.41`) in `ResNet-50` 2-view FFDM even under clean patient-level splitting.
