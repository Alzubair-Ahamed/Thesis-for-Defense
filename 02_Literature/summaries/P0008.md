- **Authors:** Wei-Chung Shia (p. 1).
- **Title:** *Deep learning-based classification of benign and malignant breast microcalcifications in mammography* (p. 1).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Compare EfficientNet (`B0–B4`) vs ResNet (`50, 101`) on pathology-confirmed microcalcification FFDM ROIs, test cross-domain shift on CBIS-DDSM, and inspect Grad-CAM maps (Abstract, p. 1; p. 2, p. 9).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Benign vs Malignant **microcalcification ROI patch** classification on **2D FFDM** (internal) and **digitized SFM** (`CBIS-DDSM` external) (`pp. 3, 9`; coarse calcifications >1.5 mm excluded, p. 3).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Changhua Christian Hospital FFDM (Taiwan, Jan 2015–Dec 2020; IRB `220204`, pp. 3–5, Table 1 & Fig. 2):**
  - `7,642` women screened -> `5,222` w/ calcifications -> `1,837` enrolled participants producing **`3,674` cropped calcification images**:
    - Training pool (`5-fold CV`): `869` Benign (`BI-RADS 1–2`) + `660` Malignant (`BI-RADS 5–6`) = `1,529` participants (~`700` images/fold, p. 8).
    - Held-out Validation/Test (`Fig. 2, Table 1, pp. 4–5`): `133` Benign + `175` Malignant = `308` participants. Exact image counts split across 1,529 vs 308 participants: `not reported` beyond `3,674` total.
- **External Validation (`p. 9`, `Fig. 8 p. 10`):** **CBIS-DDSM** (~`1,566` cases noted; exact CBIS-DDSM calcification image count evaluated `not reported`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Final labels require **definitive pathological confirmation** (`FNA, CNB, or open biopsy`) paired with **ACR BI-RADS `1–2` (Benign) vs `5–6` (Malignant)**; **`BI-RADS 0` and `BI-RADS 4` (`4A/4B/4C`) explicitly excluded** (`pp. 2–4, 12`). Radiologists (`>10 yrs` exp.) consensus-annotated calcification polygons in Labelme (`first 400 manual`, rest model-assisted + senior verification, p. 3).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`patient_level_5fold`** (`1,529` CV participants vs `308` held-out validation participants, Table 1 p. 5; ensemble averaging 5 folds on validation, p. 4). *Caveat: Separate CC/MLO inference on internal cohort noted on p. 12; exact fold-wise participant disaggregation `not reported`.*
- **External Validation:** **Zero-shot evaluation on CBIS-DDSM** (`Fig. 8, pp. 9–10`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **ImageNet-Pretrained EfficientNet `B0–B4`** (binary linear head + `Dropout(0.5)`, `200x200` to `380x380` variant sizing; B5+ halted due to decline/2x compute, p. 3, p. 5) vs **ResNet-50** (`23.5M`) & **ResNet-101** (`42.5M`) (`Table 2, p. 12`). PyTorch, NVIDIA A100 (`80 GB`). Optimizer, LR, loss, batch size, epochs, and seeds: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (p. 3):** GE Senographe / Hologic Selenia DICOM -> **16-bit grayscale PNG** (`2560x3320`) -> ROI crop (`224x224` & `260x260`, scaled `200x200`..`380x380`). Bit normalization, CLAHE, and CBIS-DDSM harmonization: `not reported`.
- **Augmentation:** Multi-scale training (`224/260`) + random affine (`resizing, rotation, reflection, cropping, translation`; exact params `not reported`, p. 3).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method:** **Grad-CAM** (`EfficientNet-B3` vs `ResNet-50`, targeting malignant class; `Fig. 9`, pp. 10–11).
- **Target Layer:** `not reported`.
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`2` cases: `1` benign, `1` malignant in Fig. 9 p. 11). Labelme calcification masks were **NOT** used for quantitative XAI overlap (`IoU/PG/Deletion/Adebayo = absent`).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Internal Results (`Abstract p. 1; Fig. 3 p. 5; Fig. 4 p. 6; Table 2 p. 12`):**
  - **EfficientNet-B3:** **Acc `86.9%` (`86.85%`)**, **wF1 `0.869`**, Macro F1 `0.867` (`Benign 0.881, Malignant 0.853`), **Table 2/Abstract AUC `0.998`** vs **Fig. 4(d) ROC plot AUC `0.93` (SEVERE MISMATCH!)**.
  - **EfficientNet-B0 (`4M`):** Acc `85.7%`, wF1 `0.857`, Table 2 AUC `0.996` vs **Fig. 4(a) AUC `0.94`**. **B4 (`17M`):** Acc `86.7%`, wF1 `0.868`, Table 2 AUC `0.998`.
  - **ResNet-50 (`23.5M`) / ResNet-101 (`42.5M`):** Acc `64.6% / 62.8%`, wF1 `0.650 / 0.633`, Malignant Recall `42.1% / 37.6%`, Table 2 AUC `0.77 / 0.75`.
- **External CBIS-DDSM (`Fig. 8, pp. 9–10`):** Bar charts only (exact numbers omitted): EfficientNet-B3 Acc `~0.52` / wF1 `~0.54`, B0 `~0.46`, ResNet-50/101 `~0.41–0.44` (steep FFDM -> SFM domain drop!).
- **Statistical Tests (`Figs. 6–7, pp. 8–9`):** 5-fold pairwise paired $t$-test & **Wilcoxon signed-rank** with **Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction**: paired $t$-test significant (`p <= 0.018` B0–B4 vs ResNets), **Wilcoxon non-significant (`all FDR p = 0.119`, Fig. 7)**.
- **Calibration (ECE/Brier), 95% CIs, DeLong, McNemar:** `not reported`.
