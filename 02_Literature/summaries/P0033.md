## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Adam M. Ibrahim, Jianqiang Li, Faheem Akhtar, Anis Ahmad Chaudhary, Mohamed A. M. Ali, Haneef Khan, and Musa Osman.
- **Title:** *A multi-modal deep learning framework for enhanced breast cancer diagnosis using mammograms and clinical data*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2026**, Vol. `16`, Article `17175`, pp. `1–23`.
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-026-48085-2`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q3` — 4-Dataset Multimodal Framework With Quantitative ROI Dice/IoU, AOPC & Reader Study).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Improve breast cancer diagnosis across heterogeneous cohorts by fusing **2D mammogram images** with **synthetic clinical metadata** through cross-modal attention and dual quantitative XAI (`pp. 1–10`).
- **Modality:** **Multimodal (2D Mammogram `224 x 224` + Synthetic Clinical Risk Tabular Features)** across digitized film (`CBIS-DDSM`) and FFDM (`INbreast`, `BCDR-D01`, `CMMD`) (**outside strict `O6` imaging-only scope** for fusion runs, though an image-only ablation is reported).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Four Public Imaging Cohorts Paired With Synthetic Clinical Attributes (`p. 11`, `Table 4`):**
  - `CBIS-DDSM`: `2,620` images / `1,566` patients (`train/val/internal`).
  - `INbreast`: `410` images / `115` patients (`train/val/internal`).
  - `BCDR-D01`: `1,734` images / `348` patients (**external test**).
  - `CMMD`: `5,000` images / `1,775` patients (**external test**).
  - **Synthetic Clinical Metadata (`Abstract`, `p. 11`, `Limitation 1`, p. 19):** Age, BMI, family history, BIRADS, etc. were **synthetically generated** due to missing clinical EHR records.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Pathology/biopsy labels across `CBIS-DDSM`, `CMMD`, `BCDR-D01`; BI-RADS/biopsy across `INbreast`. Explicit benign vs normal-vs-malignant sample totals across confusion matrices exhibit internal mismatches (see Section 11).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Patient-Level Protocol (`p. 11`):** `70%` train / `15%` val / `15%` test enforced at patient level, plus external testing on **`BCDR-D01`** (`AUC 0.947`, `Sens 91.8%`, `Spec 89.5%`) and **`CMMD`** (`AUC 0.939`, `Sens 90.7%`, `Spec 88.9%`, `Table 4`).
- *Caveat:* Confusion matrices `Fig. 9` (`n = 336`) and `Fig. 11` (`n = 950`) have inconsistent test totals relative to `Table 3`/`Table 6`.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Cross-modal transformer + CNN image branch paired with tabular clinical MLP (`pp. 6–10`). Specific image-branch resolution is `224 x 224` (`p. 11`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Images resized to **`224 x 224`** and **z-normalized** (`p. 11`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Quantitative XAI Metrics (`p. 17`):**
  - ROI Attention Localization: **`Dice = 0.76 (95% CI: 0.72–0.80)`**, **`IoU = 0.68 (95% CI: 0.64–0.72)`**.
  - Feature Attribution Faithfulness (`perturbation`): **`AOPC = 0.83`** (cross-modal / feature attribution).
  - **Clinical Reader Evaluation:** `3` radiologists rated `100` cases (`82%` aligned), and `7` radiologists completed a usability survey (`85.7%` rated explanations helpful, while **`42.9%` found cross-modal attention maps hard to interpret**).
- **Missing Checks:** Zero `Adebayo` weight/data randomization sanity checks (`p. 17`).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Full Multimodal vs Image-Only Ablation (`Tables 3–8`, pp. 12–18):**
  - Multimodal overall (`Table 6`, p. 16): **`AUC = 0.963 (0.951–0.975)`**, `Sens = 94.1%`, `Spec = 91.2%`, `Acc = 94.6%` (`vs 92.4%` reported in `Table 3`, p. 12 — internal discrepancy).
  - External (`Table 4`): `BCDR-D01 AUC 0.947`, `CMMD AUC 0.939`.
  - **Image-only ablation (`Table 5`):** drops sharply to **`AUC = 0.912, Sens = 87.3%, Spec = 84.6%`**.
  - **Density Stratification (`Table 7`, p. 18):** monotonically degrades from `BI-RADS A AUC = 0.978` down to **`BI-RADS D AUC = 0.921`** (`-0.057` AUC gap!).
  - **Statistics:** DeLong vs `MM-TransFuse` (`p = 0.008`); `5` random seeds (`AUC SD = 0.007`).
- **Calibration:** `not reported` (`zero` `ECE`, `Brier score`, or reliability curve).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 19`):** Reliance on **synthetic clinical covariates** (Limitation 1); single retrospective curation pipeline.
- **Reviewer critique:** (1) Out of strict `O6` imaging-only thesis scope because clinical inputs boost AUC by `+0.051` (`0.912 -> 0.963`) using **synthetic clinical data** (`risk of target leakage or idealized covariate distribution`); (2) Unresolved internal table mismatches (`Acc 92.4% Table 3` vs `94.6% Table 6`; `n = 336 Fig. 9` vs `n = 950 Fig. 11`); (3) Useful reference point for **`Dice/IoU + AOPC + BI-RADS A->D degradation` (`0.978 -> 0.921`)**, yet lacks `ECE/Brier` and `Adebayo` checks.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B2` (Multimodal Synthetic-Clinical Comparator; Excluded From Strict `O6` Imaging-Only Baseline):** Segregated from pure-imaging models (`B1`), though quantitative `AOPC/Dice/IoU` (`p. 17`) and density drop (`0.978 -> 0.921`, `Table 7`) inform `RG1–RG4`.
