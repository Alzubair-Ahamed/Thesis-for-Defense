## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Mitigate cross-dataset shift across SFM (`CBIS-DDSM`) and FFDM (`INbreast`) via attention fusion of DenseNet-121, ViT-B/16, and PyRadiomics features with stacked XGBoost/LightGBM/RBF-SVM + LR meta-learner (Abstract, p. 1; Sec. 1, p. 2; Sec. 3.1.4, p. 6).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Benign vs Malignant **2D mammography** (`CBIS-DDSM` SFM + `INbreast` FFDM) using **lesion-centered ROI crops (`224x224`)** extracted from segmentation masks or +10% expanded boxes (Sec. 3.1–3.2.2, pp. 5–7).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **CBIS-DDSM (SFM, Sec. 3.1.1 & 3.1.3, pp. 5–6):** Described approximately as `"about 3000 mammographic images"` (`~52%` malignant, `~48%` benign). Exact N, patients, exams, mass/calc breakdown: **`not reported`**.
- **INbreast (FFDM, Sec. 3.1.2 & 3.1.3, p. 6):** Stated `410` images (`~51%` malignant, `~49%` benign after filtering). Exact filtered N, patients, exams: **`not reported`**. Combined cohort N: `not reported`.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- CBIS-DDSM pathology labels (p. 5); INbreast BI-RADS/confirmed labels (p. 6; exact BI-RADS cutoff `not reported`). Normal handling (`BI-RADS 1`) & `BENIGN_WITHOUT_CALLBACK` policy: `not reported`.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** Formally specified **`patient_level_5fold_cv`** (`~80/20`, Sec. 3.1.4, p. 6; Sec. 3.9, pp. 14–15), with histogram matching, augmentation, and z-score fitted on training folds only and out-of-fold stacking (`Sec. 3.6–3.7`).
- **External Validation:** Bidirectional cross-dataset transfer (`CBIS -> INbreast` and `INbreast -> CBIS`, Tables 3–4, p. 18).
- **Caveats:** Exact fold patient/image counts unreported; relies on ground-truth ROI segmentation masks at test time (Radiomics alone hits `94.8%` Acc vs CNN `92.8%`, Table 5 p. 20); multi-seed stability `not reported`.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **3-Branch Architecture (`224x224`, Sec. 3.3–3.7, pp. 8–13):** **DenseNet-121** (ImageNet, frozen first 10 ep, `1024-d`) + **ViT-B/16** (ImageNet, `768-d`) + **PyRadiomics v3.0** (`12` features: 4 GLCM, 4 shape, 4 intensity) -> attention-weighted fusion -> out-of-fold stacked **XGBoost + LightGBM + RBF-SVM** -> **Logistic Regression** (`C=0.1`). PyTorch 2.0, RTX 3090, AdamW (`lr=1e-4`), max `50` ep *(internal mismatch between Sec. 3.6 batch `32`/dropout `0.1` vs Sec. 3.9 batch `16`/dropout `0.3`)*.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Z-score -> histogram equalization -> mask/+10% box ROI crop -> `224x224` -> **domain histogram matching** + `0.5–99.5` percentile clipping (train-fold statistics only, Sec. 3.2, p. 7; Sec. 3.9.4, p. 16). Train-only geom/intensity augmentations (`exact params not reported`, Sec. 3.2.3, p. 7).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Methods (Sec. 5, pp. 22–25):** **Grad-CAM** (CNN; Fig. 5 labels `DenseNet Layer 4/3`, exact layer name absent from Sec. 5.2), **Attention Rollout** (ViT-B/16, 12 layers), **SHAP** + XGBoost feature gain.
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`mean |SHAP| feature ranking` + abstract visual overlays** (Fig. 5, p. 24 ranks top 10 features e.g. GLCM Contrast ~`0.28`, ViT Class Token ~`0.24`; Fig. 4 p. 23 depicts synthetic concentric-circle overlays). Authors explicitly acknowledge (Sec. 5.6, p. 25) zero spatial ROI-mask overlap metrics (IoU/PG), zero faithfulness/Adebayo checks, and zero radiologist review.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Within-Dataset 5-Fold CV (Tables 1–2, p. 17):** CBIS-DDSM Hybrid Acc **`95.8 ± 0.5%`**, AUC **`0.978 ± 0.004`**, MCC **`0.915 ± 0.008`** (vs DenseNet `90.2%`/`0.931`); INbreast Hybrid Acc **`96.6 ± 0.7%`**, AUC **`0.982 ± 0.006`**, MCC **`0.933 ± 0.011`** (vs DenseNet `91.9%`/`0.944`).
- **Bidirectional Cross-Dataset (Tables 3–4, p. 18):**
  - `CBIS -> INbreast`: Acc **`89.8 ± 1.0%`**, Sens `91.4%`, Spec `88.0%`, **AUC `0.938 (0.92–0.95)`**, MCC `0.801`.
  - `INbreast -> CBIS`: Acc **`88.5 ± 1.2%`**, Sens `89.6%`, Spec `87.3%`, **AUC `0.925 (0.90–0.94)`**, MCC `0.775`.
- **Combined Ablation (Table 5, p. 20):** Full Stacked Acc **`96.3%`** (AUC `0.983`, MCC `0.928`).
- **Statistical Tests (Sec. 4.2, p. 19):** **DeLong test** AUC `p = 0.012` within CBIS-DDSM (Proposed vs CNN+ViT) & `p = 0.014` on `CBIS->INbreast`. McNemar, Calibration (ECE/Brier), PR-AUC, Confusion Matrices: `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (pp. 25–26):** Spatial XAI overlap and clinician evaluation omitted; larger cohorts needed.
- **Reviewer critique:** Promising bidirectional SFM<->FFDM validation with DeLong statistics, weakened by omitted exact filtered N/patient counts, reliance on oracle lesion masks at inference (`94.8%` Radiomics-only Acc), hyperparam mismatches (Sec. 3.6 vs 3.9), synthetic Fig. 4 visuals, and missing spatial XAI, calibration, confusion matrices, and code.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — ROI+Radiomics Cross-Dataset Regime):** Key reference for bidirectional `CBIS-DDSM <-> INbreast` generalization and DeLong comparison, though non-comparable numerically to mask-free whole-mammogram DL models due to ROI/shape-radiomics oracle cropping and unreported exact cohort N.
