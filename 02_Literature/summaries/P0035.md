## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Jon Donnelly, Luke Moffett, Alina Jade Barnett, Hari Trivedi, Fides Schwartz, Joseph Lo, and Cynthia Rudin.
- **Title:** *AsymMirai: Interpretable Mammography-based Deep Learning Model for 1–5-year Breast Cancer Risk Prediction*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Radiology*, **2024**, Vol. `310`, No. `3`, `e232780`, pp. `1–10`.
- **DOI:** `10.1148/radiol.232780`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Landmark Inherently Interpretable Bilateral-Asymmetry Risk Model With `DeLong` CIs & Confounder Audit).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Determine whether localized bilateral breast tissue dissimilarity underpins `Mirai`'s 1–5-year breast cancer risk predictions and build an inherently interpretable spatial alternative (`AsymMirai`) approximating `Mirai` (`pp. 1–2`).
- **Task & Modality:** **1–5-year future breast cancer risk prediction** (NOT lesion malignancy classification) on **4-view 2D screening FFDM** (`L-CC, R-CC, L-MLO, R-MLO`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Emory BrEast imaging Dataset (`EMBED`; `p. 2`, `Fig. 1` p. 3, `Table 1` p. 7):**
  - Full `EMBED` screening sample after exclusions (removing data errors `42` pts, non-2D/DBT `88` pts, missing 4 views `5,810` pts, diagnostic exams `1,228` pts): **`210,067` screening mammograms from `81,824` patients** (mean age `59.5 ± 11.4` yrs; `42.0%` African American [`34,369`], `40.8%` White [`33,352`], `Hologic 92% / GE 6% / Fujifilm 2%`).
  - **Primary Evaluated Validation Split (`Fig. 1`, `Table 1`):** `16,314` patients / `41,988` exams (down to `5,419` pts / `10,598` exams with full 5-yr follow-up; `236` cancer patients in filtered validation split). Notably reveals that excluding diagnostic exams lowered `Mirai` 5-yr AUC from `0.76` to `0.71` (`p. 4`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Prospective Risk Endpoint (`p. 2`):** Pathologically confirmed cancer within `n` years (positive) vs negative follow-up screening at least `n` years later (negative); diagnostic exams excluded (`Fig. 1`, p. 3).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Patient-Level Protocol (`p. 2`, `Fig. 1`):** Follows `EMBED` official patient-level train (`70,136` pts) / validation (`23,382` pts before screening filter) / test (`23,333` pts) split (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN` on screening-only subset; notes prior `Mirai` validation leaked diagnostic signal by keeping diagnostic mammograms, `+0.05` AUC bias).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`AsymMirai` vs `Mirai` (`Fig. 2`, p. 4):**
  - Shared `ResNet-18` CNN backbone encodes each of 4 views (`L/R CC & MLO`).
  - `Mirai` passes all features to a Transformer + Additive Hazard Layer (`AHL`) black box.
  - `AsymMirai` strips out the Transformer & clinical risk inputs and computes localized `L–R` latent feature dissimilarity per view, taking the spatial maximum prediction window (`1/25` of view size) and averaging CC + MLO dissimilarity scores (`Fig. 2`, p. 4).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Standardized 4-view 2D FFDM input matching `Mirai` without spatial image registration (deliberately omitted to avoid confounding comparison against `Mirai`, though identified in `Limitation 3`, p. 8, as a cause of positioning confounders).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Inherently Interpretable Design (`S-XAI`, `pp. 1–5`, `Figs. 2–4`):**
  - Rejects post-hoc `Grad-CAM` / `Grad-CAM++` as unreliable (`p. 1`, citing `Rudin 2019`, `Adebayo 2018`, `Arun 2021`).
  - Spatial dissimilarity heat map + red prediction box (`1/25` view area) directly correspond to the additive prediction term (`100%` structural faithfulness to the module's score).
  - **Quantitative Reasoning & Confounder Audits:**
    - Correlation vs `Mirai`: Pearson `r = 0.6832` (1-yr), `0.7011` (3-yr), `0.6987` (5-yr) (`Fig. 5C`, p. 8).
    - **Location Consistency (`Fig. 4`, p. 6; `10,001` longitudinal patients):** When prediction window shift is `≤40%` (`n = 183` 3-yr valid pts), 3-yr AUC reaches **`0.92 (0.86, 0.97)`** (`1-yr 0.92, 5-yr 0.88`), though only `0.9%` developed cancer vs `2.6%` overall.
    - **Confounder Exposure (`Fig. 3D–F`, p. 5):** Visual overlays expose false high risk driven by excluded unilateral implants (`Fig. 3D`), poor positioning (`Fig. 3E`), and benign dystrophic/microcalcifications (`Fig. 3E–F`) — proving interpretable localization enables confounder audits impossible in `Mirai`.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Discriminative AUC (`Abstract`, `p. 4`, `Table 2` p. 9, `Fig. 5` p. 8; `DeLong` 95% CI):**
  - `1-yr`: `AsymMirai 0.79 (0.73, 0.85)` vs `Mirai 0.84 (0.79, 0.89)`, `p = 0.002`
  - `2-yr`: `0.69 (0.65, 0.73)` vs `0.74 (0.70, 0.78)`, `p < 0.001`
  - `3-yr`: `0.68 (0.65, 0.71)` vs `0.72 (0.69, 0.76)`, `p < 0.001`
  - `4-yr`: `0.67 (0.64, 0.70)` vs `0.72 (0.69, 0.75)`, `p < 0.001`
  - `5-yr`: `0.66 (0.63, 0.69)` vs `0.71 (0.68, 0.74)`, `p < 0.001`
- **Subgroup Equity Gap (`Table 2`, p. 9):** Both models underperformed on **African American** (`3-yr AUC: AsymMirai 0.63, Mirai 0.69`, `n = 6,812`) vs **White** women (`0.73` / `0.77`, `n = 6,689`), attributed to Mirai's `3.75%` AA training backbone (`p. 8`).
- **Calibration & Density Subgroup:** **Probability calibration (`ECE`, `Brier score`) and BI-RADS density-stratified AUCs are `not reported`**.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- **Open-Source Release (`p. 2`):** `https://github.com/jdonnelly36/AsymMirai/releases/tag/radiology-1.0`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 8`):** Transformer features outside bilateral dissimilarity remain unexplained; inherited racial bias from `Mirai` backbone (`3.75%` AA training); lack of spatial registration causes alignment confounding; longitudinal follow-up attrition (`10.7%` at 5 yrs for `≤40%` shift).
- **Reviewer critique:** Landmark demonstration of why **`CO-12` XAI enables clinical confounder auditing** (`Fig. 3`) and longitudinal consistency quantification (`Fig. 4`), plus exposing `0.05` diagnostic-exam leakage inflation in `Mirai` (`p. 4`). Not directly comparable to lesion-malignancy classification baselines (`task mismatch: 1–5-yr risk` on `EMBED`), and omits calibration (`ECE/Brier`) and density stratification.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (Interpretable Mammography Design & Confounder-Audit Landmark):** Methodological gold standard for inherent localization (`S-XAI`), DeLong subgroup testing, and longitudinal stability audits; distinct task (`1–5-yr future risk`) and lacks `ECE/Brier` or BI-RADS density breakdown.

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- Probability calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`); BI-RADS A–D density-stratified risk AUCs; post-hoc vs `AsymMirai` pointing/IoU comparison on biopsy-localized lesions.
