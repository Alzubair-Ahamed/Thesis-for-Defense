## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Suxing Liu, Galib Muhammad Shahriar Himel, and Jiahao Wang (p. 196647 / PDF p. 1).
- **Title:** *Breast Cancer Classification With Enhanced Interpretability: DALAResNet50 and DT Grad-CAM* (p. 196647).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Pages:** *IEEE Access*, **2024**, Vol. `12`, pp. `196647–196659` (Published 20 Dec 2024, p. 196647).
- **DOI:** `10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3520608` (p. 196647).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q1a`, `GS-Q1c`, `GS-Q2b`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Reduce SE/CBAM attention overhead and noisy Grad-CAM localization via a dual-activated lightweight attention module (`DALAResNet50`) and Otsu-thresholded `DT Grad-CAM` (Sec. I, pp. 196647–196648).
- **Modality Isolation (`B4_CTX` Quarantine):**
  - **2D Mammography Arm (`Mini-DDSM`):** Digitized SFM reduced subset, 3-class (`Normal`, `Benign`, `Malignant`) whole-image classification resized to `224x224` (Sec. IV-B–C, pp. 196651–196652; Table 5, p. 196652).
  - **Off-Modality Histopathology Arms (`BreakHis`, `BACH` — excluded from mammography synthesis):** BreakHis (40x–400x binary) & BACH (4-class). *(Paper repeatedly mislabels Mini-DDSM and the whole task as "histopathology images" across Abstract, Sec. I, Fig. 5, and Sec. IV-C.)*

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Mini-DDSM (2D Mammography):** Lekamlage et al. (2020) [44] (`2,000` images: `500` Normal, `700` Benign, `800` Malignant; subset selection vs full Mini-DDSM `not reported`, Table 5, p. 196652). 6:1:3 split (Table 6, p. 196653): Train `1,200`, Val `200`, Test `600`. Patient/exam/per-class split counts: `not reported`.
- **BreakHis (Off-modality):** `7,909` images across 40x (`1,995`), 100x (`2,081`), 200x (`2,013`), 400x (`1,820`) (text p. 196651 `2,429+5,429` vs Table 3 `2,480+5,429` mismatch); 6:1:3 splits in Table 6 (p. 196653).
- **BACH (Off-modality):** `400` H&E images (`100`/class; Table 4, p. 196652); 6:1:3 split = `240/40/120` (Table 6, p. 196653).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Mini-DDSM inherits `Normal` / `Benign` / `Malignant` classes from [44] without specifying biopsy vs BI-RADS provenance (`not reported`, Table 5, p. 196652). Normal (`500` images) kept as an explicit 3rd class.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** `image_level_60_10_30` (Sec. IV-C, p. 196652; Table 6, p. 196653). Zero patient-level or CC/MLO view grouping (`not reported`), creating leakage risk. Text order in Sec. IV-C leaves ambiguity regarding pre- vs post-split augmentation (Table 6 totals match raw counts).
- **External Validation:** None (internal test splits only).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`DALAResNet50`:** Pretrained ResNet50 + dual-activation attention (`AAP -> FC -> LeakyReLU -> FC -> ReLU`, residual fusion) inserted in layer-4 based on BreakHis ablation (Table 2, p. 196651; Sec. III-B, pp. 196650–196651); `24,040,643` params on Mini-DDSM (Table 10, p. 196654 vs unexplained `25,611,842` on BreakHis Table 9).
- **Comparators:** SEResNet50, DenseNet-121, VGG16, VGG16Inception, ViT, Swin-Transformer, Dinov2_Vitb14, ResNet50 (Tables 7–12; baseline weight provenance `not reported`). Input `224x224`, LR=`1e-4`, batch=`32`, epochs=`50`, dropout=`0.25` (PyTorch 2.0.1, RTX A4000, Sec. IV-A, p. 196651). Optimizer, loss function, scheduler, and seeds: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Resized to `224x224`, pixel values scaled to `[-1, 1]`, random rotation + horizontal flips (exact angles/probs `not reported`, Sec. IV-C, p. 196652). Bit-depth, CLAHE, and view-marker removal absent/not reported (`LMLO`/`RMLO` stamps visible in Fig. 5, p. 196652).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Methods:** Proposed **DT Grad-CAM** (Gaussian noise Perturbation -> weighted average Grad-CAM -> Otsu adaptive thresholding -> morphological cleanup; Sec. V-A, pp. 196654–196656), Grad-CAM, Score-CAM, Eigen-CAM.
- **Target Layer:** Generic "last convolutional layer" (Sec. II-B, p. 196649; exact layer name, `N`, `sigma`, and kernel `not reported`).
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`absent` on Mini-DDSM mammograms (0 figures, 0 metrics)**; BreakHis-only IoU/Dice/Recall/F1 (`0.3931–0.4009` IoU, `0.5567–0.5644` Dice for DT Grad-CAM vs `0.2969–0.3132` IoU Grad-CAM, Table 14 & Fig. 7, p. 196656) without disclosing ground-truth mask source on BreakHis. No deletion/insertion/ROAD, Adebayo sanity check, or clinician study.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Mini-DDSM (3-class, N=600, Table 8 p. 196653 & Table 12 p. 196655):**
  - `DALAResNet50`: Acc `0.8070`, F1 `0.806/0.807`, IBA `0.818`, GMean `0.851`, Sens `0.809`, Spec `0.806`, PPV `0.881`, NPV `0.704` (`5990.31` s, Table 10).
  - Top baseline `ViT`: Acc `0.7515`, Sens `0.753`, Spec `0.750`; `ResNet50`: Acc `0.6686`, `Sens/Spec/PPV/NPV = 0.669/0.667/0.781/0.531`; `Dinov2_Vitb14`: Acc `0.667` with **identical** `Table 12` row (`0.669/0.667/0.781/0.531` copy-paste error!).
  - **AUC-ROC on Mini-DDSM:** `not reported`.
- **Off-modality BreakHis (`DALAResNet50`, Tables 7 & 11):** `98.5%` (40X), `98.7%` (100X), `97.9%` (200X), `94.3%` (400X). **BACH:** Acc `0.891` (Tables 8, 12).
- **Calibration / 95% CIs / Statistical Tests (DeLong/McNemar):** `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated:** Future integration into clinical workflows only (Sec. VI, p. 196657).
- **Reviewer critique:** (1) Severe numerical integrity errors on Mini-DDSM: duplicated Table 12 metrics for `ResNet50` & `Dinov2_Vitb14`, non-integer accuracies over `N=600` (`0.8070*600=484.2`), missing AUC-ROC; (2) zero mammography XAI experiments and undisclosed BreakHis ground-truth mask provenance in Table 14; (3) image-level splitting, unexplained 2,000-image Mini-DDSM sampling, unremoved `LMLO`/`RMLO` stamps (Fig. 5), and miscited Ref [34].

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B4_CTX` (Contextual Quarantine):** Primarily histopathology paper whose Mini-DDSM sub-experiment lacks patient splits, AUC-ROC, and mammography XAI results, plus Table 12 copy-paste anomalies; retained only for `DT Grad-CAM` Otsu-thresholding methodological reference.
