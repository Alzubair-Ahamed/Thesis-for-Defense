## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Author:** Ahmed Jaheen (New York University, New York, NY, USA).
- **Title:** *Do Saliency Maps Fail Dense Breasts? A Chance-Normalized, Confounder-Aware Audit of Explanation Fairness in Mammography*.
- **Venue / Year / Preprint ID:** **arXiv preprint** `arXiv:2611.05909v1 [cs.CV]`, **2026** (4 Nov 2026), pp. `1–5`.
- **DOI / Identifier:** `arXiv:2611.05909v1` (OpenAlex discovery `P0810` via Q2/Q4 screening).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (Stage-2 Open-Access Newly Discovered Landmark; supersedes naive `RG4`/`RG1` assumptions).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Test whether post-hoc saliency maps locate cancers worse in dense breasts (`BI-RADS 3–4`) than non-dense breasts (`BI-RADS 1–2`) after accounting for chance localization level and case-mix confounding, and verify faithfulness (`ROAD`) and lesion reliance (`LSR`) (`Sec. 1`, p. 1).
- **Task & Modality:** Whole-image digitized film **2D SFM** (`CBIS-DDSM`) binary **malignant vs benign lesion-view classification** (`"Every view holds a lesion that a radiologist found... It is not screening"`, `Sec. 2.1`, p. 2).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **`CBIS-DDSM` (`Sec. 2.1`, p. 1; `Fig. 1`, p. 2):**
  - Full CBIS-DDSM cohort: `3,568` radiologist-outlined lesions across `3,103` digitized film images of `1,566` patients -> merging `71` duplicates yields **`3,032` views** (evaluated across 5 patient-grouped folds for AUC `Sec. 3`, p. 3).
  - **Primary Malignant Saliency Audit Set (`P`):** **`1,345` malignant views** (`751` patients) with known pre-5th-edition BI-RADS breast density: **`713` non-dense** (BI-RADS 1–2) vs **`632` dense** (BI-RADS 3–4) (`p. 1, Fig. 1`).
  - Dense malignant views were smaller (median breast area `159` vs `198 cm^2`) and contained more calcifications (`58.7%` vs `31.8%`) than non-dense views (`p. 1`).
  - **External validation / Digital FFDM:** None (explicitly stated future work, `p. 4`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Biopsy-confirmed malignant vs benign pathology labels and radiologist lesion contours from CBIS-DDSM; reader-assigned pre-5th-edition BI-RADS breast density (`p. 1, p. 4`).
- **Normal-case Handling:** **Zero normal (`BI-RADS 1` normal) views** — strictly malignant vs benign lesion-containing views (`p. 2`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`patient_grouped_5fold_cv`** across all `3,032` merged views (`15` classifiers = `3` architectures × `5` folds; `p. 2`).
- **Leakage Assessment:** Zero patient leakage in internal CV (`71` duplicate views merged prior to splitting); temperature `T` and decision threshold `t` fitted cleanly on internal validation logits inside training folds (`p. 2`). Single digitized-film dataset only (no external site).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **3 ImageNet-Pretrained CNNs (`Sec. 2.1`, pp. 1–2):** **`ResNet-50`**, **`EfficientNet-B3`**, **`ConvNeXt-T`** (binary malignancy logit $z$).
- **Training & Calibration:** AdamW with early stopping on validation AUC, **temperature scaling (`T`)** fitted on validation logits (`p. 2`). Primary results use `1` training seed (`plus 2 additional seeds` for `Grad-CAM` exploratory stability analysis, `p. 3–4`). Vision Transformers (`ViT/Swin`) and explicit seed integers/resolutions: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (`Sec. 2.1–2.2`, pp. 1–2):** Breast mask $B$ = largest connected region above `2%` of image intensity range joined with malignant outline mask $M$. Note `4` acquisition proxies tracked in `M1` including left-right flipped storage indicator (`p. 2`). Exact resize dimensions/augmentation: `not reported`.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **3 XAI Methods (`Sec. 2.2`, p. 2):** **`Grad-CAM`** (last conv layer, `~6 mm` cells), **`Integrated Gradients` (`IG`)** (`64–512` steps from black image, completeness `<5%` Δz), and **`Occlusion`** (`12.5-mm` blur window shifted by `6-mm` steps). Only positive attributions $S \ge 0$ inside $B$ scored.
- **Quantitative XAI Localization Metrics (`Sec. 2.2`, p. 2; `Table 1`, p. 3):**
  1. **Pointing Game** (`1-mm` smoothing, hit within `5 mm` of $M$, $D_5$, chance $\pi_{PG}=|D_5|/|B|$): Crude dense-minus-non-dense gaps = **`Grad-CAM -14.1 pp [-19.3, -9.2]`** (`44%` dense vs `58%` non-dense hits), **`IG -10.4 pp [-14.9, -5.9]`**, **`Occlusion -12.7 pp [-17.4, -8.1]`** (`Table 1`, p. 3).
  2. **Chance-Normalized Energy Lift ($\lambda_E$, bits; Eq. 1 p. 2):** Crude dense gaps = **`Grad-CAM -0.86 bit [-1.23, -0.51]`**, **`IG -0.62 bit [-0.80, -0.45]`**, **`Occlusion -1.27 bit [-1.72, -0.84]`**.
  3. **M1 Case-Mix Adjusted GEE Gaps (`Table 1`, pp. 3–4):** Adjusting for lesion area, breast area, lesion type (calcification vs mass), view, multiple lesions, margin, and flip proxy removed `81%` of Grad-CAM gap (`-0.15 [-0.53, +0.23]`, TOST eq. to zero, Holm `p=0.83`), `118%` of IG gap (`+0.10 [-0.14, +0.35]`, Holm `p=0.83`), while **`Occlusion` retained a Holm-significant `-0.56 bit [-1.00, -0.12]` gap (Holm `p=0.040`)**, concentrated in `EfficientNet-B3` (`-1.19 bit` vs `-0.07` `ResNet-50`, `-0.52` `ConvNeXt-T`). *(With method-specific slopes [exploratory], no M1 gap remained significant, p. 4).*
- **Faithfulness, Sanity & Controls (`Sec. 2.4 & 3.3`, pp. 3–4):**
  - **`ROAD` Faithfulness (`SRG`):** `1%–20%` breast pixel interpolation; **`SRG > 0` in `94%–99%` of views**, crude negative gaps for Grad-CAM (`-0.20`) and Occlusion (`-0.24`), no adjusted SRG gap Holm-significant (`p >= 0.33`).
  - **Adebayo Randomized-Network Sanity Check (`Grad-CAM` all CNNs; `IG` on `ResNet-50`):** Randomized maps showed zero density gap; trained gaps exceeded randomized by `0.84` bit (Grad-CAM) and `0.67` bit (IG, both Holm `p = 0.0125`).
  - **Lesion-Specific Reliance (`LSR`):** Logit drop replacing $D_5$ vs random breast regions (`n=1,276` views); lower in dense breasts both crudely (**`-0.34 [-0.52, -0.16]`**) and adjusted (**`-0.24 [-0.46, -0.02]`**).
  - **Conspicuity Control (`DoG`):** `-0.44 bit` in dense breasts; crude XAI gaps exceeded DoG by `0.18–0.83 bit`.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Held-Out 5-Fold CV Classification (`3,032` CBIS-DDSM views, `Sec. 3`, p. 3):**
  - Average held-out **AUC `0.79`**; lower in dense breasts (**`0.755` vs `0.820`**, gap **`-0.065 [-0.098, -0.032]`**, TOST failed `±0.05` equivalence).
- **Statistical Rigor:** `2,000` patient-clustered bootstrap resamples; **GEE** with Mancl-DeRouen bias-reduced SEs; g-computation; **Holm step-down multiplicity correction** (families of 3 and 25 tests); **TOST equivalence** (`±0.5 bit`, `±10 pp`).
- **Calibration Reporting:** Temperature scaling `T` fitted (`p. 2`) for margin/ROAD scaling, **but numeric `ECE` and `Brier` calibration scores are `not reported`**.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 4`):** Single digitized-film dataset (`CBIS-DDSM`), reader-assigned pre-5th-edition BI-RADS density, benign-vs-malignant lesion task (not full screening), digital FFDM replication left as future work.
- **Reviewer critique:** Exceptionally rigorous confounder-aware XAI audit that **directly tests density-stratified XAI fairness (`RG4`)** using chance-normalized Energy Lift ($\lambda_E$), Pointing Game, `ROAD` faithfulness, Adebayo weight randomization, `LSR`, DoG controls, GEE, Holm, and TOST. Critically leaves **four unresolved empirical gaps directly aligned with `O1–O6`**: (1) uses digitized film (`CBIS-DDSM`) only without **multi-site digital FFDM** (`VinDr-Mammo`, `CMMD`, `DMID`) or **external cross-site validation** (`RG3`); (2) includes zero screening Normal (`BI-RADS 1`) exams; (3) evaluates CNNs only (no Vision Transformers / hybrid CNN-ViT models); and (4) fits temperature scaling but omits **quantitative calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`)**, reliability diagrams, and cross-density calibration fairness audits (`RG4/RG2`).
