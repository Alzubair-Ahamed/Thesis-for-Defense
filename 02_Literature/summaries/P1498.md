## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **Objective:** Release a categorized public CESM collection pairing standard low-energy (`DM`, `26–31 kVp`) and contrast-subtracted mammograms with clinical annotations, hand-drawn radiologist segmentations, and EfficientNetB0 + Grad-CAM baseline validation.
- **Modality Caveat (`RF15`, `OS §4.1`):** Distributes **both** `1,003` **Low-Energy (`DM`)** images (`0.64 GB`, standard 2D mammography physics) and `1,003` **Subtracted (`CESM`)** contrast-enhanced images (`0.82 GB`) as lossless **`.jpg`** exported via RadiAnt DICOM Viewer (`no raw DICOM headers`). Standard 2D mammography tracks under `RF15` must restrict inputs to **Low-Energy (`LE/DM`)** only.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Collection (`CDD-CESM`):** `326` female patients (`18–90` yrs, NCI Cairo University, Egypt, Jan `2019`–Feb `2021`), **`2,006` total images** (`average 2355 x 1315 px`; `~30%` cropped of unused borders; `1,003` Low-Energy `DM` + `1,003` Subtracted `CESM`), `CC` & `MLO` views for Left/Right breasts (`Table 2`):
  - `757` Normal (`341` DM + `416` CESM), `587` Benign (`331` DM + `256` CESM), `662` Malignant (`331` DM + `331` CESM; incl. `445` IDC, `42` ILC, mixed).
  - Includes hand-drawn radiologist polygonal segmentations (`Radiology_hand_drawn_segmentations_v2.csv`), clinical metadata (`Radiology-manual-annotations.xlsx`: `ACR` breast composition, `BI-RADS`, mass/calc/enhancement tags), and `326` de-identified `DOCX` clinical reports.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Pathology-confirmed malignant subtypes & benign findings alongside normal cases (`Table 2`) and full `ACR BI-RADS` descriptors (`Table 1`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- Descriptor validation trains a 3-class (`Normal/Benign/Malignant`) EfficientNetB0 model + Grad-CAM segmentation thresholding without specifying strict connected-component patient-level split bounds in the descriptor validation section.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Baseline `EfficientNetB0` (ImageNet-pretrained, `224 x 224` input, Adam optimizer, categorical cross-entropy, GAN + geometric augmentation).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- DICOM exported to lossless JPEG (`~30%` cropped borders), resized to `224 x 224`, mean/std normalized, plus geometric & GAN-based augmentation.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation

- Computes `Grad-CAM` from ground-truth classes, strips image-corner pectoral highlights, intersections top `25%` Grad-CAM with top `15%` white pixels, and evaluates `overlap50` (`IoU` / `Dice` / `overlap50`) against radiologist hand-drawn segmentations (`81%` Low-Energy DM vs `86%` Subtracted CESM; `75%` Benign vs `90%` Malignant).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics

- Reports `IoU` / `Dice` / `overlap50` across age, density, modality, and lesion subtypes; omits `ECE/Brier` calibration and formal cross-model DeLong/McNemar statistics.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Dataset hosted on TCIA (`10.7937/29kw-ae92`, `CC BY 4.0`, `~1.49 GB`, Aspera Faspex for full image bundle + direct HTTP for reports/CSV/XLSX); helper repo at `https://github.com/omar-mohamed/CDD-CESM-Dataset`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- Single-center Cairo cohort (`326` patients), JPEG export discards DICOM header tags (`no MONOCHROME/LUT audit`), `~30%` manual pre-cropping varies aspect ratio, and half of the `2,006` files are iodine contrast-subtracted (`must isolate Low-Energy DM subset for RF15 compliance`).
