## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Kosmia Loizidou, Galateia Skouroumouni, Costas Pitris, and Christos Nikolaou.
- **Title:** *Digital subtraction of temporally sequential mammograms for improved detection and classification of microcalcifications*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *European Radiology Experimental* (Springer Nature; **2024 JIF 3.6, Q1** in Radiology, Nuclear Medicine & Medical Imaging), **2021**, Vol. `5`, Issue `1`, Article `40`.
- **DOI / IDs:** `10.1186/s41747-021-00238-w` | `PMCID: PMC8440760` | `PMID: 34519867` | Dataset Zenodo DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.5036062` (`CC BY 4.0`).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`Dataset Descriptor & Open-Access Candidate Discovery — BMCD`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **Objective:** Evaluate temporal digital subtraction of prior vs recent 2D `FFDM` mammograms for detecting and classifying microcalcifications (`BI-RADS` benign vs suspicious).
- **Modality:** Strictly `2D FFDM` (`8-bit` DICOM `4096 x 3328` pixels per dataset documentation, `CC` and `MLO` views).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Collection (`BMCD`):** `100` pairs of temporally sequential FFDM exams collected `2012–2020` across Cyprus hospitals (Nicosia General Hospital, Limassol General Hospital) under Cyprus National Bioethics Committee approval (`#EEBK EΠ 2020.01.144`): `50` normal/benign (`BI-RADS 1–2`) + `50` suspicious (`BI-RADS 4–5` in recent round), totaling **`400` mammograms** (`2` views x `2` screening rounds x `100` patients) + **`400` PNG expert microcalcification annotation overlays** (annotated by `2` expert radiologists, `97.1%` benign / `98.2%` suspicious interobserver agreement; biopsy confirmation recorded for `32` suspicious cases in `Description.xlsx`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** `BI-RADS 1` (normal), `BI-RADS 2` (benign), `BI-RADS 4–5` (suspicious microcalcifications); `32/50` suspicious cases include biopsy confirmation in `Description.xlsx`, with follow-up recorded for normal cases (`UD08` note: BI-RADS-stratified + partial biopsy subset).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- Leave-one-patient-out (`LOPO`) cross-validation across patients (`patient-disjoint`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Engineered radiomic pipeline (`96` shape, intensity, GLCM features) comparing `9` classical/MLP classifiers (`9-NN`, Decision Tree, Random Forest, MLP, AdaBoost, Bagging, Gradient Boosting, Ensemble Voting) — no ImageNet CNNs or Grad-CAM.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Normalization, border removal, gamma correction, and non-rigid/affine temporal registration + subtraction (`~57x` contrast ratio improvement).

## 8. XAI method(s) and quantitative evaluation

- Classical feature importance (`t-test`) rather than deep XAI attribution; `96`-feature selection documented in Supplemental Table S1.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics

- **Detection:** Temporal subtraction removed `18%` of pre-existing `BI-RADS 2` findings and detected remaining microcalcifications at `94.1%` accuracy (`Table 2`).
- **Classification (`BI-RADS 2` benign vs `BI-RADS 4–5` suspicious):** `90.3%` accuracy and `0.87` AUC with temporal subtraction vs `82.7%` accuracy and `0.81` AUC on recent mammograms alone (`p = 0.003`). Zero calibration (`ECE/Brier`) reported.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Full dataset and spreadsheets openly released under **`CC BY 4.0`** at `https://zenodo.org/records/5036062` (`DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.5036062`, `2.96 GB` total).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- Small cohort size (`100` patients / `400` mammograms), microcalcification-focused selection (`no soft-tissue mass-only cohort`), `8-bit` DICOM representation, and partial biopsy coverage (`32/50` suspicious cases). Ideal as an independent European `CC BY 4.0` external localization/microcalcification benchmark with clean patient IDs and `400` PNG expert annotations.
