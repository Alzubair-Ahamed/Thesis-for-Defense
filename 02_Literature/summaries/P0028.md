## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Divyakant T. Meva, Kalpesh Popat, Hirenkumar Kukadiya, Nikisha Jariwala (p. 1).
- **Title:** *Deep learning-based breast cancer detection with customized ensemble attention* (p. 1).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2026**, Vol. `16`, Article `20022`, pp. `1–16` (published 29 Apr 2026, p. 1).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-026-50563-6` (p. 1).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q3a`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Evaluate 3 custom attention modules (`MSCA`, `SMA`, `HDA`) inside a heterogeneous ResNet-50 + DenseNet-121 + EfficientNet-B3 ensemble (Abstract, p. 1; pp. 4–6).
- **Modality Isolation (`B4_CTX` Quarantine):**
  - **2D Mammography Arm (`CBIS-DDSM`):** Binary Benign vs Malignant classification on SFM **ROI crops (`224x224`)** (p. 8; Table 2, p. 8).
  - **Off-Modality Histopathology Arms (`BreakHis`, `BACH` — excluded from mammography comparison):** BreakHis 40x binary (`1,995` images) and BACH 4-class (`9,600` H&E patches from `400` WSIs, plus 6-pathologist reader study) (`pp. 7–11`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **CBIS-DDSM (2D Mammography — isolated):** `2,620` studies / `224x224` ROIs (`1,318` benign, `1,302` malignant; p. 8; Table 2, p. 8). 5-fold CV fold size: `1,677 Train / 419 Val / 524 Test`. Patient & mass/calc counts: `not reported`. External mammography validation: **None**.
- **BreakHis 40x (Off-modality):** `1,995` images (`625` benign, `1,370` malignant). **BACH (Off-modality):** `400` WSIs -> `9,600` patches (Table 2, p. 8; Table 7, p. 10). Cross-dataset Table 9 (`p. 11`) runs only between BreakHis and BACH.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- CBIS-DDSM uses binary Benign vs Malignant lesion labels (p. 8; biopsy verification & `BENIGN_WITHOUT_CALLBACK` policy `not reported`). Normal class exists **only** on off-modality BACH histopathology.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **CBIS-DDSM Split Level:** `case_level_5fold_cbis` (groups CC/MLO of same breast/case within folds, Table 2, p. 8; does not enforce full patient-ID disjointness across contralateral/multiple cases; ROI cropping inflates metrics). Fixed `seed=42`; zero mammography external validation.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **3-CNN Ensemble (`53.3M` params, `10.6 GFLOPs`, Table 8, p. 11):** ResNet-50 + DenseNet-121 + EfficientNet-B3 (all **ImageNet-1K** initialized) augmented with `MSCA`, `SMA`, and `HDA` attention blocks (`224x224`). Stage 1 (`10` ep frozen `1e-4`) + Stage 2 (`40` ep unfrozen `1e-5`/`1e-4`), AdamW (`wd=1e-4`), Cosine restarts, Focal Loss ($\gamma=2$) + KL diversity ($\beta=0.1$), batch `16` (RTX 2000 ADA).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **CBIS-DDSM Preprocessing (p. 4):** Resize `224x224` + **CLAHE** (`clip=2.0, 8x8` — ablation `-0.9%` acc) + ImageNet normalization.
- **Train-Only Augmentation (p. 4):** H/V flips (`0.5`), rotation (`±15°`), shear (`±0.2`), color jitter, blur, random erasing (`0.2`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **On CBIS-DDSM Mammography:** **None** (`not reported` — zero XAI visualizations or metrics evaluated on mammograms).
- **On Off-Modality Histopathology:** Attention IoU `0.76±0.10` on BreakHis; Grad-CAM + 6-pathologist reader study (`+4.8 pp`, `p=0.012`) on BACH (pp. 10–12). Target layers, faithfulness, and sanity checks: `not reported`.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **CBIS-DDSM 5-fold CV (Tables 3, 5–6, pp. 9–10):** Proposed Ensemble **Acc `93.7 ± 1.0%`**, **F1 `93.4 ± 1.1%`** (vs ResNet-50 `89.7%`, DenseNet-121 `90.8%`, EfficientNet-B3 `91.5%`; paired $t$-test vs Kim 2021 `p=0.031`).
- **CBIS-DDSM Sensitivity, Specificity, Precision, AUC-ROC, PR-AUC, MCC, Calibration (ECE/Brier), DeLong, McNemar:** **`not reported`** (omitted despite metric definitions on p. 7).

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Public URL: `not reported` (states "upon reasonable request", p. 14).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (pp. 12–13):** ROI-level crops, compute cost (`53.3M` / 72h), **no formal calibration/uncertainty**, no causal XAI.
- **Reviewer critique:** CBIS-DDSM evaluation omits AUC-ROC, Sensitivity, Specificity, external validation, patient-ID isolation, and any mammography XAI; Table 3 compares CBIS-DDSM Acc `94.2%` for Li et al. (2021) [42] which is actually a histopathology paper (`BACH`/`BreakHis`); Table 7 BACH F1 (`93.7%`) contradicts Table 3 (`91.0%`).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B4_CTX` (Contextual Quarantine):** Primarily histopathology paper whose secondary `224x224` CBIS-DDSM arm lacks AUC-ROC, Sensitivity/Specificity, XAI evaluation, calibration, and external mammography validation.
