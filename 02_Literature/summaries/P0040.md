## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Joe Logan, Paul J. Kennedy, and Daniel Catchpoole.
- **Title:** *A review of the machine learning datasets in mammography, their adherence to the FAIR principles and the outlook for the future*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Data* (Nature), **2023**, Vol. `10`, Article `595`, pp. `1–11` (Published 8 Sep 2023).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41597-023-02430-6`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Benchmark FAIR-Principles & Label-Quality Audit Across 8 Public/Licensed Mammography Repositories).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Audit the technical format, resolution, label provenance (histology vs radiologist opinion), localization masks, background density availability, and Bishop & Hank quantitative `FAIR` scores across `8` cornerstone mammography datasets (`pp. 1–9`).
- **Modality Covered:** Both digitized screen-film (`DDSM, CBIS-DDSM, MIAS, BCDR-F`) and 2D FFDM (`BCDR-D, INbreast, OPTIMAM, CMMD`) repositories.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Eight Audited Datasets (`Tables 1–2`, p. 3; `Fig. 1`, p. 7; `Fig. 2`, p. 8):**
  - `DDSM` (`1996`, Film, `11,560` imgs / `2,890` cases, LJPG, mixed histology/opinion labels, known mask inaccuracies).
  - `CBIS-DDSM` (`2017`, Film, `1,644` studies in Table 1 / p. 6 lists `753` calc + `891` mass cases, DICOM, histological labels, re-annotated polygons, no background density column cited in Table 2 though `BI-RADS breast_density` exists in CSVs).
  - `MIAS / Mini-MIAS` (`1994`, Film, `322` imgs, PGM `50 / 200` micron, unclear/opinion labeling, coarse elliptical masks, includes density background descriptors).
  - `BCDR-F` (`2012`, Film, `3,703` imgs, 8-bit TIFF) & `BCDR-D` (`2012`, FFDM, `3,612` imgs, 16-bit TIFF) — mixed histology (`276/1,493` earliest segmentations biopsied, rest opinion), `2`-month gated DUA.
  - **`INbreast`** (`2011`, FFDM, `410` imgs, DICOM + OSIRIX XML polygonal masks, **100% histologically verified pathologies** across mass/calc/asymmetry/distortion, highest FAIR interoperability/reusability precision, small volume).
  - **`OPTIMAM`** (`2020`, FFDM, `100,000+` DICOM imgs, `~20k` biopsied cancers + `40k` normals, high commercial fee = `1/5` Accessibility).
  - **`CMMD`** (`2021/2022`, FFDM, `3,728` studies in Logan Table 1 [note VinDr Table 1 cites `1,775` pts / `5,202` imgs], full DICOM, **100% histologically confirmed + CMMD2 molecular subtypes**, **ZERO lesion bounding boxes/segmentations & ZERO breast density metadata**).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Exposes critical cross-dataset interoperability fractures (`p. 4, 8`): original `DDSM` benign/normal labels and `MIAS` labels frequently lack histological confirmation; `BCDR` mixes biopsy and clinical impression (`276/1,493`), whereas `CBIS-DDSM`, `INbreast`, and `CMMD` supply histopathologically proven malignancy (`Table 2`, p. 3; `pp. 5–7`). Also warns about `Film vs Digital` scanner shift (`p. 8`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- Meta-audit noting prior literature (`Levy & Jain`, `Chougrad`, `Agarwal`, `Boudouh`) suffers non-comparable curation, aggressive in-distribution augmentation (`INbreast 410 -> 7,632`), and weak external generalization (`pp. 4–7`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Catalogs severe image format friction (`LJPG` deprecated in `DDSM`, `PGM` in `MIAS`, `TIFF` in `BCDR`, `DICOM` in `CBIS-DDSM/INbreast/OPTIMAM/CMMD`) and warns that resolution disparities & format conversion distort cross-dataset pipelines (`p. 8`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Implications for Quantitative XAI Localization (`Fig. 2`, p. 8):** Explicitly partitions datasets by reusability use case: **Lesion Localization** (`INbreast, CBIS-DDSM, OPTIMAM, BCDR-F`) vs **Whole-Mammogram Classification** (`CMMD, BCDR-D, CBIS-DDSM, DDSM`) vs **Background Density** (`MIAS`). Explains why **`CMMD` cannot natively support XAI `Pointing Game / IoU` evaluation** (no lesion coordinates!) whereas **`INbreast` and `VinDr-Mammo` + `CBIS-DDSM` do** (`RF`, `RG1`, `RG3`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 8`):** Bishop & Hank FAIR quantification depends subjectively on target use case; predates newer 2022–2023 FFDM releases (`VinDr-Mammo`, `RSNA`, `EMBED`, `DMID`).
- **Reviewer critique:** Indispensable companion to `P0039` (`VinDr-Mammo`) when designing a multi-dataset (`≥5`-dataset) Q1 study (`RF`, `RG3`): confirms why mixing digitized film (`CBIS-DDSM/MIAS`) and FFDM (`CMMD/INbreast/VinDr`) induces scanner/domain shift, explains why `CMMD` supports breast-level histopathology AUC/calibration while `INbreast` & `VinDr-Mammo` support bounding-box XAI localization (`PG/Dice/IoU`), and highlights FAIR access links (`p. 9`).
