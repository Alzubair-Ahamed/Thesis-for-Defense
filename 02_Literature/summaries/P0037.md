## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Joshua W. D. Rothwell, Priya Rogers, Nicholas R. Payne, Yuan Huang, Josh D. Kaggie, Sarah E. Hickman, Fleur Kilburn-Toppin, Bahman Kasmai, Arne Juette, and Fiona J. Gilbert.
- **Title:** *Evaluation of a Mammography-Based Deep Learning Model for Breast Cancer Risk Prediction in a Triennial Screening Program*.
- **Venue / Year / Version:** Author manuscript (`25` pp.; cites NHS 2022–23 report accessed May 25, 2025, `p. 17` — unpublished/pre-print manuscript PDF).
- **DOI:** `not reported` (unassigned in manuscript PDF).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Multi-Vendor UK NHS Triennial External Evaluation of `Mirai` With `DeLong` & `Holm-Bonferroni`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Externally evaluate `Mirai` 1–3-year interval cancer (`IC`) risk predictions across two UK NHS triennial screening centers (`Philips` vs `GE`), age quartiles, and BI-RADS breast density subgroups (`pp. 4–8`).
- **Task & Modality:** **Interval cancer (`IC`) risk prediction** following negative screening exams (NOT lesion classification) on **4-view 2D FFDM** (`Philips` & `GE`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Two UK NHS Breast Screening Program Sites (`Jan 2014 – Dec 2016`, women aged `50–70` yrs; `pp. 7, 10, Table 1` pp. 18–19):**
  - `143,596` initial exams -> excluded unavailable (`221`), non-standard views (`2,090`), subsequent exams (`5,037`), baseline screen-detected cancers (`1,102`), next-round cancers (`929`) -> **`134,217` women / baseline negative exams** (mean age `59.1 ± 7.9` yrs), yielding **`524` interval cancers (`ICs`)** within 36–40 months (`73` in 6–12 mo, `216` in 12–24 mo, `235` in 24–36 mo).
  - **Site 1:** `57,446` women (`244` ICs), predominantly **Philips Healthcare (`93.2%`, `53,536`)**, Hologic `4.6%`, GE `1.8%`.
  - **Site 2:** `76,771` women (`280` ICs), **GE HealthCare (`100.0%`, `76,768`)**.
  - **Density Availability (`Table 1`):** `124,186` exams (`10,031` unavailable), assigned via Lunit INSIGHT MMG (`v1.1.7 / v1.1.9`) composite mapped to Volpara distributions (`BI-RADS a: 15.9%, b: 46.3%, c: 28.5%, d: 9.3%`, with `60.8%` of all ICs occurring in dense `c+d` tissue!).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Reference Standard (`p. 8`):** Histopathologically confirmed ICs (`414` `"True"` Normal/Benign-preceding ICs, `104` `"False"` Suspicious-missed ICs, `6` unknown) vs minimum **40-month cancer-free screening follow-up** for negatives.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Strict One-Exam-Per-Woman External Deployment (`pp. 8, 14`):** `Mirai` (`OncoServe_Public`, trained exclusively on US Hologic MGH data) evaluated strictly out-of-sample with exactly one baseline exam per woman (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`Mirai` (`ResNet-18` + Transformer + Risk Factor Predictor + Additive Hazard Layer)** deployed without retraining or fine-tuning (`p. 8`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Processed verbatim inside `OncoServe_Public` Docker container (`p. 8`); no cohort-specific intensity normalization or augmentation applied.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **`NONE` (`0` XAI methods):** Explicitly flags in Limitations (`p. 14`) that `Mirai` is a **`"black box" without a validated attention map`**, making it impossible to know whether it detected missed subtle cancers or risk parenchymal features.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Pooled `IC` Discrimination (`Table 2`, p. 20):** Harrell's `C-index = 0.70 (0.68–0.72)`; Yr 1 AUC `0.72 (0.65–0.78)`, Yr 2 `0.67 (0.64–0.70)`, Yr 3 `0.67 (0.65–0.70)` (no between-year difference, `p ≥ 0.63`).
- **Crucial Cross-Vendor Hardware Domain Shift (`p. 11`, `Table 2`, p. 20):**
  - **GE Site 2 (`C-index 0.74 [0.71–0.76]`, Yr 1–3 AUC `0.78, 0.71, 0.71`)** substantially outperformed **Philips Site 1 (`C-index 0.65 [0.62–0.69]`, Yr 1–3 AUC `0.67, 0.63, 0.62`)**: **statistically significant for Year 2 (`p = 0.017`) and Year 3 (`p < 0.001`)** (and Philips-only vs GE-only Yr 2 `p = 0.016`, Yr 3 `p < 0.001`)! Matches domain-shift hypothesis: Hologic-trained `Mirai` transfers poorly to Philips (`0.62–0.65`).
- **Density Subgroup Comparison (`Table 2` p. 20 vs prose p. 12 — *internal manuscript discrepancy flagged*):**
  - Pooled low density (`BI-RADS a+b`) Yr 3 AUC `0.65 (0.61–0.69)` vs high density (`c+d`) `0.66 (0.63–0.69)` (`DeLong p = 0.68`).
  - *Internal discrepancy:* Prose `p. 12` cites per-density AUCs `a: 0.73 (0.64, 0.82), b: 0.68 (0.63, 0.73), c: 0.66 (0.63, 0.69), d: 0.56 (0.49, 0.64)`, whereas **`Table 2` (p. 20)** lists `Yr 3 AUC` as `a: 0.68 (0.49, 0.77), b: 0.64 (0.59, 0.68), c: 0.65 (0.61, 0.69), d: 0.68 (0.63, 0.73)` (`Table 2` values should take precedence unless supplemental `Table A3` differs).
- **Statistical Rigor (`p. 9`):** Mann-Whitney U, `DeLong`'s test with `10,000` bootstrap samples + **`Holm-Bonferroni` multiplicity correction**, Cox `C-index`, and `2,500`-bootstrap recall yield thresholds (`top 20%` captures `42.4% [222/524]` of ICs, `Table 4`).
- **Calibration:** **Zero quantitative calibration metrics (`ECE`, `Brier score`)** computed, though authors explicitly urge **`system-specific calibration and post-market surveillance`** (`pp. 14–15`).

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Inference ran via public `Mirai` Docker (`https://github.com/yala/OncoServe_Public`, `p. 8`); analysis scripts not linked; data on request (`p. 2`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`pp. 14–15`):** Retrospective design; ethnicity missing `<30%`; synthetic composite `Lunit -> Volpara` density calibration rather than real radiologist BI-RADS; small `BI-RADS a` power; black-box opacity; need for vendor-specific calibration.
- **Reviewer critique:** High-powered (`134k` women) real-world external proof that **mammography system hardware (`Philips` vs `GE` vs `Hologic`) shifts deep learning AUC significantly (`-0.09` C-index, `p < 0.001`)**! Directly strengthens `RG3` (`VinDr/CMMD/INbreast` multi-vendor generalisation) and `RG1` (authors themselves identify lack of validated saliency/attention as a clinical barrier). Also documents the unpublished-manuscript `p. 12 vs Table 2` typo (`0.56 vs 0.68` on `BI-RADS d`).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (Multi-Vendor Hardware Shift & `DeLong` + `Holm-Bonferroni` Methodological Benchmark):** Validates vendor shift (`Philips vs GE`) and `10,000`-bootstrap `DeLong + Holm-Bonferroni` protocol; addresses future `IC` risk rather than lesion diagnosis, and lacks `ECE/Brier` and XAI.
