## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Shu Jiang, Debbie L. Bennett, Simin Chen, Adetunji T. Toriola, and Graham A. Colditz.
- **Title:** *Automated Breast Density Assessment for Full-Field Digital Mammography and Digital Breast Tomosynthesis*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Pages:** *Cancer Prevention Research* (AACR), **2025**, Vol. `18`, No. `1`, pp. `23–29` (Published online 25 Oct 2024).
- **DOI:** `10.1158/1940-6207.CAPR-24-0338`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Multi-Racial External Validation of "For-Presentation" BI-RADS Density Estimation).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Develop and externally validate an automated **BI-RADS (5th edition) breast density estimator** operating directly on archived `"for presentation"` **2D FFDM and synthetic DBT** images across racially diverse cohorts (`pp. 23–25`).
- **Task & Modality:** **4-class & binary BI-RADS breast density classification** (NOT cancer/malignancy diagnosis) using 4-view 2D FFDM (`1,664 x 2,048`) and synthetic 2D DBT (`1,664 x 2,048`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Derivation (`WashU` Joanne Knight Breast Health Cohort, St. Louis; `p. 24`, `Table 1` p. 26):**
  - `9,714` women with `≥2013` FFDM (`BI-RADS 5th ed.`; Hologic `100%`; `26.3%` NH Black, `69.8%` White; `469` breast cancers `4.8%`), split `65%` train / `15%` val / `20%` test (`test n = 1,943` in `Fig. 1`). Plus `4,736` women with DBT (`test n = 945` in `Fig. 2`).
- **External Validation (`EMBED` 20% Public Access; `p. 24`, `Table 1` p. 26):**
  - `22,383` initial -> excluded diagnostic (`2,734`) and incomplete 4-view (`1,289`) = `18,360` women: **`FFDM n = 15,629`** (`42.1%` NH Black, `41.1%` White, `408` cancers `2.6%`; `Hologic 92%`, `GE 6%`, `Fujifilm 2%`) and **`DBT n = 6,586`**.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Density Ground Truth (`pp. 24–25`):** Clinical radiologist-reported 5th-edition **BI-RADS breast density (`A, B, C, D`)** from routine screening reports (no pathology biopsy ground truth evaluated, though cancer follow-up subsets appear in Supplementary `Figs. S2–S3`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Patient-Level Internal + Pure External (`p. 25`):** Random patient-level `65/15/20` split at `WashU` (training women excluded from `n = 1,943` test) and `100%` out-of-institution external validation on `EMBED` (`n = 15,629` FFDM; `Leakage Risk: CLEAN`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **4-View `ResNet-18` Ensemble (`pp. 24–25`):** Each of 4 standard views is encoded independently by `ResNet-18` + global max pooling (`512`-d each -> concatenated `2,048`-d vector). Trained on FFDM (`Adam`, `lr = 1e-4`, `wd = 1e-5`) and transfer-fine-tuned to synthetic DBT (`lr = 1e-5`, frozen ResNet weights, replaced final head). Outputs continuous density score discretized via fixed thresholds `(0, 1.5], (1.501, 2.3], (2.301, 3.3], >3.3`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- `"For presentation"` DICOM converted to **`16-bit PNG`** (`pydicom/PIL`), resized to high resolution **`1,664 x 2,048`**, column-wise de-meaned and normalized using `WashU` training mean/SD applied strictly onto external `EMBED` (`p. 24`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **`NONE` (`0` XAI methods applied or evaluated).**

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Interrater Agreement (`Cohen's κ` & Confusion Matrices, `Figs. 1–2`, pp. 26–27):**
  - **WashU Internal FFDM (`n = 1,943`):** `κ = 0.74 (95% CI: 0.73–0.75)`; binary dense (`C/D`) vs nondense (`A/B`) agreement `84%` nondense / `91%` dense; 4-class diagonal `A: 42% (58% -> B), B: 78%, C: 82%, D: 55% (45% -> C)`.
  - **External `EMBED` FFDM (`n = 15,629`):** **`κ = 0.72 (95% CI: 0.71–0.73)`** (outperforming prior Volpara `0.57` and Quantra `0.46`); nondense `87%`, dense `84%`; 4-class diagonal **`A: 41% (55% -> B), B: 81%, C: 77%, D: 48% (48% -> C)`**.
  - **External `EMBED` DBT (`n = 6,586`):** `κ = 0.71 (0.69–0.73)` (`nondense 90% / dense 80%`, `B 83% / C 74% / A 41% / D 41%`).
- **Calibration & AUC:** **Zero probability calibration metrics (`ECE`, `Brier score`, reliability curve) and zero cancer-detection AUC reported** (authors use `"calibrates well"` purely colloquially to mean concordance with radiologists, `p. 27`).

## 10. Code/weight availability

- `not reported` (WashU images require DUA; patent pending by S. Jiang & G. Colditz, `p. 28`; `EMBED` external test public link listed).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 28`):** Training restricted solely to Hologic equipment; multi-reader ground-truth comparison needed beyond routine clinical labels at Emory.
- **Reviewer critique:** Strong demonstration of high-res (`1,664x2,048`) 16-bit `"for presentation"` DICOM preprocessing + strict mean/SD external transfer across `EMBED`'s `42%` NH Black cohort. However: (1) addresses **BI-RADS density estimation**, not malignancy detection (`out of core diagnostic task`); (2) extreme density classes suffer heavy boundary regression (`BI-RADS D` correct only **`48%`** on FFDM / **`41%`** on DBT, with `48–55%` misclassified as `C`); (3) contains zero XAI and zero probabilistic calibration metrics.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (External Racially Diverse `EMBED` Density Protocol & High-Res 16-Bit Preprocessing Reference):** Directly validates `EMBED` vendor/demographics (`Hologic/GE/Fujifilm`, `42%` Black) and high-res preprocessing, but targets BI-RADS density rather than lesion classification/XAI.
