## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Ajay Kumar, Mudassir Khan, Gunjan Mittal, Izhar Husain, Sambhavi Shukla, Syed Arshad Ali, Anu Sayal, Janhvi Jha, and Riaz Ahmad Ziar.
- **Title:** *Explainable vision transformer framework for breast lesion classification with Grad-CAM support*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Cancer Imaging* (BMC / Springer Nature), **2026**, Vol. `26`, Article `150`, pp. `1–15` (Received 14 Jan 2026 / Accepted 23 Jul 2026 / Published 30 Jul 2026, p. 13).
- **DOI:** `10.1186/s40644-026-01097-7`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q2`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Develop a multi-view (`CC + MLO`) Vision Transformer framework (`ExViT-Breast`) with a unified XAI module and evaluate classification, calibration (`ECE`), faithfulness (`deletion/insertion`), clinical plausibility (`pointing game`), and uncertainty across mammography and cross-modality ultrasound (pp. 1–4).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary/case-level lesion classification on **2D SFM (`CBIS-DDSM`)** and external **2D FFDM (`INbreast`)** (`CC + MLO` fused via cross-view attention, resized to **`224x224`**; p. 7), plus cross-modality evaluation on single-view breast ultrasound (**`BUSI`**; `p. 7–8`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Internal (`CBIS-DDSM`, `Table 1–2`, pp. 4–5):** `1,566` subjects, `10,239` images, `1,644` abnormal cases (`753` calcifications: `414` benign / `339` malignant; `891` masses: `472` benign / `419` malignant; total `886` benign [`53.9%`] / `758` malignant [`46.1%`] cases; `1,189` benign / `1,249` malignant abnormalities).
- **External Mammography (`INbreast`, `Table 1`, pp. 4–5):** `115` cases, `410` 14-bit Siemens MammoNovation FFDM images (XML lesion contours + BI-RADS).
- **Cross-Modality Ultrasound (`BUSI`, `Table 1`, pp. 4–5):** `600` patients, `780` US images (`133` normal, `437` benign, `210` malignant).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance (`pp. 4–5`):** Biopsy-confirmed benign vs malignant labels and ROI outlines/masks on `CBIS-DDSM`; XML radiologist contours/BI-RADS on `INbreast`; ultrasound lesion masks + normal category on `BUSI`.
- **Normal-case Handling:** `CBIS-DDSM` primary cohort comprises benign vs malignant lesion cases (`zero` normal screening exams); `BUSI` includes `133` normal US images (`Table 1`, p. 5; `AUC 0.85` for normal vs `0.91` for malignant tissue reported on `p. 9`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Protocol (`p. 7`):** Patient-level splits enforced throughout:
  - `CBIS-DDSM`: **`90%` training / `10%` validation** stratified random split *(Critical Flag: **no held-out internal CBIS-DDSM test split** separate from validation! Also unclear how paired CC+MLO registration `[Dice > 0.85, p. 8]` operated across official/combined CBIS subsets).*
  - `INbreast`: **`100%` reserved for external validation**.
  - `BUSI`: `70%` train / `15%` val / `15%` test.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Architecture (`ExViT-Breast`, `Fig. 5` & `Sec. Model architecture`, p. 7):** `ViT-Base/16` (`224x224` input, `16x16` patches -> `14x14` token grid, `12` transformer encoder blocks, Hugging Face Transformers), **multi-head cross-attention cross-view fusion** (`CC <-> MLO`, bypassed for `BUSI`), `2`-layer MLP head (`dropout 0.1`, sigmoid).
- **Pretraining & Training (`pp. 7, 10–11`):** Text/Table 3 (`p. 10`) mentions both **`ImageNet-22k`** pretraining (`p. 11`) and **`DINOv2`** initialization (`Table 3 p. 10`). `AdamW` (`lr=3e-5`, cosine annealing, `wd=0.01`, `bs=32` / eff `64`, `100` ep, patience `10`), BCE with label smoothing (`alpha=0.1`). PyTorch `1.13.0`, NVIDIA `RTX A6000` (48GB). Random seeds: `not reported`. Baselines: `ResNet-50`, single-view `ViT`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (`pp. 7–8`):** Bicubic resize to **`224x224`**; zero-mean unit-variance standardization; `INbreast` 14-bit DICOMs linearly rescaled to `[0, 255]` using DICOM window center/width; automated mutual-information affine + `32x32` NCC `CC–MLO` registration (`Dice > 0.85`, p. 8).
- **Train Augmentation (`p. 8`):** Random H/V flips (`p=0.5`), rotation (`±15°`), affine transforms, brightness/contrast (`±20%`), Gaussian noise (`σ=0.01`), elastic deformations (mammography) / speckle noise (US).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **4 XAI Methods (`p. 7, Table 4 p. 9`):** `Grad-CAM`, `Grad-CAM++`, `LayerCAM`, and `Transformer-LRP` (exact layer names: `not reported`).
- **3 Quantitative XAI Metrics (`Table 4`, p. 9; `5,000` bootstrap resamples `p. 8`):**
  1. **Faithfulness (Deletion/Insertion AUC, `Fig. 6`):**
     - `CBIS-DDSM`: **`Transformer-LRP 0.71 ± 0.05`**, `LayerCAM 0.68 ± 0.06`, `Grad-CAM++ 0.65 ± 0.07`, `Grad-CAM 0.62 ± 0.08` (`p < 0.001`).
     - `INbreast`: **`Transformer-LRP 0.70 ± 0.06`**, `Grad-CAM 0.60 ± 0.09`.
     - `BUSI`: **`Transformer-LRP 0.69 ± 0.07`**, `Grad-CAM 0.58 ± 0.10`.
  2. **Clinical Plausibility (Pointing Game %, `Table 4`, p. 9):**
     - `CBIS-DDSM`: **`Transformer-LRP 79.5 ± 9.8%`** (`82.3%` malignant vs `76.8%` benign), `LayerCAM 76.9%`, `Grad-CAM++ 74.2%`, `Grad-CAM 71.4 ± 12.3%` (`p=0.002`).
     - `INbreast`: **`Transformer-LRP 80.1 ± 10.2%`**, `Grad-CAM 69.8 ± 11.9%`.
     - `BUSI`: **`Transformer-LRP 78.3 ± 9.9%`**, `Grad-CAM 67.9 ± 12.5%`.
  3. **Ensemble Uncertainty ($\times 10^{-2}$, lower is better):**
     - `CBIS-DDSM`: **`Transformer-LRP 2.65 ± 0.89`** vs `Grad-CAM 3.45 ± 1.12` (`p < 0.001`, correlated with correctness `r=-0.68`).
- **Sanity Checks & Density XAI Stratification:** Zero Adebayo weight/data randomization checks; error text notes `62%` of misclassifications occurred in BI-RADS C/D dense tissue (`p. 10`), **but XAI faithfulness, plausibility, and `ECE` are NOT stratified by breast density**.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Classification & Calibration (`Table 3`, p. 8):**
  - **`CBIS-DDSM` (90/10 val):** **AUC `0.94` (`0.92–0.96`)**, Sens@1FP/exam `90.3%`, Spec `88.5%`, F1 `0.89`, **`ECE 0.042`** (vs `ResNet-50` `0.89`, `p<0.001`; single-view `ViT` `0.91`, `p=0.003`).
  - **`INbreast` (External):** **AUC `0.91` (`0.88–0.94`)** (`ΔAUC=-0.03, p=0.09` DeLong), Sens@1FP/exam `88.7%`, Spec `86.2%`, F1 `0.87`, **`ECE 0.056`**.
  - **`BUSI` (Cross-Modality):** **AUC `0.88` (`0.84–0.92`)**, Sens `85.1%`, Spec `83.4%`, F1 `0.84`, **`ECE 0.068`**.
- **Stats Used (`p. 8`):** DeLong test, McNemar test, `5,000`-bootstrap CIs, Holm-Bonferroni (`α=0.05`). *(`Brier score` listed in Evaluation Framework p. 8, yet numeric Brier scores omitted from Table 3!).*

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`pp. 7, 12–13`):** `224x224` input reduces microcalcification visibility (`~2%` area), zero radiologist reader study, high compute burden, needs multi-institutional validation.
- **Reviewer critique:** Structurally aligns very closely with multi-view ViT + quantitative XAI + ECE, **EXCEPT carries severe methodological & internal-consistency concerns**: (1) `CBIS-DDSM` uses only a **`90%` train / `10%` validation split with no separate internal test set** (`p. 7`); (2) claims `72 h` on RTX A6000 for 10,239 images yet `36 h` for just 780 BUSI images and `8 h` for 410 INbreast inference/training (`p. 10, 12`); (3) lists `Brier score` in Methods (`p. 8`) yet omits all numeric Brier values; (4) `Table 3` (`p. 10`) has duplicate `"Table 3"` numbering (`p. 8` vs `p. 10`), reports `w/o Transformer-LRP` altering `Faithfulness` (`0.71->0.65`) even though LRP is a post-hoc explainer; (5) **`224x224` downsampling** fails microcalcifications (`confirmed by Kag P0016`); (6) **zero Adebayo randomization checks**, **zero density-stratified XAI/ECE metrics**, and **no public code** despite claiming `"made available"`. Must be cited carefully with explicit methodological audit flags!
