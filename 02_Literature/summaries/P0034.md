- **Title:** *Cross-Sensor and Cross-Population Generalization of Deep Learning Models for Digital Mammography: A Controlled Four-Country Benchmark of Five Backbone Architectures with Statistical Significance Testing*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Sensors* (MDPI), **2026**, Vol. `26`, Article `3911`, pp. `1–26`.

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Test in-distribution discrimination, density-aware focal loss (`DAF`), BI-RADS density fairness, probability calibration (`ECE`/`Brier`), and zero-shot cross-sensor/cross-population external transfer across 5 CNN/ViT/foundation backbones (`pp. 1–7`).
- **Modality:** **Full-image 2D mammography** (`518 x 518`) spanning digitized film (`CBIS-DDSM`, `MIAS`), FFDM (`CMMD`), and mixed digital (`DMID`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Four-Country Benchmark (`Table 1`, pp. 6–7):**
  - **Source Training / Internal Test:** `CBIS-DDSM` (USA digitized SFM; held-out patient-disjoint validation split `n = 714`, `40%` malignant prevalence, stratified across BI-RADS `D1 = 100`, `D2 = 264`, `D3 = 210`, `D4 = 140`).
  - **Zero-Shot External Target 1:** `CMMD` (China FFDM; `n = 1,032`, `70%` positive).
  - **Zero-Shot External Target 2:** `DMID` (India mixed digital; `n = 509`, `23%` positive).
  - **Zero-Shot External Target 3:** `MIAS` (UK digitized film; `n = 322`, `16%` positive).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Pathology/biopsy labels on `CBIS-DDSM` and `CMMD`; expert radiologist/pathology benign vs malignant vs normal labels on `DMID` and `MIAS`.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Strict Patient-Disjoint Split (`p. 6`):** Explicit patient-level separation inside `CBIS-DDSM` and true **zero-shot cross-dataset external testing** across `CMMD`, `DMID`, and `MIAS` (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Five Backbones (`Tables 2–4`):**
  1. `ResNet-50` (ImageNet CNN)
  2. `Mammo-CLIP (EfficientNet-B5)` (mammography VLM backbone)
  3. `Mammo-FM` (mammography foundation model)
  4. `DINOv2-B14` (self-supervised ViT)
  5. `Rad-DINO` (biomedical SSL ViT)
- Trained with `3` random seeds (`42, 7, 123`), `AdamW`, `10` epochs (`1e-4` head / `1e-5` backbone). Also tests **Density-Aware Focal Loss (`DAF`)** (`Tables 3–4`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Standardized `518 x 518` bilinear resizing; temperature scaling (`T = 1.38–2.11`) tested for post-hoc external recalibration.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **`NONE` (`0` XAI methods evaluated):** Complete absence of `Grad-CAM`, `IG`, `LRP`, attention rollout, quantitative localization (`PG`/`Dice`/`IoU`), perturbation faithfulness, or `Adebayo` sanity checks.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **In-Distribution `CBIS-DDSM` (`Table 2`, p. 10):**
  - **`ResNet-50` AUC `0.867 ± 0.006`** (`ECE 0.055`, `Brier 0.147`, `Sens@95Spec 0.465`) significantly outperformed `Mammo-CLIP B5` (`0.847`, `ECE 0.105`, BH-FDR `p_adj = 0.047`), `Mammo-FM` (`0.846`, `ECE 0.096`, `p_adj = 0.031`), `DINOv2-B14` (`0.813`, `p_adj < 0.001`), and `Rad-DINO` (`0.703`, `p_adj < 0.001`).
  - **Density Stratification & `DAF` Null Result (`Tables 3–4`):** Density-aware focal loss (`DAF`) monotonically **degraded overall AUC (`0.836 -> 0.806`) and doubled calibration error (`ECE 0.104–0.116`)**. On extremely dense breasts (`BI-RADS D4, n = 140`), `Mammo-FM` led directionally (`0.870` vs `ResNet-50 0.842`, overlapping CIs).
- **Zero-Shot External Collapse (`Tables 5–7`, pp. 13–17):**
  - Every model dropped **`-0.165` to `-0.320` AUC** externally (`ResNet-50`: `CMMD 0.568`, `DMID 0.547`, `MIAS 0.630`; `Sens@95Spec 0.107–0.224`).
  - **Calibration Explosion:** `ECE` surged to **`0.186–0.446`** (`Mammo-CLIP` on `CMMD`: `0.403`). Standard temperature scaling (`T = 1.38–2.11`) reduced external `ECE` by **only `-0.009` to `-0.049`**, leaving severe residual miscalibration under prevalence/sensor shift (`CMMD 70%` vs `CBIS 40%`).
- **Statistical Rigor:** Paired DeLong test + `5,000` patient-cluster bootstrap + Stouffer per-seed meta-analysis + Benjamini-Hochberg (`BH-FDR`) correction (`p. 9`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated:** Single digitized-film source (`CBIS-DDSM`) drives domain shift; external targets (`CMMD`, `DMID`, `MIAS`) lack BI-RADS density tags; temperature scaling cannot fix prevalence shift without target intercept recalibration.
- **Reviewer critique:** Flagship empirical anchor for `RG2`, `RG3`, and `RG4`: proves foundation models do not automatically solve calibration/shift (`ECE -> 0.446`, AUC `-0.165` to `-0.320`), temperature scaling barely helps (`<= -0.049`), and `DAF` hurts calibration. Directly motivates training on multi-vendor FFDM (`VinDr-Mammo`, `CMMD`, `INbreast`) AND auditing whether **XAI faithfulness/localization collapses alongside calibration under domain shift (`zero XAI` in `P0034`)**!

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study
