## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Ling Liao, Eva M. Aagaard (p. 1).
- **Title:** *An open codebase for enhancing transparency in deep learning-based breast cancer diagnosis utilizing CBIS-DDSM data* (p. 1).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2024**, Vol. `14`, Article `27318`, pp. `1–9` (published 9 Nov 2024, p. 1).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-024-78648-0` (p. 1).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q3a`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Provide an end-to-end transparent CBIS-DDSM mass preprocessing codebase and quantify input crop resolution impact (`224x224` vs `448x448`; `299x299` vs `599x599`) (pp. 1–2, 5).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Benign vs Malignant **mass** classification on 2D **digitized SFM (`CBIS-DDSM`)** using **zero-masked, centroid-cropped ROI patches** (background pixels outside ROI mask zeroed out prior to cropping, Methods, p. 6).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **CBIS-DDSM Mass Subset Only (calcifications excluded, pp. 1–2, 4, 6–7; Fig. 1B–C, p. 2):** `892` patients, `1,592` full images, `1,696` ROIs (`915` benign, `781` malignant).
- **Splits (`80/10/10`, pp. 1, 4, 7; Table 1, p. 3):** Train `~1,356` unaugmented (`6,780` 5x-augmented train copies), Val `~170`, Test `170` unaugmented. External validation: None (`not reported`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Pathology labels from `Mass-Training/Test-Description` CSVs; `BENIGN` & `BENIGN_WITHOUT_CALLBACK` mapped to `0`, `MALIGNANT` to `1` (pp. 6–7). Normal (BI-RADS 1): absent.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`image_roi_level_80_10_10`**. Randomly splits `1,696` ROIs in `all.csv` ignoring official CBIS-DDSM splits and lacking patient grouping (`892` patients -> `1,696` ROIs causes high patient/view leakage risk; unseeded per-model runs produce varying test class totals `89/81`, `95/75`, `99/71`, `86/84` in Table 1, p. 3). Augmentation is restricted strictly to training split (`pp. 2, 4, 7`). External validation: None.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **ImageNet-Pretrained CNNs (`224x224` & `448x448` ResNet-50; `299x299` & `598x598`/`599x599` Xception, Table 1 p. 3, Fig. 3 p. 4, p. 7):** Custom 1->3 channel BN+LeakyReLU frontend + frozen/fine-tuned backbone + `GAP -> Linear(2048,512) -> ReLU -> Dropout(0.3) -> Sigmoid`. Adam (`lr` `1e-3` to `1e-6`), BCE, batch `32`, early stopping patience `3`, NVIDIA A100 (80 GB). Seeds: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (pp. 1–3, 6–7):** Preserves **16-bit PNG** full mammograms (`[0,1]` via `/65535.0`) and 8-bit ROIs; masks non-ROI tissue to `0`, centroid-crops to `598x598`, manually removes **white film-border artifacts in `18` ROIs** (`Fig. 2C/F`, p. 3), and intentionally omits CLAHE/denoising (`pp. 2, 5–6`).
- **Train-Only 5x Augmentation (`ImageDataGenerator`, pp. 1, 6–7):** Rotation `±20°`, shift `±10%`, shear `±20°`, zoom `80%–120%`, H-flip.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **None** (`not reported` / not performed).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Test Performance ($n=170$, Table 1, p. 3):**
  - ResNet-50 (`448x448`): **AUC `0.7421` (`[0.6651, 0.8147]`)**, Acc `0.6824` (`[0.6118, 0.7529]`), Prec `0.6800`, Rec `0.6296`, F1 `0.6538`.
  - ResNet-50 (`224x224`): AUC `0.7269` (`[0.6500, 0.7965]`), Acc `0.6765`.
  - Xception (`599x599`): AUC `0.6820` (`[0.6006, 0.7642]`), Acc `0.6824`.
  - Xception (`299x299`): AUC `0.7024` (`[0.6176, 0.7764]`), Acc `0.6059`.
- **ROI Area Confounder Test (`wilcox.test`, pp. 2, 6):** Malignant mass crops have significantly larger lesion area % (`18.6%`, IQR `11.6–31.2%`) than benign (`14.5%`, IQR `9.2–22.2%`), **`P < 0.001`**.
- **Calibration (ECE/Brier), DeLong/McNemar, PR-AUC:** `not reported`.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- **Public GitHub Codebase:** `https://github.com/lingliao/Transparency-in-CABCDTD/tree/main` (p. 8). Trained weights URL: `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (pp. 5–6):** 18 noisy/white-edge CBIS-DDSM ROIs; lack of radiologist ROI validation; mass-only scope.
- **Reviewer critique:** Crucial data-curation benchmark (documents 16-bit normalization, `BENIGN_WITHOUT_CALLBACK` mapping, and 18 white-edge ROI artifacts), but zeroing background tissue leaks lesion-size cues (`P<0.001`), random ROI split leaks patients across folds, calcifications are omitted, and XAI/calibration/external validation are absent.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B2` (Data-Curation & Preprocessing Benchmark Reference):** Essential preprocessing/artifact audit comparator on CBIS-DDSM; not directly comparable to whole-mammogram patient-disjoint XAI benchmarks due to zero-masked ROI crops, random ROI splitting, mass-only subset, and zero XAI.
