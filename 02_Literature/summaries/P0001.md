## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Build and evaluate integrated end-to-end segmentation, geometric staging, classification, and Grad-CAM CAD pipelines across both mammography and ultrasound (Sec. 1–2, pp. 1933–1935).
- **Tasks:** U-Net segmentation -> contour ROI extraction & coverage-percentage staging (`T1–T4` on CBIS-DDSM; `Stage I–IV` on BUSI) -> classification -> Grad-CAM overlays -> Django prototype (Abstract, p. 1932; Sec. 3.4–3.9, pp. 1936–1940).
- **Modality Isolation (`B4_CTX` Quarantine):**
  - **2D Mammography Arm (CBIS-DDSM):** Digitized SFM; **whole or loosely localized single-view mammogram** classification resized to `224x224x3` (Sec. 3.6, p. 1938; Sec. 5, p. 1947).
  - **Off-Modality Ultrasound Arm (BUSI — excluded from direct mammography synthesis):** B-mode ultrasound segmented at `256x256`, then **tightly cropped `128x128x3` ROI** 3-class classification (Sec. 3.6, p. 1938; Tables 5–6, pp. 1938–1939) + `6` independent clinical US cases in Django pilot (Sec. 4.6, Table 12, pp. 1945–1946).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **CBIS-DDSM (2D Mammography):** Kaggle curated subset of DDSM (Table 1, p. 1935; URL/version `not reported`). Internal count conflicts:
  - Sec. 3.1 & Table 1 (p. 1935): `882` images and `882` masks.
  - Sec. 3.3 (p. 1935): States `"704 benign vs. 362 malignant before split"` (`1,066` sum; conflates total raw train `704` with benign count).
  - Table 2 (p. 1936, `80/20` split): Benign Train `415` / Test `104` (sum `519`); Malignant Train `289` -> balanced `415 (+126 aug)` / Test `73` (sum `362`). Table 2 total raw = `881` (`704` Train / `177` Test).
  - Sec. 4.2.1 (p. 1940) & Fig. 3 (p. 1941): Evaluate classifiers on **`354` test images (`208` benign, `146` malignant)** — double Table 2's `177` without explanation.
  - Validation counts & patient/exam counts: `not reported`.
- **BUSI (Off-Modality Ultrasound):** Kaggle Al-Dhabyani mirror, `780` images (`437` benign, `210` malignant, `133` normal; Table 1, p. 1935); `80/20` test = `157` (`88` benign, `42` malignant, `27` normal; Table 2, p. 1936; Table 11, p. 1942); plus `6` clinical US test images (Table 12, pp. 1945–1946).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **CBIS-DDSM:** Pathology tags parsed from accompanying JSON annotations (`benign` vs `malignant`; Sec. 3.1, p. 1935). Normal (BI-RADS 1) absent/excluded. Rule-based coverage staging (`T1–T4`, Table 4, pp. 1937–1938) explicitly does not match clinical TNM or BI-RADS (Sec. 3.5, p. 1937; Sec. 6, p. 1947).
- **BUSI:** 3-class (`benign`, `malignant`, `normal [no lesion]`; Sec. 3.1, p. 1935).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** `image_level_80_20` (stratified per class before training-only class balancing; Sec. 3.3, p. 1935; Table 2, p. 1936). Zero patient-ID or multi-view grouping verified (`not reported`; risks same-patient leakage on CBIS-DDSM). Validation split definition `not reported`.
- **External Validation:** None on CBIS-DDSM (Sec. 6, p. 1947).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Segmentation U-Nets (scratch, `256x256`, Sec. 3.4, Table 3, pp. 1936–1937):** CBIS-DDSM (`256x256x1`, `64->1024`, Focal Tversky loss, Adam `1e-4`, batch `4`, 40 ep); BUSI (`256x256`, `32->512`, Dice loss, Adam `1e-4`, batch `4`, 50 ep).
- **CBIS-DDSM Classifiers (`224x224x3`, batch `16`, BCE with class weights, Tables 5–6, pp. 1938–1939):**
  - ResNet50 (ImageNet): 10 ep frozen warm-up (`lr=1e-3`) + 20 ep top ~30% unfrozen (`lr=1e-5`); `GAP -> Dropout(0.5) -> Dense(256, ReLU) -> Dropout(0.4) -> Dense(1, Sigmoid)`.
  - VGG16 (ImageNet, 30 ep, lr `not reported`) and Custom CNN (scratch, 30 ep, lr `not reported`).
- **BUSI Classifiers (`128x128x3`, 50 ep, sparse categorical CE):** ResNet50 (ImageNet), EfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet), Custom CNN (scratch). Random seeds: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Bilateral filter denoising (params `not reported`), resize `256x256`, mask binarization `{0,255}` (Sec. 3.2, p. 1935). Bit-depth, DICOM handling, pectoral/artifact removal, normalization, and CLAHE: `not reported` (full breasts retained, Figs. 4a–5, pp. 1943–1944).
- **Augmentation (train-only post-split, Sec. 3.3, pp. 1935–1936):** Albumentations offline balancing + on-the-fly flips, vertical flips, rotations, zoom, brightness/contrast, elastic deformation (exact params `not reported`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method:** Grad-CAM applied to CBIS-DDSM ResNet50 & VGG16 (Sec. 3.8, p. 1939; Sec. 4.5, p. 1944).
- **Target Layer:** `conv5_block3_out` for CBIS-DDSM ResNet50 (Sec. 3.8, p. 1939); VGG16 & BUSI exact layer names `not reported`.
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`3` visual overlays on Fig. 6, p. 1944). Zero deletion/insertion/ROAD faithfulness, zero Adebayo sanity checks, zero Grad-CAM ROI localization metrics (explicitly noted as absent in Sec. 6, p. 1948), and zero reader studies. Authors observe diffuse, poorly localized Grad-CAM activation across breast tissue (Sec. 4.5, p. 1944).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **CBIS-DDSM Segmentation (Table 7, p. 1940):** Train Dice `0.264`, Val Dice `0.247`, IoU `not reported`. (BUSI Val Dice `0.462`, Val IoU `0.337`).
- **CBIS-DDSM Classification (`N=354` test, Table 8, p. 1941; Sec. 4.2.1, p. 1940):**
  - ResNet50: Train Acc `64.56%`, Test Acc `61.86%`, Prec `55.7%`, **Recall (Malignant) `37.0%`**, Spec `79.3%`, F1 `60.0%`, `AUC (val) 0.647`. *(Contradiction: Fig. 3 p. 1941 ResNet50 CM shows `123 TN, 85 FP, 66 FN, 80 TP` -> `57.3%` Acc, `54.8%` Recall.)*
  - VGG16: Test Acc `56.00%`, Prec `47.1%`, Recall `56.8%`, Spec `55.3%`, F1 `56.0%`, `AUC (val) 0.593`.
  - Custom CNN: Test Acc `58.19%`, Prec `48.7%`, Recall `26.0%`, Spec `80.8%`, F1 `54.8%`, `AUC (val) 0.503`.
- **BUSI Classification (`N=157` test, Tables 10–11, pp. 1942–1943):** ResNet50 Test Acc `84.07%` (F1 `84%`), EfficientNet-B0 `79.62%` (F1 `80%`), Custom CNN `69.43%` (F1 `70%`); clinical pilot `5/6` (`83%`, Table 12, p. 1945).
- **Calibration / 95% CIs / Statistical Tests (DeLong/McNemar):** `not reported` (ECE noted as future work, Sec. 7, p. 1949).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (Sec. 6, pp. 1947–1948):** Single dataset per modality without external validation; low CBIS-DDSM U-Net Dice (`0.247`) and malignant recall (`37%`); qualitative-only Grad-CAM; modality/resolution/task confounding between BUSI and CBIS-DDSM.
- **Reviewer critique:** (1) Severe internal inconsistencies: missing `Fig. 1` and `Table 9`, missing graphic under `Fig. 2`, CBIS-DDSM totals `882` vs `881` vs `1,066`, test split doubling `177` (Table 2) -> `354` (Fig. 3), `Fig. 3` CM vs `Table 8` mismatch, and `AUC (val)` reported instead of test AUC; (2) image-level split without patient grouping; (3) `224x224` resizing loses microcalcification detail; (4) no calibration, CIs, or statistical tests.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B4_CTX` (Contextual Quarantine):** Mixed ultrasound (`BUSI`) + mammography (`CBIS-DDSM`) design, coupled with missing tables/figures, count contradictions, and ungrouped splits, precludes using `P0001` in direct numerical benchmarking tables; retained solely as contextual evidence showing weak baseline CBIS-DDSM performance (`37%` recall) and diffuse Grad-CAM maps at `224x224`.
