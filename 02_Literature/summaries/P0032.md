## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Fuse a Swin Transformer branch with a dual-attention multi-scale CNN branch (`Swin-DAMFN`) plus cDCGAN augmentation for multi-class mammogram classification (`pp. 7–8, 13–19`).
- **Task & Modality:** 2D **digitized SFM (`CBIS-DDSM`, `MIAS` — erroneously called "Digital mammogram" in Table 2, p. 9)**; **4-class** CBIS-DDSM (`Benign/Malignant Calc/Mass`) and **3-class** MIAS (`Benign/Malignant/Normal`), `224x224` input (ambiguous whole-vs-cropped selection on CBIS-DDSM `p. 8–9`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **CBIS-DDSM (p. 8; Table 2 p. 9; Table 3 p. 11):** `1,459` original (`398` BC, `417` BM, `300` MC, `344` MM) -> augmented to `6,600`.
- **MIAS v1.21 (p. 8; Table 2 p. 9; Table 3 p. 11):** `322` original (`63` Benign, `52` Malignant, `207` Normal) -> augmented to `1,200`. Exact split/patient counts: `not reported`. External validation: **None** (`p. 28`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Dataset-supplied 4-class lesion labels (`CBIS-DDSM`) and 3-class including `Normal` (`207` MIAS scans, p. 8); biopsy confirmation protocol `not reported`.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level (`80/10/10`, pp. 8, 19–20):** Patient-level on CBIS-DDSM; manual laterality/view-checked image split on MIAS (lacks patient IDs). **Severe text contradiction on p. 12:** Claims *"the training phase utilized only synthetic samples, and the testing and validation subsets did not contain any actual data"*, contradicting `pp. 11, 20` claim of training-only augmentation!

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`Swin-DAMFN` (`35.2M` params, `6.8 GFLOPs`, `224x224x3`, Table 4 p. 18, Table 10 p. 23):** Swin branch + DAMFN (CBAM + Triplet Attention). **CNN baselines (ResNet50, DenseNet121, VGG19, InceptionV3, Xception) trained from scratch** (`p. 26`), NOT ImageNet TL; Swin-DAMFN/DaViT/MobileViT pretraining `not reported`. Hyperparams conflict across Alg. 2 (`lr=1e-3, bs=32, ep=50`), Table 6 (`lr=1e-4, bs=16, ep=100`), and Table 13 (`lr=1e-3`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Grayscale -> morph label/artifact removal -> `[0,1]` min-max -> adaptive median -> **CLAHE** (`clip = alpha*(N_AVG/L)`, tile size `not reported`, p. 10) -> `224x224`; cDCGAN (`FID 18.7` p. 12 vs `16.8` p. 22) + photometric/geom augmentation (`pp. 11–12, 22`).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **5-Seed `Swin-DAMFN` (Table 7 p. 21; p. 24; Table 11–12 pp. 26–27):** CBIS-DDSM 4-class Acc `99.30 ± 0.12%`, AUC `0.993` (Fig. 13 p. 25); MIAS 3-class Acc `98.75 ± 0.10%`, AUC `0.987` (Fig. 14 p. 25). **Contradiction:** Validation curves in `Figs. 11–12` (p. 24) plateau near `~0.90–0.92`, not `99%`!
- **Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test (`Table 12`, p. 27):** $p < 0.01$ vs DaViT (`97.20%`) and MobileViT (`96.40%`) (exact p-values omitted). DeLong, McNemar, Calibration (ECE/Brier), Confusion Matrices: `not reported`.
