## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Uçman Ergün, Tuğçe Çoban, and İsmail Kayadibi (p. 1).
- **Title:** *BCECNN: an explainable deep ensemble architecture for accurate diagnosis of breast cancer* (p. 1).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making*, **2025**, Vol. `25`, Article `374`, pp. `1–22` (Published 13 Oct 2025, pp. 1, 20).
- **DOI:** `10.1186/s12911-025-03186-2` (p. 1).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q1a`, `GS-Q1b`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Test whether majority-voting CNN ensembles (`BCECNN`) improve small-dataset mammography classification and generate clinically aligned Grad-CAM/LIME visualizations (Abstract, p. 1; pp. 2–5).
- **Tasks (`AISSLab` sub-datasets `v1–v5`, Fig. 1, p. 5; Table 2, p. 7):** `v1` (Normal vs Abnormal), `v2` (Malignant vs Normal), `v3` (Benign vs Malignant), `v4` (3-class Benign/Malignant/Normal), `v5` (4-class BI-RADS 2/3/4/5).
- **Modality:** **Pure 2D mammography** (CC and MLO whole-mammogram images from a GE Senographe 800T system in Yemen; zero off-modality experimental arms; p. 6; Fig. 3, p. 7).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **AISSLab MDCMI-BC (Yemen, Mendeley `10.17632/zp8yfhvndv.1`, Ref [55]):** **`266` raw images** (Normal `100`, BI-RADS 2 Benign `18`, BI-RADS 3 Benign `48`, BI-RADS 4 Malignant `54`, BI-RADS 5 Malignant `46`; Table 1, p. 6). Derived sub-datasets released on Kaggle/Mendeley (`10.17632/rg8wv3csxg`, p. 20).
- **90/10 Raw vs 4x Augmented Splits (Table 2, p. 7):** `v1` `266->1,064` (Test `27->106`); `v2` `200->800` (Test `20->80`); `v3` Benign vs Malignant `166->664` (Test `17->66`); `v4` `266->1,064` (Test `27->106`); `v5` `166->664` (Test `17->66`). Patient, exam, and validation split counts: `not reported`.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Radiologist-assigned **BI-RADS categories** (2–3 = Benign, 4–5 = Malignant; p. 6; Table 1, p. 6); biopsy confirmation `not reported`.
- **Normal Handling:** Explicitly included (`100` Normal images without BI-RADS) in `v1`/`v2`/`v4` and excluded in `v3`/`v5` (Table 2, p. 7).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`image_level_90_10_preaug_leakage`**. Both raw and 4x pre-augmented corpora were split 90/10 at the image level (`Table 2`, p. 7; p. 6), directly leaking augmented variants of raw training scans into test sets (`Test` expanded 4x: `17->66`, `20->80`). Zero patient or CC/MLO view grouping (`not reported`).
- **External Validation:** None (single-center AISSLab only, p. 20).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Backbones (ImageNet-pretrained, convolutional layers frozen, only FC layers trained; Table 3, p. 8; p. 9):** AlexNet (`224x224`), ResNet-18 (`224x224`), VGG16 (`224x224`), EfficientNetB0 (`224x224`), XceptionNet (`299x299`).
- **Ensembles (majority voting, pp. 9–10; Table 5, p. 9):** Best triple ensemble (`TECNN` / `BCECNN`) = **AlexNet + VGG16 + EfficientNetB0**; plus 5-model `QECNN`. Hyperparams (Table 7, p. 12): Adam, `lr=1e-4`, batch `32`, dropout `0.5`, `50`/`100` epochs, CE/NLL loss; RTX 4090 GPU. Random seeds: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- `"standard image normalization and resizing"` (exact formula `not reported`, p. 8). Bit-depth, CLAHE, and artifact/pectoral removal: `not reported` / absent (Fig. 3 p. 7 retains `R MLO`/`L CC`/`AX` labels, and Normal scans show heavy purple/cyan false-color tint vs grayscale Benign/Malignant!).
- **Augmentation (pre-split 4x, Table 4, p. 8):** Zoom/refraction crop (`<=5%`), rotation (`±30°`), grayscale conversion (`15%`), saturation (`±25%`), brightness (`±15%`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Methods:** **Grad-CAM**, **LIME**, **LIME-Mask** on AlexNet, VGG16, EfficientNetB0 (p. 10, p. 17; Fig. 8, p. 18).
- **Target Layer:** Generic `"last convolutional layers"` (exact names `not reported`, p. 10).
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`visual_anecdotes_only` + informal unblinded review by 1 radiologist (35 yrs exp.)** qualitatively preferring EfficientNetB0/VGG16 over AlexNet (p. 17; 4 examples in Fig. 8, p. 18 showing AlexNet border shortcut focus). Zero deletion/insertion/ROAD faithfulness, zero Adebayo sanity checks, zero ROI-mask overlap, and zero blinded rubric.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Unaugmented Single CNNs (Table 8, p. 15):** `v3` Benign vs Malignant (`N=17`): EfficientNetB0 Acc `76.47%` (AUC `0.4697`), VGG16 Acc `58.82%` (`AUC 0.7273`); `v5` BI-RADS 2–5 (`N=17`): EfficientNetB0 `35.29%`, VGG16 `11.76%`; `v2` Malignant vs Normal (`N=20`): AlexNet `85.00%` (`AUC 0.9120`).
- **Pre-Split Augmented Ensembles (`TECNN`: AlexNet+VGG16+EfficientNetB0, Table 10, p. 17; Table 11, p. 18):** Headline `v2` Malignant vs Normal Acc **`98.75%`** (Abstract p. 1); `v1` `91.59%`/`92.52%`; `v3` Benign vs Malignant **`91.04%`**; `v4` `87.85%`/`90.65%`; `v5` `86.57%`; mean `91.53%` (note Table 10 also shows `AlexNet+VGG16+XceptionNet` `94.39%` on `v1` and `92.54%` on `v3`).
- **McNemar Test (`TECNN` vs `QECNN`, Table 12, p. 18, `N=80`):** $n_{11}=70, n_{01}=1, n_{10}=9, n_{00}=0$, $\chi^2=5.33$, $p=0.023$ (invalidated by pre-split augmentation dependence).
- **Calibration (ECE/Brier) & 95% CIs & DeLong:** `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (pp. 19–20):** Small cohort size (`266` images), low sample diversity, non-comparable Table 13 baselines, absence of ViTs.
- **Reviewer critique:** (1) Severe 4x pre-split augmentation leakage + absent patient/view grouping (`v5` `11.76%–35.29%` -> `88.06%` post-aug); (2) Normal-class purple/cyan color shortcut artifact (Fig. 3, p. 7) driving `v2` `98.75%`; (3) BI-RADS 4 labeled malignant without biopsy; (4) Table 2 test counts (`106`, `66`) mismatch Table 11 CM sums (`107`, `67`, `v5` `72`); (5) frozen conv backbones, qualitative-only XAI (`Fig. 8` AlexNet border focus), zero calibration/CIs.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Flagged for Pre-Split Leakage & Color Confounding):** Directly on-scope 2D mammography CNN+Grad-CAM/LIME+McNemar study, quarantined in quantitative comparisons due to pre-split augmentation leakage and AISSLab Normal false-color artifact.
