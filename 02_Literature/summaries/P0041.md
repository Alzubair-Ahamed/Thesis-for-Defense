## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Chen Chen, Enyu Wang, Vicky Yang Wang, Xiayi Chen, Bojian Feng, Ruxuan Yan, Lingying Zhu, and Dong Xu.
- **Title:** *Deep learning prediction of mammographic breast density using screening data*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2025**, Vol. `15`, Article `11602`, pp. `1–10` (Published 4 Apr 2025).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-025-95275-5`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Asian Dense-Cohort Screening BI-RADS Classification With External RSNA Test & Holm-Bonferroni).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Benchmark 4 ImageNet CNNs for 4-class ACR BI-RADS breast density classification on a large Chinese screening FFDM cohort, compare against 3 radiologists using `Holm-Bonferroni` testing, and validate externally on RSNA screening cases (`pp. 1–6`).
- **Task & Modality:** **4-class ACR BI-RADS density classification** (`A, B, C, D`, NOT malignancy/lesion diagnosis) on **2D FFDM (`224 x 224`)**.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Internal (`Taizhou Cancer Hospital`, Zhejiang, China, 2015–2020; `pp. 2–4`, `Table 1` p. 4):**
  - `57,282` Hologic FFDM images from **`9,621` women** (no implants; young mean age **`46.6 ± 7.8` yrs**, `~69%` under 50 -> **`89.1%` dense** [`BI-RADS A: 0.6%, B: 10.3%, C: 46.1%, D: 43.0%`], mirroring `VinDr-Mammo`'s dense Asian epidemiology!).
  - Partitioned `7:2:1` (`Train: 38,551 imgs / 6,734 pts`, `Val: 12,316 imgs / 1,924 pts`, `Test: 6,415 imgs / 963 pts`; patient counts sum exactly to `9,621`, `Table 1`, indicating patient-level counts match cleanly across subsets).
- **External Test Set (`p. 3`):** `500` patient cases randomly sampled from Kaggle `RSNA Breast Cancer Detection` competition (`dicom-resized-png-jpg`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Density Reference Standard (`p. 2`):** Blinded dual consensus + 3rd senior arbiter (`>10` yrs experience) using ACR BI-RADS 5th-ed. categories `A–D`. No pathology/biopsy cancer endpoints evaluated.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- Patient totals (`6,734 + 1,924 + 963 = 9,621`, `Table 1`) confirm patient-level splitting without overlap across internal sets, plus `500`-case external RSNA validation (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN` on partition counts, though external Kaggle source uses resized community PNGs).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Internal Architecture Text vs Table Mismatch (`p. 3` vs `Table 2` p. 4):** Methods text (`p. 3`) lists `ResNet50, InceptionV3, DenseNet121, Xception`, yet **`Table 2` (p. 4) prints `VGG19, InceptionV3, ResNet50, Xception`** (`DenseNet121` omitted from results!). Trained `CE loss`, `Adam`, `lr = 1e-3`, `bs = 64`, `100` epochs, soft voting across multiple views per exam (`p. 3`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Downsampled severely to **`224 x 224`**, histogram equalization contrast normalization, geometric + brightness/contrast augmentations on train (`p. 3`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **`NONE` (`0` XAI methods evaluated).**

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Taizhou Test (`Table 2`, `Fig. 2`, pp. 4–5):** `InceptionV3` OvR `AP`: `A = 0.895, B = 0.857, C = 0.953, D = 0.952`; accuracies `A: 0.987, B: 0.965, C: 0.931, D: 0.911`.
- **Reader Comparison (`500`-case subsample, `Table 3–4`, p. 6):** `InceptionV3` `κ = 0.831 (0.798–0.863)` in `0.027 s/img` vs 3 radiologists (`4, 7, 9` yrs exp) scoring extraordinarily low individual `κ = 0.060–0.071` (`13.5–18 s/img`)! Pairwise two-tailed t-tests adjusted via **`Holm-Bonferroni`** (`11/12` comparisons `p < 0.001`, `Table 4`).
- **RSNA External (`500` cases, `Table 5`, `Figs. 4–5`, pp. 6–8):** `κ = 0.803 (0.768–0.838)`; OvR `AUC: A 0.976, B 0.944, C 0.973, D 0.960`; accuracy `A: 0.946, B: 0.896, C: 0.960, D: 0.902`.
- **Statistical & Calibration Anomalies:** (1) Unbounded normal-approx Wald CIs exceed `1.0` (`Table 3` Rad 1/3 Recall `1.174`; `Table 5` Cat `c` Precision **`1.007`**!); (2) **Zero `ECE` / `Brier score` calibration metrics**; (3) Extreme mismatch between `99.9%` reader disagreement (`κ = 0.06`) vs consensus raises question on junior-reader blinding/calibration.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 8`):** Young Chinese screening cohort (`low Category a 0.6%`); radiologists lacked clinical history; subjective 4-category BI-RADS reference without DBT 2.5D ground truth.
- **Reviewer critique:** Documents real **Asian dense-breast prevalence (`89.1% C+D`, matching `VinDr-Mammo 89.96% C+D`)** and demonstrates **`Holm-Bonferroni`** multiplicity control (`Table 4`) plus RSNA external testing (`Table 5`). However, three quality issues prevent direct reuse beyond statistical/demographic context: (1) `DenseNet121` vs `VGG19` Methods-vs-Table-2 discrepancy; (2) unclipped Wald `CI > 1.000` (`1.007, 1.174`) and implausible radiologist `κ = 0.06`; (3) zero calibration (`ECE/Brier`), zero XAI, and `224x224` downsampling.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (Density Epidemiology & `Holm-Bonferroni` Multiple-Comparison Methodological Context):** Reinforces dense-breast external shift (`89.1% C+D` Asian vs RSNA) and `Holm-Bonferroni` testing, while excluded from lesion-diagnosis/XAI benchmarks due to task mismatch and paper quality glitches.
