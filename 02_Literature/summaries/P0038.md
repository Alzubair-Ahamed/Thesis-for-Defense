## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Chang Liu, Min Sun, Dooman Arefan, Margarita Zuley, Jules Sumkin, and Shandong Wu.
- **Title:** *Deep learning of mammogram images to reduce unnecessary breast biopsies: a preliminary study*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Breast Cancer Research*, **2024**, Vol. `26`, Article `82`, pp. `1–8`.
- **DOI:** `10.1186/s13058-024-01830-9`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Clinical Operating-Point Benchmark `Specificity@95/99/100% Sensitivity` With `DeLong` & `LIBRA` Segmentation).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Evaluate whether CNNs trained on diagnostic FFDM can stratify biopsy-proven BI-RADS 4 findings across 4 binary clinical decision tasks to reduce unnecessary core-needle and excisional biopsies at high fixed sensitivity (`pp. 1–2`).
- **Task & Modality:** **Lesion malignancy / atypia triage** on whole-breast-segmented **2D FFDM (`256 x 256`, bilateral CC & MLO)**.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **University of Pittsburgh / Magee-Womens Hospital Private Cohort (`2016–2018`, `pp. 2–4`, `Table 1` p. 4):**
  - **`847` patients** with screening-detected BI-RADS 4 lesions (`Hologic/Lorad Selenia` FFDM; `56.4%` dense [`BI-RADS C 49.4% + D 0.9%` out of reported; `54` unknown]; mean age `59 ± 12` yrs).
  - Five pathology strata: `194` benign, `198` pure atypia, `200` DCIS, `200` invasive, `55` atypia upstaged to malignancy at excision (`4` binary tasks defined across these groups, `Fig. 1`, p. 3).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Gold-Standard Biopsy Ground Truth (`p. 2`):** Every included patient (`847/847`) underwent core needle (and excisional for atypia) histopathologic verification; normal non-biopsied exams were not included (BI-RADS 4 triage setting).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Patient-Level Protocol (`p. 3`):** Strict patient-wise `70%` train / `10%` validation / `20%` independent test split for Tasks 1–3, and `10-fold` CV for small-sample Task 4 (`n = 253`), with CC+MLO late-fusion logistic regression fit strictly on train (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN` internally, but **zero external validation**).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- ImageNet-pretrained **`VGG16`** per view (`TensorFlow 1.13/Keras 2.1.6`, `Adam`, `lr = 1e-4`, `bs = 32`, `dropout = 0.5`), combined across CC and MLO predictions via logistic regression (`p. 3`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Automated whole-breast background removal via **`LIBRA v1.0.4`** -> `[0, 1]` normalization -> bicubic downsample to **`256 x 256`** -> horizontal flip augmentation (`p. 3`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **`NONE` (`0` XAI methods):** Completely opaque predictions — problematic given the clinical goal of deferring biopsy on suspicious BI-RADS 4 lesions.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **AUC & High-Sensitivity Operating-Point Specificity (`Tables 2–3`, p. 6; `Fig. 2`, p. 5):**
  - **Task 1 (`benign vs atypia+DCIS+invasive`):** `CC+MLO` AUC **`0.72 [0.62, 0.80]`**; `Spec@95%Sens = 33%`, `Spec@99%Sens = 16%`, `Spec@100%Sens = 16%` (*note minor typo in Discussion `p. 5` writing `"5% (or 14%)"` at `100%/99%`, disagreeing with `Table 2` `16%/16%` and `p. 4` `16%/9%`*).
  - **Task 2 (`benign+atypia vs malignancy`):** `CC+MLO` AUC `0.67 [0.60, 0.74]`; `Spec@95%Sens = 23%` (`MLO 25%`).
  - **Task 3 (pairwise):** benign vs atypia `0.70 (MLO)` / `Spec@95% = 30%`; benign vs DCIS `0.73 (CC+MLO)` / `Spec@95% = 25%`; benign vs invasive `0.72` / `Spec@95% = 46% (MLO)` / `43% (CC+MLO)`.
  - **Task 4 (`pure atypia vs upstaged`, `10-fold CV`):** `CC` AUC `0.67 ± 0.14`, `Spec = 35%`.
- **Statistical Rigor:** Bootstrap `95% CIs` + **`DeLong`'s test** (`MATLAB R2020a`, comparing CC vs MLO vs CC+MLO: all `p = 0.12–0.77` NS).
- **Calibration:** `not reported` (`zero` `ECE`, `Brier score`, or reliability curve despite operating at strict `95%–100%` clinical sensitivity cut-offs).

## 10. Code/weight availability

- `not reported` (code not shared; imaging private under HIPAA/DUA, `p. 7`).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 7`):** Single retrospective center (`Hologic` only), modest sample size, older screen cohort (`mean ~59`), outdated `VGG16` backbone, no DBT/multimodal inputs, no external validation.
- **Reviewer critique:** Valuable clinical protocol exemplar for **`Specificity @ 95%/99% Sensitivity`**, `DeLong` CIs, and `LIBRA` whole-breast segmentation (`p. 3`). Yet severely dated technically (`256x256 VGG16`), single-vendor, lacks calibration (`critical when choosing a 95% sensitivity threshold`) and XAI (`Clinicians cannot verify why a BI-RADS 4 lesion is spared biopsy`), and contains an unchecked Discussion-vs-Table text typo (`p. 5` `"5%/14%"` vs `Table 2` `16%/16%`).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (Clinical High-Sensitivity Specificity Protocol & Biopsy-Proven Cohort Reference):** Defines the clinically actionable `Specificity@95%Sens` operating-point metric alongside `DeLong`, though private dataset, legacy `VGG16`, and absent XAI/calibration preclude direct quantitative comparison.

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- External multi-vendor validation; probability calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`); XAI faithfulness/localization; resolution of `p. 5` `"5%/14%"` vs `Table 2` `"16%/16%"`; public code URL.
