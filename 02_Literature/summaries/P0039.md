## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Hieu T. Nguyen, Ha Q. Nguyen, Hieu H. Pham, Khanh Lam, Linh T. Le, Minh Dao, and Van Vu.
- **Title:** *VinDr-Mammo: A large-scale benchmark dataset for computer-aided diagnosis in full-field digital mammography*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Data* (Nature), **2023**, Vol. `10`, Article `277`, pp. `1–8` (Published 12 May 2023).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41597-023-02100-7` (PhysioNet dataset DOI: `10.13026/br2v-7517`).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q4` — Official Primary Benchmark Dataset Descriptor for `VinDr-Mammo`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Introduce and document an open-access Vietnamese multi-vendor **4-view 2D FFDM benchmark dataset** with standardized iterative-stratified train/test splits, breast-level BI-RADS & breast density labels, and local finding bounding boxes (`pp. 1–6`).
- **Modality:** `FOR PRESENTATION` 2D FFDM DICOMs (`L-CC, R-CC, L-MLO, R-MLO`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **VinDr-Mammo Cohort (`pp. 3–6`, `Tables 3–5`):**
  - **`5,000` four-view exams (`20,000` FFDM images)** randomly sampled (`2018–2020`, mix of screening and diagnostic) from Hanoi Medical University Hospital (`HMUH`) and Hospital 108 (`H108`), Vietnam.
  - **Vendors (`p. 3`):** `SIEMENS`, `IMS`, and `Planmed` (`3` distinct FFDM manufacturers).
  - **Breast-Level BI-RADS Distribution (`Table 3`, p. 5, `n = 10,000` breasts / `20,000` views):** `BI-RADS 1: 6,703 (67.03%)`, `BI-RADS 2: 2,338 (23.38%)`, `BI-RADS 3: 465 (4.65%)`, `BI-RADS 4: 381 (3.81%)`, `BI-RADS 5: 113 (1.13%)` (**no BI-RADS 6**).
  - **Breast Density (`Table 4`, p. 5):** **`A: 50 (0.50%)`, `B: 954 (9.54%)`, `C: 7,646 (76.46%)`, `D: 1,350 (13.50%)`** -> **`89.96%` dense (`C+D`)** (crucial Asian dense-breast shift vs Western cohorts!).
  - **Annotated Findings (`Table 5`, p. 5):** Only findings with `BI-RADS > 2` are boxed (`Mass: 1,226`, `Suspicious Calcification: 543`, `Focal Asymmetry: 269`, `Architectural Distortion: 119`, `Asymmetry: 97`, `Skin Thickening: 57`, `Suspicious Lymph Node: 57`, `Nipple Retraction: 37`, `Global Asymmetry: 26`, `Skin Retraction: 18`). Notice **`BI-RADS 2` benign findings are NOT localized with bounding boxes** (`p. 4`), and cross-view bounding boxes (CC vs MLO of the same lesion) are unlinked (`p. 6`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **CRITICAL Provenance Constraint (`Usage Notes`, p. 7):** **Zero histopathologic biopsy confirmation (`no BI-RADS 6`).** Ground truth relies strictly on **independent double-reading + third-reader arbitration by 3 board-certified breast radiologists** (`14–22` yrs experience, mean `13,333` exams/yr, `Table 2` p. 2). Authors explicitly warn: *"should not be used to directly evaluate CAD for diagnosis purposes, but only used in training settings"* or converted to 3-class screening triage (`BI-RADS 1`, `BI-RADS 2`, recall = `BI-RADS 3/4/5`)!

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Pre-Defined Exam-Stratified Split (`pp. 4–5`):** Iterative stratification divides the `5,000` exams (`20,000` images) cleanly into **`4,000` training exams (`8,000` breasts / `16,000` imgs)** and **`1,000` test exams (`2,000` breasts / `4,000` imgs)** preserving BI-RADS, density, and finding proportions (`Tables 3–5`, `Fig. 4` age distribution). *Caveat:* Stratification operates per exam (`study_id`), and missing longitudinal patient IDs across multiple years means rare repeat-patient exams cannot be verified externally (`data anonymized`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Dataset descriptor paper — trains no deep learning classifiers.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Pseudonymization blacks out corner burned-in text rectangles (`p. 3`). **Critical Technical Caveat (`p. 7`):** Authors note *"the introduced dataset is not DICOM-compliant and it would fail to be processed properly by DICOM processing libraries"* without standard defensive handling.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Localization Enabler (`pp. 3–6`):** Supplies `2,449` bounding-box finding annotations (`BI-RADS 3–5` non-benign findings across CC and MLO) that directly enable quantitative XAI localization metrics (`Pointing Game`, `IoU`, `Activation Precision`) on suspicious/recalled exams, while missing boxes for `BI-RADS 2` benign findings.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- Descriptive benchmark statistics only (`Tables 3–5`, `Figs. 3–4`); no classifier metrics.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- **Open-Source Dataset & Scripts (`p. 5, 7`):** Images/CSVs via PhysioNet Credentialed License 1.5.0 (`https://doi.org/10.13026/br2v-7517`); pseudonymization & stratification scripts at `https://github.com/vinbigdata-medical/vindr-mammo`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 7`):** Absence of biopsy pathology ground truth; `BI-RADS 2` lesions un-boxed; `<40` samples for rare signs (skin/nipple retraction, global asymmetry); non-strict DICOM compliance; no CC–MLO lesion correspondence links.
- **Reviewer critique:** Essential primary specification defining how our thesis MUST use `VinDr-Mammo`: (1) strictly enforce the official `4,000/1,000` exam split; (2) evaluate triage (`Recall BI-RADS 3–5 vs Normal/Benign 1–2`) OR train localization/XAI bounding-box IoU/PG on `BI-RADS 3–5` findings rather than claiming pathology-verified "cancer vs benign" on unbiopsied VinDr labels (`contrasted against biopsy-confirmed CMMD/CBIS-DDSM/INbreast`, solving false conflation!); (3) handle non-compliant DICOM headers explicitly and leverage its **`89.96%` dense (`C+D`) + 3-vendor (`SIEMENS/IMS/Planmed`)** profile for density & hardware-shift audits (`RG3`, `RG4`).

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B3_METH` / `B4_CTX` (Foundational Multi-Vendor FFDM Dataset Specification):** Governs label semantics (`BI-RADS triage` vs biopsy), pre-defined split integrity (`4,000/1,000`), bounding-box XAI localization bounds (`BI-RADS > 2`), and dense-breast demographic distribution (`89.96% C+D`).

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- Repeat-patient overlap across the `5,000` randomly sampled exams; biopsy follow-up linkage; `BI-RADS 2` benign lesion localization masks.
