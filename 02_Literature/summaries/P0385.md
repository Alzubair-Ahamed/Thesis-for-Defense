## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Quantify under a uniform training protocol how strongly **evaluation granularity (`side-level` vs `patient-level`)**, **enrichment class imbalance**, and **multi-class aggregation rules** dictate headline performance compared to backbone choice (`CNN vs ViT`) or multi-view fusion strategy (`Sec. 1`).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary malignancy diagnosis (`nonmalignant` vs `malignant`) and 5-class BI-RADS scoring (`0–4` -> `1–5`) on **4-view full-image 2D FFDM (`384 x 384`)**.

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Chinese Mammography Database (`CMMD`, `Sec. 3.1`, `Table 1`):**
  - Filtered to all patients having complete **4 views (`L_CC, L_MLO, R_CC, R_MLO`)**: **`706` patients / `1,412` breasts / `2,824` FFDM images**.
  - **Crucial Hidden Imbalance Exposed (`Sec. 3.1.1, 5.6`):**
    - **Side level (`1,412` breasts):** near-balanced **`682` malignant (`48.3%`)** vs **`730` nonmalignant (`51.7%`: `683` normal contralateral BI-RADS 0 + `16` BI-RADS 1 + `26` BI-RADS 2 + `5` BI-RADS 3)**.
    - **Patient level (`706` patients):** **`679/706 (96.2%)` malignant** (`352` L-only, `324` R-only, `3` bilateral) vs only **`27/706 (3.8%)` bilaterally benign**! Thus **`CMMD` is an enriched diagnostic biopsy cohort, NOT a `~0.5%`-prevalence screening cohort**!

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- Biopsy-confirmed benign/malignant labels on affected breasts (`682` malignant + `47` biopsied benign), with normal contralateral breasts labeled `BI-RADS 0` (`683` sides); authors explicitly avoid confounding `"benign/normal"` and evaluate `y_patient = max(y_left, y_right)` (`Sec. 3.1, 3.6.3`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Strict Patient-Level 5-Fold Stratified CV (`Sec. 3.6.1`):** All `4` views of each patient stay locked in the same fold (`80/20` train/val within train folds); **fit-free per-image `[0, 1]` normalization** prevents statistic leakage across splits (`Sec. 3.1.2`, `Leakage Risk: CLEAN`). Single-dataset only (`no external validation`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`16` Configurations Completed (`6` ImageNet-pretrained backbones x `3` fusions; `Sec. 3.2–3.3, 4.1`):**
  - `ResNet-18` (`11.2M`), `ResNet-50` (`23.5M`), `EfficientNet-B0` (`4.0M`), `DenseNet-121` (`7.0M`), `ConvNeXt-Tiny` (`28.6M`), and `ViT-B/16` (`86.6M`). (*`Swin-Tiny` and two `Crossview` hooks failed architectural extraction.*)
  - Fusions: Concatenation (`4d`), Bilateral Asymmetry (`|f_L - f_R|`, `7d`), Cross-view spatial attention.
  - Shared recipe (`Sec. 3.5`): `AdamW (lr=1e-4, wd=0.1)`, cosine schedule, `bs=8`, max `50` ep, patience `15`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Per-image min-max `[0, 1]` scaling (fit-free, 3-ch replicated, no ImageNet mean/SD), resized **`384 x 384`** (`noted caveat for tiny microcalcifications`), random affine (`±10 deg, ±5% shift, 0.95–1.05 scale`) + independent `RandomHorizontalFlip` (`Sec. 3.1.2` notes flip breaks L/R correspondence conservatively).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Landmark Side-vs-Patient Granularity Gap (`Tables 2–4`):**
  - **Side-level AUC (`5-fold mean`, `Table 2`):** Top CNNs cluster tightly within `0.025` (`ConvNeXt-T Concat 0.975 ± 0.008`, `ResNet-50 Bilateral 0.973 ± 0.007`, `EffNet-B0 Bilateral 0.969 ± 0.012`, `DenseNet-121 Bilateral 0.964`, `ResNet-18 Bilateral 0.963`). **`ViT-B/16` drops `8–10%` (`0.864–0.880`)** with different error patterns (`Jaccard 0.29` vs CNNs `0.42`).
  - **Patient-level AUC (`Table 3`):** Plummets **`-0.175` on average (range `-0.127` to `-0.224`, `Table 4`)**! Best patient-level model `EffNet-B0 Bilateral` drops to `0.842 ± 0.077` (`ConvNeXt-T Concat 0.975 -> 0.751`; `ViT-B/16 Concat 0.879 -> 0.670`). Patient-level **Specificity collapses to `0.033–0.220`** due to `96.2%` patient prevalence (`always predicting malignant gives 0.96 Acc`), and Bayes projection shows a `96%`-prevalence threshold collapses PPV to **`0.54%`** at `0.5%` screening prevalence (`Sec. 5.6`)!
- **Aggregation Bug Discovery (`Abstract`, `Sec. 4.8.1, 5.5`):** Standard BI-RADS `label=max, prob=mean` averaging produces **degenerate `macro-AUC = 0.000`**; proposes `max-class`, `per-class max-pooling`, or direct patient heads.
- **Statistical Power Finding (`Sec. 4.7`):** `5-fold Wilcoxon` hits **`p = 0.0625` floor** resolving `0` pairs, while pooled-score **`DeLong` (`N=1,412` sides)** resolves `77/120` pairs (`38/78` CNN-CNN, all CNN-ViT `median p < 1e-12`). Also documents up to **`15%` single-fold variance** (`ConvNeXt-T Bilateral 0.989 -> 0.833`).
- **Calibration:** **`not reported` (`zero ECE / Brier score`)**.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`Sec. 5.7`):** Modest `706` 4-view `CMMD` size; `384x384` microcalcification loss; independent horizontal flip attenuates bilateral fusion; no external cohort test.
- **Reviewer critique:** Must-cite methodological cornerstone for `RG2` and `RG3`: cleanly isolates **`CMMD`'s `96.2%` patient-level enrichment confound**, proves **`+17.5` AUC side-vs-patient inflation** (`dwarfing CNN choice <3%`), documents `ViT-B/16` small-data deficit (`0.874` vs `~0.966`), explains why `5-fold Wilcoxon` is underpowered (`0.0625` floor vs `DeLong`), and reveals how missing `ECE/calibration + prevalence shift + zero XAI` render high side-level AUCs unready for clinical triage.
