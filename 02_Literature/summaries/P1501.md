## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Ahmed A. H. Alkurdi and Amira Bibo Sallow.
- **Title:** *Calibrated Transformer Fusion for Dual-View Low-Energy CESM Classification*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Journal of Imaging* (MDPI), **2026**, Vol. `12`, Issue `1`, Article `41`.
- **DOI / IDs:** `10.3390/jimaging12010041` | `PMCID: PMC12842785` | `PMID: 41590926`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`Low-Energy CDD-CESM Calibration & Grad-CAM Comparator`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Evaluate whether dual-view (`CC` + `MLO`) dual-backbone (`ResNet-101 + DenseNet-121`) transformer fusion (`DVTF`) with Monte Carlo dropout uncertainty and post-hoc logistic calibration improves side-level screening abnormality detection strictly on **Low-Energy (`DM`)** images of `CDD-CESM` (`Sec. 3.1`), adhering to `CLAIM` & `TRIPOD+AI` (`Sec. 3`).
- **Modality:** Exclusively `1,003` **Low-Energy (`DM`)** images from `CDD-CESM` (`excludes recombined contrast images`, aligning cleanly with `RF15`).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **`CDD-CESM` (`TCIA 10.7937/29kw-ae92`):** `326` patients (`2,006` total images), filtered strictly to `1,003` Low-Energy `DM` views (`Left/Right` `CC` + `MLO`) evaluated at the **breast-side level** (`Sec. 3.1`).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance and Normal-case handling

- **Endpoint Merging Caveat:** Merges `Benign` + `Malignant` into a single **`Tumorous`** positive class vs **`Normal`** (`Sec. 3`), differing from `RF05`'s **Benign vs Malignant** diagnostic distinction.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Leakage Control (`Sec. 3.4`):** `5-fold StratifiedGroupKFold` grouped strictly by `PatientID` (`prevents same-patient views crossing train/test folds`); fold-specific threshold `tau_f` tuned on validation by `F1`.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- Dual ImageNet-pretrained CNNs (`ResNet-101` + `DenseNet-121`) + `2`-layer `TransformerEncoder` (`Models A–E` ablation), MC-dropout (`K=10` default, sensitivity `K in {5, 10, 20}`), post-hoc logistic calibration (`logit(p_cal) = a*logit(p_mc) + b` fit on validation split, `Sec. 3.9`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- Offline 8-bit grayscale loading, background masking (`pixel <= 5`), `CLAHE` enhancement, `224 x 224` vs `384 x 384` resolution sensitivity analysis, and training-only random horizontal flip (`p=0.5`) & `±10°` rotation (`Sec. 3.2–3.3, 4.3`).

## 8. XAI method(s) and quantitative evaluation

- **Visual XAI (`Sec. 3.14, 4.5`):** Extracts transformer token attention matrix (`[CLS], Res-CC, Res-MLO, Den-CC, Den-MLO`) + **Grad-CAM** overlays from the ResNet stream across `TP/FP/FN/TN` examples (`qualitative visual overlay only` — zero `Pointing Game / IoU / Deletion / Adebayo` checks!).

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics

- **Comprehensive Metrics (`Table 1`, `Table 3`, `Sec. 3.12`):** Reports `Accuracy`, `Sensitivity`, `Specificity`, `Precision`, `F1`, `ROC-AUC`, `PR-AUC`, `Brier score`, `10-bin ECE`, and `NLL` across 5 folds (`Model E` ROC-AUC ranges `0.9753–1.0000` across folds in `Table 1`, `NLL` `0.0483–0.2383`; `384x384` slightly improves discrimination over `224x224`).

## 10. Code/weight availability

- Built on public `CDD-CESM` (`TCIA 10.7937/29kw-ae92`) under Colab `PyTorch 2.9.0`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Authors (`Sec. 5.4`):** Single `326`-patient cohort with zero external validation, Low-Energy only, no cross-site test.
- **Critique:** Exemplary calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`, `NLL`, `StratifiedGroupKFold` by `PatientID`) and `RF15`-aligned **Low-Energy (`DM`)-only filtering** of `CDD-CESM`; however, merges `Benign+Malignant` into `Tumorous` (instead of Benign vs Malignant) and stops at qualitative Grad-CAM without quantitative localization/faithfulness checks.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` / `B3_METH` (`Low-Energy CDD-CESM` Calibration + Grad-CAM Exemplar):** Directly informs our `Low-Energy`-only filtering protocol if `CDD-CESM` is used, while proving calibration (`ECE/Brier/NLL`) + quantitative XAI gap on `CDD-CESM`.

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- Exact Benign vs Malignant sub-task metrics; external cross-cohort validation; quantitative Grad-CAM localization (`IoU/PG`) against `CDD-CESM` hand-drawn segmentations.
