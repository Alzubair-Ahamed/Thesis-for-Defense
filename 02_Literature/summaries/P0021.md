## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Core Methodological Contributions Relevant to Mammography XAI (`pp. 11, 13, 18, 23, 26, 28`):**
  1. Explicitly emphasizes that **an attention map, Dice segmentation overlap, or example-retrieval match does NOT establish explanation faithfulness** (`"However, an attention map alone does not establish that the highlighted information faithfully explains the prediction"`, p. 11; `Fig. 3`, p. 13; `p. 18`).
  2. Demands **separate XAI validation** (`Fig. 3b`, p. 13): perturbation/intervention tests, **Adebayo model-randomization sanity checks (`[92]`, p. 11)**, stability under noise, and task-specific clinical evaluation.
  3. Notes **explanation readability or visual plausibility is dangerous** if coupled with a poorly calibrated model (`"An explanation can reflect the behavior of a poorly calibrated model without making its predictions reliable"`, p. 27).
  4. Summarizes joint **FDA / Health Canada / MHRA Good Machine Learning Practice / Transparency Principles (`[307]`, p. 26)**: explainability is only one component alongside calibration, human factors, and device validation (`p. 26`).
