## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Kengo Takahashi, Yuwen Zeng, Zhang Zhang, Kei Ichiji, Takuma Usuzaki, Ryusei Inamori, Haoyang Liu, and Noriyasu Homma.
- **Title:** *Transformer-based Deep Learning Models with Shape Guidance for Predicting Breast Cancer in Mammography Images*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Pages:** *Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine* (Springer), **2026** (published 19 Dec 2025), Vol. `39`, pp. `4038–4051`.
- **DOI:** `10.1007/s10278-025-01773-3`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q1b`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Evaluate whether shape-guided Vision Transformers (`Masked ViT` and `Masked SwinT V2` masking background outside segmented breast tissue) improve whole-mammogram breast cancer prediction over standard Transformers (`ViT`, `SwinT V2`) and `ResNet50`.
- **Task & Modality:** Whole-image **2D FFDM** binary classification: **Cancer (`1,167`) vs Non-Cancer (`1,269`: `215` benign + `1,054` normal)** using **MLO view only** (`2294x1914` native resized to `2048x2048`, LANCZOS; GE Senographe DS & Siemens Mammomat Inspiration; p. 3 / 4040).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **TOMPEI-CMMD** (enhanced version of `CMMD`, p. 3 / 4040):
  - **Initial MLO pool:** `2,601` MLO images.
  - **Exclusions (`171` reported in text; breakdown lists `6` phyllodes, `2` neurofibromatosis, `1` lymphedema, `8` ports, `5` foreign bodies, `3` artifacts, `140` no identifiable lesions):** Note arithmetic of listed counts sums to `165` (`2601 - 165 = 2,436` retained images; p. 3 / 4040).
  - **Analyzed Set:** **`2,436` MLO images** (`1,167` cancer, `215` benign, `1,054` normal; exact unique patient count across the `2,436` MLO images `not reported` in verified extract).
  - **External validation cohort:** None (`single-dataset limitation`, p. 4047).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Label Provenance:** Biopsy-confirmed benign/malignant ground truth inherited from CMMD + lesion/breast segmentations annotated by a radiologist (`>20 yrs` experience) and `5` technologists (p. 3 / 4040).
- **Normal-case Handling:** `1,054` normal MLO images pooled into the `Non-Cancer` class alongside `215` benign MLO images (`1,269` total non-cancer; `1,167` cancer).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`patient_level_3fold_cv`** (`70%` train / `30%` validation per fold; Tables 1–2, pp. 4042–4044).
- **Leakage / External Validation Assessment:** Patient-wise splitting prevents patient leakage across internal CV folds, but there is **no independent held-out test split separate from CV** and **no external cross-site/cross-vendor validation** (explicitly noted as a single-dataset limitation, p. 4047).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **Architectures (`2048x2048` input, `128x128` patch size, `12` layers, `12` heads, `768-d`, Table 1 p. 4043; Fig. 2 p. 4041):**
  - **Masked ViT** & **Masked SwinT V2:** Binary breast-segmentation masks (`>0` intensity, BICUBIC) zero out non-breast background self-attention (`-inf` attention mask) in **Shallow (`L1–L4`)**, **Deep (`L9–L12`)**, or **Shallow+Deep** layers.
  - **Comparators:** Conventional `ViT`, `SwinT V2`, `ResNet50`.
- **Initialization & Training:** **Trained from scratch** (explicitly stated limitation, p. 4049; no ImageNet pretraining). PyTorch `2.5.1`, `timm` `1.0.12`, Weighted Cross-Entropy `(0.415..0.585)`, AdamW (`lr=1e-5 -> 1e-6`), Cosine Annealing, batch size `4`, `30` epochs. Random seed numbers: `not reported`.

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing:** GE Senographe DS & Siemens Mammomat Inspiration FFDM (`2294x1914`) resized to **`2048x2048`** via **LANCZOS**; binary breast-shape segmentation masks derived via `>0` intensity thresholding and **BICUBIC** resizing (`Fig. 2`, p. 4041). Intensity standardization/CLAHE and data augmentation details: `not reported`.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method(s):** **Grad-CAM** (`Figs. 4–5`, pp. 4046–4047) and **per-head attention maps** (`Fig. 6`, p. 4048).
- **Target Layer:** Exact target layer name `not reported`.
- **Evaluation Mode:** Main PDF presents **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`Figs. 4–6`); text (p. 4046) states quantitative overlap and coverage between heatmaps and annotations were measured in **Online Appendix 5** (`Supplementary Info`), **with quantitative numbers omitted from the main PDF**. Zero Deletion/Insertion/ROAD faithfulness, zero Adebayo sanity checks, zero reader study.

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **3-Fold Patient-Wise CV Results (`Table 2`, p. 4044; `1,000`-bootstrap `95%` CIs + DeLong test `Fig. 3`, p. 4045):**
  - **Masked ViT (Shallow+Deep):** Mean **AUROC `0.885` (`[0.849–0.918]`)**, **AUPRC `0.874` (`[0.823–0.924]`)**, Acc `0.844`, Sen `0.876`, Spec `0.802`, F1 `0.864`, MCC `0.689`.
  - **Masked SwinT V2:** AUROC **`0.879` (`[0.845–0.912]`)**, **AUPRC `0.890`**.
  - **Conventional SwinT V2:** AUROC **`0.877`**.
  - **Conventional ViT:** AUROC **`0.864`**.
  - **ResNet50 (from scratch):** AUROC **`0.731` (`[0.685–0.777]`)**.
- **Statistical Significance (`Fig. 3`, p. 4045):** **DeLong test** shows significant difference (`p < 0.05`) for `Masked ViT` vs `ViT` in **Folds 2 & 3**, and vs `ResNet50` across all folds.
- **Calibration (`ECE`, `Brier`), McNemar, subgroup/density stratification:** `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated:** Single-dataset evaluation (`p. 4047`); models trained from scratch rather than pretrained (`p. 4049`); CC view omitted (MLO-only cohort).
- **Reviewer critique:** Strong high-resolution (`2048x2048`) patient-wise whole-FFDM benchmark with AUPRC, MCC, bootstrap 95% CIs, and DeLong testing (`p<0.05`); however, trained from scratch (disadvantaging `ResNet50` at `0.731` AUROC), uses single-view MLO only, reports overlap metrics only in inaccessible Online Appendix 5, lacks calibration (`ECE/Brier`), lacks Adebayo/perturbation faithfulness checks, and has no external validation.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — Patient-Split Whole-FFDM Transformer Benchmark):** Direct whole-mammogram Transformer/CNN benchmark on `TOMPEI-CMMD` (`2048x2048`, patient-wise CV, `AUROC 0.885` / `AUPRC 0.874`), limited by MLO-only views, from-scratch training, single dataset, and qualitative-in-main-text XAI.
