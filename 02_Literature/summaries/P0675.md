## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Luke Moffett, Alina Jade Barnett, Jon Donnelly, Fides Regina Schwartz, Hari Trivedi, Joseph Lo, and Cynthia Rudin.
- **Title:** *Multi-site validation of an interpretable model to analyze breast masses*.
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *PLOS ONE*, **2025**, Vol. `20`, No. `6`, `e0320091`, pp. `1–18` (Published 26 Jun 2025).
- **DOI:** `10.1371/journal.pone.0320091`.
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`Stage-2` High-Priority Full-Text Open-Access Verification — First External Validation of `IAIA-BL` & Cross-Site Activation Precision Decay).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Externally validate the inherently interpretable `IAIA-BL` mass-margin and malignancy prototype classifier across independent multi-vendor populations (`Emory EMBED` and `iCAD`), evaluating whether **both discriminative performance AND lesion-focused interpretability (`activation precision`) generalize under distribution shift** (`pp. 1–4`).
- **Task & Modality:** **ROI-cropped breast mass margin (`3` classes) + binary malignancy classification** (`bounding box + 100 px context window`, 2D screening FFDM).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **Three Multi-Vendor Institutions (`Tables 1–2`, pp. 6–7; `Fig. 3`, p. 7):**
  - **Training / Internal Test (`Duke University`):** `516` train lesion imgs (`5,000` augmented) + `78` test imgs (`77` masses / `49` patients; `GE 80% / Hologic 20%`, `56%` White / `41%` Black).
  - **External Target 1 (`Emory EMBED`):** `103` imgs (`58` biopsied masses / `58` patients; `Hologic 100%`, `48%` Black / `45%` White; `47` circumscribed, `39` indistinct, `17` spiculated; `27/103` malignant; proprietary Emory window/leveling applied earlier!). Plus `100`-pt val set for context window tuning (`p. 8`).
  - **External Target 2 (`iCAD` proprietary):** `425` imgs (`449` masses / `207` patients; `GE 50% / Hologic 27% / Siemens 23%`; `280` spiculated / `169` non-spiculated; **`99.6%` malignant (`447/449`)**!).

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- **Histopathology + Lexicon Margins (`p. 6`):** All evaluated masses have biopsy-confirmed malignancy pathology AND radiologist-coded BI-RADS mass margin labels (`single fellowship subspecialist at Duke vs multi-reader routine screening at Emory`, causing **`annotation shift`**, `p. 14`).

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- Strict patient-level internal Duke split and **true 100% out-of-institution external validation** across Emory `EMBED` and `iCAD` (`Leakage Risk: CLEAN`).

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`IAIA-BL`** (`VGG-16` ImageNet backbone + prototype layer trained with expert **fine-annotation spatial loss** on `15` margin-masked lesions + logistic regression over margin scores, `pp. 2, 8`) vs **`ProtoPNet`** (unguided prototypes) vs **`VGG-16`** black-box baseline (`re-finetuned after fixing a Barnett et al. bug`).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- DICOM window/level normalization `[0, 1]`, lesion ROI bounding box padded by **`+100` px context border**; training augmentations (flips, rotations, up to `80%` random crop, `p. 8`). Note acquisition shift: Emory used pre-processed proprietary window/leveling (`p. 13`).

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Key Quantitative Cross-Site `Activation Precision` Finding (`Table 3`, p. 9; `Fig. 5`, p. 11):**
  - **`IAIA-BL` (`fine-annotation prototype`):** Duke `0.94 (0.94–0.95)` -> Emory `1.00 (0.99–1.00)` -> iCAD **`0.92 (0.92–0.92)`**! Maintains lesion focus everywhere!
  - **`ProtoPNet` (unguided):** Duke `0.56 (0.34–0.79)` -> Emory `0.77` -> iCAD **`0.55 (0.15–0.95)`**.
  - **`VGG-16 + Grad-CAM`:** Duke `0.64 (0.53–0.73)` -> Emory `0.72` -> **collapses on iCAD to `0.16 (0.14–0.18)`**!
  - **`VGG-16 + Grad-CAM++`:** Duke `0.70 (0.60–0.80)` -> Emory `0.83` -> drops on iCAD to **`0.43 (0.40–0.47)`**!
  - **Crucial Insight (`pp. 14–16`):** Authors show both empirically and conceptually that: (1) post-hoc `Grad-CAM/Grad-CAM++` activation localization collapses severely under domain shift (`0.70 -> 0.43` / `0.64 -> 0.16`), and (2) even `IAIA-BL` keeping `0.92–1.00` lesion localization is **NOT sufficient to prevent AUC collapse** under distribution shifts (Population, Prevalence, Acquisition, Manifestation, Annotation shifts)!

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Mass Margin AUC (`Table 3`, p. 9, `DeLong` 95% CI):** All models drop from Duke (`0.92–0.95`) -> Emory (`0.63–0.68`; indistinct `0.50`!) -> iCAD spiculated (`0.70–0.72`).
- **Malignancy AUC (`Fig. 6`, p. 12; p. 9):** Duke -> Emory drops across all models: `IAIA-BL 0.84 -> 0.74 (-0.10)`, `ProtoPNet 0.87 [Fig 6; text p.9 prints 0.88] -> 0.69 (-0.19)`, `VGG-16 0.88 -> 0.80 (-0.08)` (`iCAD 99.6% pos` yields skewed `AUC 0.07–0.37`).
- **Confusion Matrices & Score Distributions (`Table 4`, p. 12; `Fig. 7`, p. 13):** Operating threshold chosen at `<20%` FPR on Duke (`0.03` IAIA-BL vs `0.29` ProtoPNet vs `0.34` VGG16) causes severe **miscalibration shift on Emory** (`IAIA-BL` predicts `65/76` benign Emory lesions malignant, mean score `0.37 ± 0.43` vs `0.04 ± 0.10` at Duke!), yet **`ECE` and `Brier score` are not formally computed**.

## 10. Code/weight availability

- **Code & Open Data Logits (`p. 2, 16`):** Base code `https://github.com/alinajadebarnett/iaiabl`, plus `S1 Source Code` & `S6 Prediction Data CSVs` (`embed-open-data_logits.csv`) on `PLOS ONE`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`p. 15`):** Trained on only 3 of 5 margin types; `iCAD` extreme `99.6%` prevalence skew; strict 5-part inclusion reduced `EMBED` sample to `58` patients; multi-site margin annotation disagreement; warns against applying fine-annotation loss to unfaithful `Grad-CAM` (`"may lead to false confidence"`, `p. 15`).
- **Reviewer critique:** Crown-jewel **Stage-2 empirical proof for `RG1 + RG3`**: explicitly quantifies **Castro's 5 domain shifts** across `Duke -> EMBED -> iCAD` AND proves experimentally that **`Grad-CAM` activation precision collapses (`0.64 -> 0.16`) while prototype activation holds (`0.92`) yet malignancy thresholding & calibration STILL break down externally** (`Table 4`, `Fig. 7`). ROI-cropped setup (`+100 px`), small external `EMBED` test (`58` pts), and missing formal `ECE`/perturbation faithfulness remain gaps our full-image ViT+CNN framework can address.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` / `B3_METH` (Landmark External XAI Generalization & `Activation Precision` Benchmark):** Directly bridges `RG1` (quantitative XAI beyond plausibility) and `RG3` (multi-site generalization + Castro shift taxonomy); provides exact external `Activation Precision` & malignancy AUC drop comparators on `EMBED`.

## 13. Unresolved / `not reported` fields

- Formal `ECE` / `Brier score` calibration metrics despite showing score distribution shift (`Fig. 7`); perturbation faithfulness (`deletion/insertion`) and `Adebayo` checks; full-image screening evaluation (`non-ROI`).
