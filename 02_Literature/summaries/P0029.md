## 1. Verified bibliographic citation and ReportRef

- **Authors:** Magy Makram, Alber S. Aziz, Mary Monir Saeid, Mostafa Thabet Mohamed (p. 1).
- **Title:** *Breast cancer detection and classification via a robust deep learning approach* (p. 1).
- **Venue / Year / Volume / Article:** *Scientific Reports*, **2026**, Vol. `16`, Article `22511`, pp. `1–12` (published 17 July 2026, p. 1).
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-026-62255-2` (p. 1).
- **ReportRef:** `none` (`GS-Q3a`).

## 2. Research question, task, and modality (2D mammogram FFDM/SFM vs ROI vs non-mammogram)

- **RQ / Objective:** Enforce strict patient-level split before augmentation paired with two-stage ResNet50 fine-tuning, Inter-View Attention Fusion (`IVAF`, CC+MLO), 100-seed stability, McNemar's test, and Grad-CAM (Abstract & pp. 1–2).
- **Task & Modality:** Binary Benign vs Malignant classification on 2D **digitized SFM (`CBIS-DDSM`)** using **ROI-cropped `224x224` patches** from a Kaggle archive (p. 4, Fig. 2 p. 5, p. 11).

## 3. Dataset(s), provenance, and reported patient/exam/image counts

- **CBIS-DDSM Kaggle Starter Archive (`kerneler/starter-cbis-ddsm-breast-cancer-image-eef73d6f-4`, p. 11; Abstract, p. 1; Table 4, p. 4):**
  - **Total:** `1,109` unique patients, `30,285` "original" images (`16,657` benign `[55%]`, `13,628` malignant `[45%]`). *(Note: ~27.3 images/patient strongly indicates pre-tiled/replicated Kaggle mirror vs ~10,239 in TCIA CBIS-DDSM).*
  - **Train (60%):** `665` patients, `18,120` original -> `50,025` augmented images post-split.
  - **Val (20%):** `222` patients, `6,048` unaugmented images.
  - **Test (20%):** `222` patients, `6,117` unaugmented images (`3,366` benign, `2,751` malignant).
  - Mass/calcification counts, exam counts, CC/MLO pairing counts, and external datasets: `not reported`.

## 4. Diagnostic label provenance (biopsy vs BI-RADS) and Normal-case handling

- CBIS-DDSM pathologic Benign vs Malignant labels (pp. 1, 4, Table 5 p. 7). Normal (BI-RADS 1): absent. `BENIGN_WITHOUT_CALLBACK` policy: `not reported`.

## 5. Split level (patient vs image vs unknown) and leakage assessment

- **Split Level:** **`patient_level_verified_60_20_20`** explicitly stratified and isolated by patient ID **prior to augmentation** (Fig. 1 & Table 4, p. 4; p. 9), evaluated across **100 random weight seeds** on that fixed split (p. 8). Zero external validation (p. 11).
- **Caveats:** Kaggle archive inflates per-patient sample density (`30,285` ROIs across `1,109` patients), ROI-cropped inputs inflate scores (`97.12%`), and odd test count (`6,117`) leaves paired CC–MLO IVAF routing underspecified.

## 6. Model architectures and transfer learning initialization

- **`ResNet50 + IVAF` (pp. 3, 5–7; Figs. 3–4):** Shared ImageNet-pretrained ResNet50 -> concat `[F_CC; F_MLO]` -> `Conv1x1(64, ReLU) -> Conv1x1(2048, Sigmoid)` gated fusion `F_fused` -> `GAP -> Dropout(0.5) -> Dense(1024) -> Softmax(2)`.
- **7 Reimplemented Comparators (Table 7, p. 9):** VGG16, VGG19, ResNet101, EfficientNet-B0, EfficientNet-B3, DenseNet121, MobileNetV2 (all trained under identical patient split & two-phase schedule: Stage I frozen `lr=1e-4`, Stage II last 2 blocks unfrozen `lr=1e-5`, batch `32`, Adam, class-balanced CCE, EarlyStopping + ReduceLROnPlateau, A100 80 GB).

## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

- **Preprocessing (p. 4; Fig. 2, p. 5):** ROI mask cropping -> bicubic resize **`224x224`** -> `[0,1]` scaling (`/255`). Bit-depth & CLAHE: `not reported`.
- **Train-Only Post-Split Augmentation (`18,120 -> 50,025`, pp. 4–5):** `±15°` rotation, `±10%` shift, `0.90–1.10` zoom, flips, `±20%` contrast/brightness.

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **XAI Method:** **Grad-CAM** (p. 9; Fig. 8, p. 10).
- **Target Layer:** Fused tensor **$F_{\text{fused}}$ output by the IVAF module** (after ResNet50 final conv layer; pp. 1, 9).
- **Evaluation Mode:** **`visual_anecdotes_only`** (`3` visual overlays in Fig. 8, p. 10, explicitly acknowledged as qualitative without IoU/PG/faithfulness/Adebayo sanity/radiologist metrics on pp. 9, 11). *(Fig. 8 "Original" panels resemble speckled synthetic patches.)*

## 9. Classification, calibration, and statistical metrics with exact page/table/figure locators

- **Held-Out Test ($n=6,117$ / 222 patients, Table 5 p. 7, Fig. 6 p. 9):** **`ResNet50+IVAF`** **Acc `97.12%`**, Sens `96.44%`, Spec `97.68%`, **AUC `0.9876`**, Macro F1 `97.09%` (`TN=3,288, FP=78, FN=98, TP=2,653`).
- **100 Random Seeds Stability (p. 1, p. 8):** Acc `97.11% ± 0.18%`, Sens `96.43% ± 0.29%`, Spec `97.66% ± 0.27%`, AUC `0.9874 ± 0.0012`.
- **Sequential Ablation (Table 6, p. 8):** Frozen Phase I (`93.42%`) -> Fine-tune (`95.28%`) -> Class-balanced loss (`96.17%`) -> Post-split aug (`96.73%`) -> +IVAF (`97.12%`).
- **Baselines (Table 7, p. 9):** ResNet101 `96.84%` (`0.9851`), EfficientNet-B3 `96.78%`, DenseNet121 `96.56%`, EfficientNet-B0 `96.31%`, VGG16 `95.82%`, VGG19 `95.45%`, MobileNetV2 `93.47%`.
- **Statistical Test:** **McNemar's test** (+IVAF vs single-view Config D, p. 8): **$\chi^2 = 8.91$, $p = 0.003$**. DeLong, Calibration (ECE/Brier), PR-AUC, 90%-sens spec: `not reported`.

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (pp. 10–11):** Single CBIS-DDSM cohort without INbreast/VinDr external validation; qualitative-only Grad-CAM without radiologist audit; 222 test patients.
- **Reviewer critique:** Rigorous split-before-augmentation & 100-seed/McNemar discipline, but `97.12%` headline AUC/Acc reflects `224x224` ROI crops from an anomalous 30,285-image Kaggle mirror (~27 ROIs/patient), odd test pairing ambiguity (`6,117`), qualitative-only Grad-CAM, and missing external validation/calibration/DeLong.

## 12. Direct comparability verdict for this study

- **`B1` (Primary Direct Comparator — ROI-Cropped Regime):** Directly relevant benchmark for patient-disjoint pre-aug splitting, two-phase ResNet50 fine-tuning, CC-MLO IVAF, 100-seed stability, and McNemar's test; not directly comparable numerically to whole-mammogram FFDM/SFM scores.
