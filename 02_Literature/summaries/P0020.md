## 7. Preprocessing and data augmentation

## 8. XAI method(s), target layers, and quantitative XAI evaluation (faithfulness/sanity/localization vs visual only)

- **Key Frequency Synthesis (`Tables 6–7`, `Fig. 2`, pp. 10–12):**
  - **Methods Used (`46` studies):** **`Grad-CAM 34/46 (73.9%)`**, `LIME 15/46`, `Grad-CAM++ 12/46`, `SHAP 10/46`, `ScoreCAM 6/46`, `Integrated Gradients 6/46`, `LayerCAM 2/46`, `EigenCAM 2/46`, `LRP 2/46`, `Attention Rollout 1/46`.
  - **Evaluation Gap (`Sec. 4.2, Fig. 2`, pp. 11–12):**
    - **`58.7%` (`27/46`)** relied on **qualitative visual inspection**!
    - **`21.7%` (`10/46`)** used expert validation.
    - **`17.4%` (`8/46`)** combined quantitative + qualitative.
    - **`10.9%` (`5/46`)** used purely quantitative evaluation (including `Cerekci 2024` `[39]` in mammography!).
  - **Proposes 4-Dimension Standardized Evaluation Framework (`Fig. 5`, p. 27):** (1) **Spatial Faithfulness** (`IoU`, `DSC`, `Pointing Game`), (2) **Explanation Fidelity** (`Deletion/Insertion curves`, pixel-flipping, counterfactual validity), (3) **Stability & Robustness** (sensitivity, test-retest variance), and (4) **Clinical Relevance** (radiologist scoring, kappa).

## 11. Author-stated limitations vs reviewer critique

- **Author-stated (`Sec. 6`, p. 32):** Excluded `PubMed/MEDLINE` and `Web of Science` (searched CS databases + Scopus only!); heterogeneous evaluation prevented quantitative meta-analysis.
- **Reviewer critique:** Directly validates `RG1` (narrowed): quantifies **`73.9%` Grad-CAM dominance**, **`58.7%` qualitative-only evaluation**, ViT–CAM architectural incompatibility, and explicitly codifies our 4-pillar evaluation stack (`Localization + Deletion/Insertion Faithfulness + Stability + Clinical Alignment`). Omission of PubMed explains why only `9` breast studies (many conference/non-mammogram) were captured.
